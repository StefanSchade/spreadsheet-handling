== 3. H2 and H5 verification

=== H2 -- certificate versus current state

PASS. H2 says certification/classification proves only that the exact bound
invocation is eligible and authorized for its named reviewed transition. It
then separately says the current-state descriptor/role is published only
after that invocation executes and its exact transition is applied, and
explicitly denies that a certificate or successful classification proves the
payload already carries the role.

That matches the Technical Model's three distinct questions: certificate/
binding identity, controlled-transition authority, and current role state. A
maintainer cannot reasonably infer certificate acquisition equals current
role publication.

=== H5 -- one managed macro seam

PASS. Source and `test_execution_state_not_wired.py` confirm the one managed
macro seam is `application.orchestrator.orchestrate`, with
`application.managed_pipeline` as its small named execution component.
`pipeline.runner.run_app` and
`application.schema_maintenance.run_schema_maintenance` delegate to
`orchestrate`; `sheets-run`, schema-maintenance CLI, and the compatibility shim
are reachability/coverage paths and are not policy owners.

The guide also keeps the correction properly bounded: it applies to
framework-managed Trusted-Ingress/controlled-state macro enforcement and
expressly does not require every future rule of every kind to live in
`orchestrate`. Acceptance and production wiring remain separate checks.

The ordering defect is corrected. The sentence nevertheless begins, "The
