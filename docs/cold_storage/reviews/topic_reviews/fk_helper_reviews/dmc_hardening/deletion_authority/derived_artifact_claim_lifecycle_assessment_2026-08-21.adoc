== A. Executive finding

[.lead]
Spreadsheet-handling has a confirmed deletion-authority defect when a cleanup
executor derives a physical helper name from FK transient provenance or durable
FK policy and treats the matching name as sufficient authority to delete the
current column.

The defect is not hypothetical and is broader than inaccurate same-run
provenance. Independent Review 001 reproduced all of the following using
supported registered-step-equivalent calls:

* Lookup materializes helper `_B_name`; FK enrichment sees that label, skips
  physical assignment, nevertheless writes FK provenance, and FK direct
  cleanup deletes Lookup's different physical values.
* Lookup is the only physical producer, FK enrichment never runs, no FK
  run-local provenance exists, and FK direct cleanup still deletes the column
  from the durable FK relation-policy fallback.
* With that same durable-policy-only setup,
  `apply_derived_column_policy(policy="drop")` independently deletes the
  Lookup-produced column through its own FK-policy fallback.

The evidenced root defect is therefore one FK/Lookup defect family spanning
two cleanup executors and two authority layers. H5 truthfully removes stale
Lookup provenance *after* FK direct cleanup, but cannot authorize or undo the
physical deletion.

The smallest evidenced canonical rule is narrower than a derived-artifact
claim lifecycle: *requested or declared identity alone is insufficient
deletion authority*. For the current FK/Lookup case, a cleanup executor must at
minimum establish that FK is the current physical producer before deleting a
matching helper. Current physical production is an evidenced minimum
precondition, not a universal grant of exclusive deletion authority. A future
explicit adoption or sharing model could add constraints, but no supported
current flow requires such a model.

Separately, maintained authority unambiguously says FK derived provenance
records materialized helper state. Runtime writes it even when FK skipped
materialization. That is a confirmed existing contract violation. FK
provenance must eventually record only helpers FK actually materialized, but
that correction alone cannot close either durable-policy fallback.

Discriminator split/merge, XRef/final cleanup, and Cell Codec remain three
independent counterexamples or constraints, not defect instances. Workbook
View, persistence, Trusted Ingress, and schema maintenance remain boundary
constraints only.

*Design-gate disposition.* A bounded canonical Semantic + Technical +
Developer Design Phase is authorized, but is not begun here. Its concrete
scope is the provenance output of `fk_helpers/enrich.py`, pre-deletion
authorization in `fk_helpers/drop.py`, and the equivalent FK-consuming branch
of `derived_column_policy.py`, with Lookup Enrichment as the paired independent
producer case. Claim registration/release, claimant/reliance as a required
primitive, reference counting, a central claim ledger, and global artifact
identity are not authorized by current evidence.

== B. Current derived-artifact model

=== Inventory boundary

This inventory includes physical Domain artifacts for which collision,
reconstruction, inverse processing, or later cleanup is material. It is not a
census of every dataframe transformation. Ordinary extraction, filtering,
sorting, and unrelated rendered carrier objects are excluded. Cells, arbitrary
sheets, external resources, and generic Python objects are not generalized into
the model.

[cols="2,2,2,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Artifact family | Physical identity | Semantic owner/source state
| Provenance and lifetime | Collision, cleanup, replay

| FK helper column
| Source frame plus first visible/first-level helper label; physical assignment
  uses a padded tuple for MultiIndex frames and flatness is restored for flat
  sources.
| FK Relation Helpers; durable v2 FK relation intent and live target-frame
  values are the source state.
| `_meta.derived.sheets.<frame>.helper_columns` is transient. Durable
  `_meta.helper_policies.fk.relations` can reconstruct the requested helper
  name after persistence.
| Existing first-level label is silently skipped. Direct cleanup drops by
  visible label, preferring transient provenance and falling back to durable
  policy.

| Lookup-enriched output and helper columns
| Named output frame plus flat helper labels inside that output.
| Lookup Enrichment; explicit source/lookup/key/helper step configuration and
  lookup helper policy define the projection.
| `_meta.derived.sheets.<output>.enrich_lookup` is transient and records the
  lookup source and helper labels. The output is a current-run projection.
| Helper collision with the source is rejected. The named output frame is
  assigned/replaced. Generic derived-column policy or FK direct cleanup may
  remove helpers; no durable output-specific physical-production record is
  replayed.

| XRef relation/matrix projection frame
| Configured logical relation and matrix frame names; inside the frame, row
  selectors and matrix-axis identities have distinct validated contracts.
| XRef Crosstable owns the scoped matrix/relation inverse. Durable tuple
  relation and/or edited matrix are physical source state depending on
  direction; XRef Intent defines their relationship.
| `_meta.xref_crosstable` preserves durable inverse Intent while runtime
  Resolution is stripped. Frames are durable only if retained by the pipeline.
| The named output is assigned/replaced. `drop_source=True` contributes an
  explicit transient final-cleanup command after loss checks. Replay resolves
  fresh Resolution from Intent and live frames.

| Discriminator split/merge frame
| Generated target frame name, or configured merge target frame name.
| Discriminator Split owns partition/recomposition meaning. Source rows,
  discriminator values, and durable split metadata are canonical inputs to the
  inverse.
| `_meta.split_by_discriminator` is durable recomposition intent including
  value-to-frame mapping and optional row order.
| Split rejects any existing generated target. Merge assigns/replaces its
  target. No family-local frame deletion occurs.

| Cell Codec output and Compact Multiaxis local intermediates
| Named output frame and configured participating physical columns; Compact
  local temporary names exist only during the composite call.
| Cell Codec owns reversible compact-value representation; explicit runtime
  codec intent and structured/compact payload are source state. Compact owns
  only composition.
| Ordinary Cell Codec writes no durable family metadata. Compact/XRef Intent
  persists where needed; local temporary frames are removed before return.
| Named outputs are assigned/replaced. Decode rejects generated-column
  collisions. Helper provenance excludes helper columns from positional
  participation; it does not grant Cell Codec deletion authority.

| Workbook-visible sheet identity and durable helper declaration
| Visible sheet name before reverse mapping, then logical frame name after
  `apply_workbook_view_sheet_mappings`; helper identity is the declared visible
  label in the phase where cleanup executes.
| Workbook View owns visible-to-logical frame identity. Feature-local FK/Lookup
  owners still own helper meaning.
| `_meta.workbook_view.sheet_mappings` and sheet-local `helper_columns` survive
  carriers; `_meta.derived` does not.
| Inbound cleanup can occur under visible identity before re-keying. The mapping
  reconstructs frame identity, not physical-producer or deletion-authority
  history.
|===

*Maintained authority.* The registry classifies `_meta.derived` as transient
producer provenance, explicitly authorizes `drop_helpers` and
`apply_derived_column_policy` to consume it for cleanup, and says it is not
canonical authority over workbook structure. These statements are compatible:
accurate operational provenance may identify cleanup candidates without
becoming canonical schema authority. The defect is the accuracy or deletion
precondition of the consumed evidence, not registry permission to consume it.

*Inference.* "Derived artifact" is a useful bounded umbrella for these
lifecycles, but the repository does not have one uniform artifact type. A later
contract must preserve family-specific collision, inverse, cleanup, and
identity constraints. Global identity is not part of the current defect.

== C. Concrete collision and cleanup cases

[cols="2,2,3,3", options="header"]
|===
| Current path | Artifact already present | Current behavior | Represented semantics

| FK helper materialization
| Any column whose first-level label equals the requested helper label
| Silently skip; no value, equivalence, origin, or ownership check; then write
  FK provenance for every resolved FK definition.
| Physical presence and FK production are conflated. Current maintained
  authority permits provenance only for helpers FK actually materialized, so
  this is a contract violation rather than an implicit adoption contract.

| FK direct cleanup, transient layer
| FK provenance names a currently present visible label
| Delete the matching column; then reconcile owner metadata.
| Accurate FK materialization provenance is sanctioned cleanup input, but the
  current same-run record may be false after FK skipped a collision.

| FK direct cleanup, durable layer
| No FK provenance exists for the sheet; durable FK relation policy declares a
  helper whose label is present
| Delete the matching column from policy alone.
| Requested/declared policy is treated as production/deletion authority even
  when FK enrichment never ran.

| Derived-column policy, FK-consuming branch
| FK transient provenance or durable FK relation policy names a present label
| Union that FK-derived name with other helper declarations and delete the
  matching column.
| A second executor independently makes the same unsafe name-to-deletion
  inference.

| Lookup helper projection
| Requested non-key helper already exists in the source
| Reject before merge to prevent shadowing.
| Existing helper is rejected; no reuse path is currently defined.

| Lookup named output
| Output frame name already exists
| Assign the newly enriched frame to that name, replacing the prior mapping.
| Explicit output replacement by operation semantics; not equivalence-based
  reuse.

| Discriminator split target
| Any generated target frame exists
| Reject all target creation before mutation.
| Existing frame is rejected by split.

| Discriminator merge target
| Target exists
| Assign recomposed frame to target.
| Explicit reconstruction replacement.

| XRef matrix/relation output
| Output frame exists
| Assign projected/recomposed frame to output.
| Explicit directional replacement; scoped relation carry-forward remains
  feature-local.

| Cell Codec output / decoded generated columns
| Output exists / generated decoded label conflicts with passthrough
| Output is assigned/replaced; decode-side column collision is rejected.
| Frame replacement and inner-column collision are different policies.

| Final pipeline cleanup declarations
| Multiple producers request the same drop
| Set-union and idempotent reuse of the same deletion command; conflicting
  explicit Workbook View mapping or keep/drop declaration fails.
| Command composition under an explicit cleanup contract, not physical-
  producer inference from a helper name.
|===

*Test evidence.* Lookup source-column rejection, discriminator target rejection,
XRef identity validation, Cell Codec conflict handling, and cleanup command
composition are pinned by current tests.

*Observed runtime fact.* FK's collision path is exactly `if helper_col in
first_cols: continue`; `first_cols` is captured before the loop. No current FK
test names or asserts the authority consequences of a pre-existing helper
collision.

*Inference.* Current Domain behavior already uses family-specific "reject" and
"replace" policies. The FK evidence requires only: absent -> materialize;
present -> do not silently infer FK production or deletion authority from the
name. Explicit adoption or shared use is a possible future extension, not a
current requirement.

== D. FK/Lookup motivating case: reachable versus synthetic states

=== Supported current construction

At the baseline, the following all use supported registered capabilities:

. Configure a durable FK relation from frame `A.id_(B)` to `B.id`, requesting
  helper `_B_name`.
. Run Lookup Enrichment from an independent lookup frame into output `A`, also
  producing `_B_name` with values different from `B.name`.
. Run FK helper enrichment.
. Run FK helper removal.

The observed trace was:

[source,text]
----
after Lookup:        _B_name = [lookup-alpha, lookup-beta]
after FK enrichment: _B_name = [lookup-alpha, lookup-beta]  # FK skipped
derived provenance:  Lookup records helper_columns [_B_name]
                     FK records helper_columns [{column: _B_name, ...}]
after FK cleanup:    _B_name absent; both records absent
----

FK did not compare those values with `[fk-alpha, fk-beta]`. It added its own
provenance despite not performing the physical assignment. FK cleanup then
deleted the label and asked Lookup only whether Lookup provenance remained true
after deletion. Because no declared Lookup helper remained, H5 correctly
removed the stale record.

*Observed runtime fact.* This state is reachable without direct `_meta`
mutation, legacy shape, plugin behavior, carrier corruption, or unsupported
internal calls.

*Maintained authority.* FK and Lookup have separate semantic ownership. H5
correctly places interpretation of Lookup's provenance behind a Lookup-owned
boundary. Nothing in current maintained authority grants an FK-consuming
executor the right to delete a physical helper merely because FK transient
provenance or durable policy names the same label.

=== Durable-policy-only construction

Independent Review 001 reproduced a stricter supported sequence:

. `configure_fk_helpers` writes a durable v2 relation requesting `_B_name`.
. Lookup Enrichment is the only operation that materializes `_B_name`.
. FK enrichment never runs in the session, so no FK
  `_meta.derived.sheets.A.helper_columns` record exists.
. `remove_fk_helpers` still deletes Lookup's `_B_name` because
  `_columns_to_drop_by_sheet` falls back from absent sheet provenance to the
  durable relation's declared helper names.

*Independent review evidence.* This sequence was reproduced from scratch with
supported registered-step-equivalent calls. The review asserted the absence of
FK run-local provenance before cleanup.

*Inference.* Inaccurate FK run-local provenance is a real, separate contract
violation, but is not fundamental to the wrongful-deletion class. Durable FK
policy currently supplies requested identity, not evidence that FK physically
produced the matching live column.

=== Second cleanup executor

Independent Review 001 repeated the durable-policy-only setup with
`apply_derived_column_policy(source="A", policy="drop")`. Its independent
`_safe_policy_helper_names` path reads the same durable FK relation names,
unions them with other declared helper names, and deletes Lookup's column.

*Independent review evidence.* The second executor reproduced the same physical
loss without calling FK direct cleanup and without any FK run-local provenance.

*Inference.* The mandatory case crosses a DMC family/executor boundary. A
correction confined to FK materialization or `fk_helpers/drop.py` would leave
the wrongful-deletion class open in `derived_column_policy.py`.

=== Two executors and two authority layers

[cols="2,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Cleanup executor | Candidate-name layer | Current exposure

| `fk_helpers/drop.py`
| transient FK provenance
| Same-run collision: FK's inaccurate record names Lookup's physical column.

| `fk_helpers/drop.py`
| durable FK relation policy
| Independently reproduced with FK enrichment never executed.

| `derived_column_policy.py`
| transient FK provenance
| The executor consumes FK helper names from `_resolve_derived_identity` and

| `derived_column_policy.py`
| durable FK relation policy
| Independently reproduced through `_safe_policy_helper_names` with no FK

|===

These are multiple reachable variants of one FK/Lookup root defect, not four
defect families.

=== Reachability classification

[cols="2,3,3", options="header"]
|===
| State class | Evidence | Disposition

| Supported current runtime
| Lookup creates a colliding helper in the same source/output frame. The helper
  is then deleted through FK direct cleanup or derived-column policy, using
  transient FK provenance or durable FK policy. The durable variants do not
  require FK enrichment to run.
| Reachable and safety-relevant. These are the primary cases.

| Supported user/caller payload
| A caller supplies a pre-existing ordinary column with the requested FK helper
  name; FK skips it, records it, and later removes it.
| Also reachable. A second provenance owner is unnecessary because requested
  name alone is already treated as deletion authority.

| Synthetic regression state
| Nested-provenance tests directly construct `_meta.derived` with FK and Lookup
  sibling records to characterize key-selective cleanup and H5.
| Valuable behavior evidence, but not proof of runtime reachability by itself.

| Historical compatibility/corrupt state
| H5 tests malformed Lookup provenance and legacy/pass-through forms.
| Constrains fail-clear behavior, not the motivating normal-path frequency.

| Maintained consumer state
| No consumer inspected in this assessment.
| Not asserted; historical reports are navigation only.

| Theoretical unsupported state
| Arbitrary external metadata can invent inconsistent producer records.
| Relevant to validation boundaries only; not needed to establish the primary
  finding.
|===

*Test evidence.* Existing tests establish owner-local merge preservation,
cleanup by provenance, H5 retention/removal, malformed-record behavior, and
durable fallback after transient provenance loss. The independent review's
runtime reproductions, rather than existing tests, establish the combined
Lookup/FK wrongful-deletion variants.

*Inference.* H5 is correct within its stated post-mutation responsibility. The
defect is earlier and common to both executors: a matching requested/declared
name is treated as sufficient deletion authority without establishing current
FK physical production.

== E. Independent evidence from other capabilities

=== Discriminator split and merge

`split_by_discriminator` computes every generated frame identity, rejects an
existing target before creating anything, and records durable recomposition
intent. `merge_by_discriminator` reconstructs and replaces its configured
target.

*Test evidence.* Existing-target and duplicate-target rejections are directly
asserted.

*Inference.* Collision policy belongs to operation semantics and direction.
The framework cannot define "existing derived artifact means reuse" as a
universal rule.

=== XRef and final domain cleanup

XRef projection/recomposition may replace a named output. When `drop_source`
is requested, XRef validates source/output distinction, addressability, and
loss conditions before contributing an explicit frame deletion command. The
final executor unions duplicate drop requests, rejects contradictions, executes
only commands, and consumes them. Persisted XRef inverse Intent may still name
a frame that was deliberately removed; that reference is reconstruction intent,
not an active requirement that the physical frame remain.

*Maintained authority.* The Functional Model says source frames remain unless a
feature or builder explicitly declares final cleanup; generic provenance or
classification does not establish precedence. The registry says cleanup
commands are executable instructions, not history.

*Test evidence.* Current XRef and pipeline-cleanup tests pin loss guards,
command production, set-union, keep/drop conflict, Workbook View conflict, and
command consumption.

*Inference.* A universal "referenced by metadata means physically present or
deletable" rule would misclassify valid XRef intent. Deletion authority can be
an explicit operation contract without a claim ledger.

=== Cell Codec

Position-based Cell Codec gathers helper labels from both durable sheet helper
declarations and transient FK/Lookup provenance, then rejects them when they
are requested as participating payload columns. It does not write a Cell Codec
production record for those helpers and does not delete them.

*Observed current fact.* Producer provenance here functions as a role/exclusion
signal for a third capability, not as evidence that Cell Codec requires the
artifact to continue to exist.

*Inference.* A provenance consumer is not thereby a physical producer or a
cleanup authority. Recasting every read as a claim would distort current
semantics.

=== Finding from the independent cases

There is *one genuinely evidenced defect family*: FK plus Lookup. Within it,
two cleanup executors consume two candidate-name authority layers, producing
multiple reachable variants of one root defect. Discriminator split/merge,
XRef/final cleanup, and Cell Codec are three independent constraints or
counterexamples. They do not establish additional defect instances and do not
justify common storage.

== F. Producer, policy, owner, executor, and authority distinctions

[cols="2,3,3", options="header"]
|===
| Concept | Required meaning | Current evidence

| Physical producer
| The operation that actually writes/replaces the column or frame state.
| Lookup is the physical producer in every reproduced collision; FK is not. In
  the durable-policy variants FK materialization never runs.

| Requested / declared policy
| A capability's transient or durable declaration that it wants a helper at a
  named identity.
| FK provenance or v2 relation policy supplies candidate helper names. A
  request/declaration alone does not prove physical production.

| Semantic owner
| The family that defines meaning, invariants, compatibility, and truthful
  provenance for its projection.
| FK and Lookup remain separate owners even under one frame/label.

| Provenance producer
| The operation that writes metadata describing a derivation or current
  projection.
| FK writes provenance for every resolved definition even when it skipped the
  physical write, violating its materialized-only contract; Lookup writes
  output-local projection provenance.

| Cleanup executor
| The operation that mutates the physical frame to remove an artifact.
| FK direct cleanup and derived-column policy are two mandatory concrete
  executors for this defect. Final pipeline cleanup is a distinct constraint.

| Deletion authority
| The semantic contract whose satisfied preconditions permit the executor to
  remove physical state.
| Explicit and guarded for XRef frame cleanup; absent when an FK-consuming
  helper cleanup trusts only a matching transient/policy-derived name.
|===

*Inference.* The minimum vocabulary required now is physical producer,
requested/declared policy, semantic owner, provenance producer, cleanup
executor, and deletion precondition. Physical producer, semantic owner, and
provenance producer often collapse in ordinary create-and-clean flows, but do
not in the motivating collision. Cleanup executor and deletion authority must
remain distinct.

=== Future vocabulary not currently required

"Claimant" or "current reliance" could become useful if a future supported
operation explicitly adopts or shares another capability's physical artifact
and requires it to remain after the original producer would otherwise remove
it. No current supported flow in the reviewed evidence does that. Lookup does
not register or release a claim; H5 is a stateless post-mutation truthfulness
predicate. Claimant, reliance, registration, and release are therefore
plausible future vocabulary only, not required primitives of the authorized
Design Phase.

*Constraint.* Establishing that FK is the current physical producer is the
minimum evidenced precondition for the current case. It is not a universal
proof that a producer has unconditional or exclusive deletion rights. A future
explicit adopt/share model could add further constraints without contradicting
this narrower rule.

== G. Current provenance semantics

=== What `_meta.derived` currently means

The maintained registry calls it current-run helper-column provenance produced
by materialization steps, useful to render, validate, position, and clean
helpers. Technical wording describes what a primitive wrote when it last
enriched a source and describes entries per materialized helper. Runtime FK
behavior is broader: it writes records for resolved FK definitions whether it
materialized the column or skipped it.

This is not an authorized mixture of meanings. The Semantic contract matrix,
Technical FK metadata concept, and maintained FK concept documentation all
state that FK derived provenance records *materialized state*: what FK wrote.
Writing an entry for a skipped definition is therefore a confirmed
runtime-versus-maintained-contract violation. The contract-aligned local
correction is materialized-only FK provenance.

Lookup provenance more closely describes the current output projection because
Lookup rejects a colliding source helper and constructs/replaces the output.
It does not create a general deletion-authority protocol for FK.

*Observed runtime fact.* Two producer-specific sibling records can refer to one
physical frame and one physical helper label.

*Maintained authority.* The registry expressly authorizes `drop_helpers` and
`apply_derived_column_policy` to consume accurate derived provenance. The
problem is not authorization to consume provenance; it is false FK provenance
on the same-run path and a missing deletion precondition on both transient and
durable-policy paths.

=== Can provenance or policy prove deletion authority?

Accurate materialized-only FK provenance can be evidence that FK physically
produced a helper in the current run. A durable FK policy cannot supply the
same fact: it says which helper FK requests, and persists across runs in which
FK materialization may never execute. Current evidence therefore requires
executors to distinguish the two rather than treating either name source as
self-sufficient deletion authority.

Other metadata reinforces why no repository-wide inference is valid:

* current FK provenance overstates physical production on collision;
* Lookup provenance describes Lookup's actual projection, not FK production;
* XRef durable intent may validly reference absent frames.
* Cell Codec reads helper provenance only to reject participation.
* pipeline cleanup metadata is an explicit command, not production evidence.

*Inference.* No claim interpretation is required. The Design Phase needs a
truthful FK materialization fact and one deletion-precondition rule applied by
both FK-consuming executors.

== H. Artifact identity: boundary constraint, not current defect

Every independently reproduced failure identified the intended physical
`_B_name` column correctly. The defect is deletion authorization, not identity
resolution. There is no evidence for one globally stable
`(frame_name, column_name)` identity, no evidence requiring UUIDs, and no
authorization to make identity a mandatory Design-Phase problem.

=== Column labels

FK creation, provenance cleanup, and direct deletion use a visible/first-level
label. On a MultiIndex or tuple label, that can identify more physical columns
than a full tuple identity would. Duplicate visible labels are not assigned an
occurrence identity. Lookup operates on flat labels and rejects helper
collisions. XRef separately validates physical labels, durable selector labels,
and carrier-stable axis identities.

*Inference.* Identity remains family- and phase-specific background. The
authorized deletion rule must use each current executor's already-correct local
column addressing; it must not generalize identity.

=== Rename and schema mutation

Schema maintenance rejects a rename target that already exists, rewrites or
blocks affected durable FK/Lookup references, and deliberately does not rewrite
transient `_meta.derived`. FK helper rename is documented as unsupported.

*Test evidence.* Current schema-maintenance tests pin durable reference updates
and non-ownership of derived provenance.

*Inference.* A new durable producer/authority identity would create a
schema-maintenance obligation. Current evidence instead supports an on-demand
runtime precondition and does not authorize that expansion.

=== Workbook View and replay phase

Workbook View persists visible-sheet-to-logical-frame mappings. Inbound helper
cleanup can run under the visible sheet name before mapping re-keys the frame to
its logical name. The mapping is authoritative for frame identity only; it
intentionally does not reconstruct a generic source/projection relationship.

*Test evidence.* The maintained XRef/Lookup inbound integration asserts cleanup
by visible identity before mapping, including the no-op behavior when the
durable helper declaration is omitted.

*Inference.* Artifact identity may be phase-relative, but that is only a
constraint against centralization. A central index keyed only by logical
runtime frame name would duplicate Workbook View authority without helping the
current correctly-identified-column defect.

=== Replacement, copy, and derived frames

Transformations commonly shallow-copy the frames mapping and replace the
DataFrame object at an existing logical name. Python object identity is not
semantic identity. Split-generated frames reject collision, while inverse
operations replace targets. This is further evidence against a universal
object-identity or name-only rule.

== I. Cleanup and deletion-authority constraints

Current cleanup mechanisms are intentionally plural:

* FK direct cleanup deletes visible labels from FK provenance or durable FK
  policy and then cleans owner metadata. Both name sources need a pre-deletion
  authorization decision.
* derived-column policy independently interprets FK and Lookup
  provenance/policy to remove projected columns in an inbound/replacement
  workflow. Its FK-consuming branch is a mandatory second concrete executor,
  not background evidence.
* XRef/other projections contribute explicit frame-drop commands after local
  validation.
* final pipeline cleanup executes explicit frame commands and never infers from
  roles or generic provenance.
* Compact composites remove their own local temporaries before returning.

The following constraints already exist:

. Removing metadata is not equivalent to deleting physical state.
. A requested/declared helper name is not sufficient evidence of FK physical
   production and is not sufficient deletion authority.
. For the evidenced FK/Lookup case, both cleanup executors must at minimum
   establish that FK is the current physical producer before deleting the
   matching helper.
. A cleanup executor must preserve sibling owner records unless their owner
   decides they became stale.
. Post-deletion reconciliation cannot substitute for pre-deletion authority.
. A durable inverse reference can remain valid after intentional physical
   deletion.
. User/canonical payload that merely collides by label must not become derived
   and deletable without an explicit collision decision.

*Inference.* The canonical obligation should be a pre-deletion authorization
protocol with family-local representation. It need not model release or count
references. Physical production is the minimum established precondition in the
current case, not proof of unconditional or exclusive producer authority for
all future cases.

*Unresolved.* The Design Phase must decide how each mandatory executor verifies
current FK physical production, how failure is reported, and whether present
materialization collisions are always rejected or can be proven to be FK's own
prior output. Explicit adoption/share remains a future extension point and is
not part of the mandatory question.

== J. Persistence and replay constraints

`_meta.derived` and `_meta.pipeline_cleanup` are stripped at the persistence
boundary. Durable state currently includes FK v2 relations, lookup helper
policies, Workbook View mappings and sheet helper declarations, discriminator
split intent, and XRef Intent. XRef Resolution is recomputed.

=== What can be reconstructed

Durable FK relations can reconstruct which helper label FK would request and
can drive fallback cleanup after transient provenance is lost. They cannot
reconstruct who physically produced an already-present colliding column, what
materialization check occurred, or whether FK ran in the current session.
Lookup policy is keyed by lookup frame and does not itself preserve a specific
source/output physical-producer fact. Workbook sheet helper declarations can
identify a cleanup candidate on reimport, but not its physical-producer
history.

*Maintained authority.* Durable Intent should survive; run-local Resolution and
derived provenance should be recomputed. One authority per fact is preferred;
metadata joins should not mirror another owner's contract.

*Independent review evidence.* FK direct cleanup and derived-column policy both
wrongfully delete Lookup's column from durable FK policy in a run where FK
enrichment never executes and no FK transient provenance exists.

*Inference.* Persistence strengthens the case against new durable claim
metadata. The missing fact is not historical claim state; it is the cleanup-
time precondition that the FK owner currently produced the live artifact. The
known same-process executors can evaluate that precondition on demand. A
durable claim tree would add a second authority without being needed by any
reproduced path.

=== How current derived-column cleanup handles loss

Current cleanup uses transient provenance when available and durable
declarations/policy when configured. That solves candidate-name discovery, not
physical-producer verification or deletion authority. The maintained inbound
roundtrip can intentionally no-op when the durable helper declaration is
absent.

*Unresolved.* The Design Phase must specify which current runtime signals can
establish FK physical production for each executor. Current evidence does not
require a new fact to survive persistence.

== K. Trusted-Ingress and schema-maintenance implications

=== Trusted Ingress

Current generic `_meta` admission establishes bounded recursive structure, not
truth of every owner-specific semantic assertion. Helper policy origins include
configuration, persisted meta, plugins, and callers. Plugins can be authorized
to run without making their semantic output correct.

The Functional statement that meta does not enter from outside is scoped to
repository/git-boundary movement of the gitignored sidecar. Same-process
caller/plugin/config admission under Trusted Ingress is a different authority
scope, not contradictory or stale documentation.

*Inference.* Trusted Ingress is not part of the current defect or mandatory
Design Phase. It becomes relevant only if a future design introduces new
authoritative metadata crossing a caller, plugin, or carrier boundary. Current
evidence calls for no such metadata.

=== Schema maintenance

Current schema maintenance owns rewriting durable references and protecting
configured relations from invalid drop/rename. It intentionally ignores
transient derived provenance. A durable global producer/authority identity that
followed column renames would therefore add a new reference family.

*Inference.* The hidden current contract is not "all metadata follows rename".
It is "schema maintenance follows registered durable references it owns".
Schema maintenance is a constraint against introducing new durable identity,
not a mandatory design site.

== M. Candidate architecture families

This comparison is non-binding research. It deliberately does not select a
runtime architecture.

=== A. Owner-local production authority plus explicit handoffs

Description: each family keeps truthful production/provenance facts in its own
shape. A DMC-distinct cleanup executor uses a narrow owner-defined precondition
or handoff rather than independently treating a declared name as authority.

Strengths:

* fits current DMC locality and one-authority-per-fact rules;
* preserves family-specific identities and collision policies;
* avoids a second durable authority and broad migration; and
* matches H5's owner-local truthfulness placement, while moving the new check
  before deletion.

Risks/limits:

* both known executors must use the same FK-owned rule consistently;
* owner-local provenance must first become truthful; and
* duplicated fallback interpretation can drift unless the contract boundary is
  explicit.

Current fit: closest current runtime architecture. The two exposed executors
and the FK/Lookup owners are statically known.

=== B. Central authoritative claim ledger

Description: a hypothetical common `_meta` structure would become canonical
authority for cross-owner artifact retention and deletion.

Strengths:

* one discovery point if unknown owners ever need dynamic discovery; and
* potentially consistent diagnostics across executors.

Risks/limits:

* must encode heterogeneous column/frame and phase-relative identity;
* duplicates producer Intent/provenance unless owners delegate authority;
* becomes a schema-maintenance, persistence, replay, and Trusted-Ingress
  contract;
* risks a semantic dumping ground and generic runtime coupling; and
* solves neither known candidate-name path more directly than an on-demand
  producer check.

*Current status: premature / unjustified.* Every reproduced executor is known,
same-process code. No dynamic owner discovery or legitimate multi-owner sharing
is required. The durable-policy-only reproduction actively shows new durable
metadata is unnecessary, not merely unproven.

=== C. Owner-local authority plus a transient shared index

Description: owners retain meaning; a common transient index could join local
artifact records for discovery while remaining recomputable Resolution.

Strengths:

* could centralize discovery without centralizing family semantics; and
* could remain transient Resolution.

Risks/limits:

* identity normalization remains difficult;
* index staleness could itself authorize a bad deletion;
* every producer would have to participate in common mechanics; and
* the current case needs verification of a known FK producer, not discovery.

*Current status: future possibility only.* It would require additional
independent evidence of legitimate shared artifacts or dynamic owner discovery,
plus a deterministic rebuild from owner-local facts.

=== D. Common deletion-authority protocol with family-local representation

Description: canonical docs state that requested/declared identity alone is
insufficient deletion authority and require an owner-specific precondition
before physical removal. Each family represents current production locally;
shared helpers may emerge later only for identical mechanics.

Strengths:

* addresses the exact semantic gap without choosing storage;
* makes FK's false provenance and both name-only deletion fallbacks separately
  reviewable;
* preserves reject, replace, explicit cleanup-command, and local-temp
  counterexamples; and
* can be applied to the three mandatory concrete sites only.

Risks/limits:

* the physical-producer check must be specified concretely enough to be
  testable at both executors; and
* current physical production must not be overgeneralized into unconditional
  exclusive producer rights.

*Inference.* D is the smallest justified Design Phase starting point; A is the
closest current runtime architecture. C is future-only without more evidence.
B is not justified by current evidence. This ranking is non-binding but the
scope boundary is load-bearing.

=== Is common claim metadata necessary?

No. Current evidence actively reinforces that durable claim metadata is
unnecessary today: both policy-only reproductions arise without transient FK
provenance and can be guarded on demand by known same-process executors. A
central authoritative claim ledger is premature and unjustified. A transient
index or shared protocol for explicit adoption/share is only a future option if
later supported flows prove dynamic owner discovery or legitimate multi-owner
retention is needed.

== N. Smallest problem statement for Design Phase

[.lead]
Spreadsheet-handling has a confirmed deletion-authority defect when a cleanup
executor derives a physical helper name from FK transient provenance or durable
FK policy and treats the name match as sufficient authority to delete the
current column. Requested/declared identity alone is insufficient deletion
authority. In the current FK/Lookup case, deletion must at minimum be
conditioned on establishing that FK is the current physical producer. The rule
must cover FK direct cleanup and the FK-consuming branch of derived-column
policy. FK run-local provenance must separately be corrected to record only
helpers FK actually materialized. No claimant/release model, central claim
ledger, or global artifact identity system is justified.

The mandatory concrete Design-Phase sites are:

. `domain/transformations/fk_helpers/enrich.py` -- make FK provenance truthful
  to its maintained materialized-only contract.
. `domain/transformations/fk_helpers/drop.py` -- apply a pre-deletion
  authorization rule to helper identities obtained from transient provenance
  and durable FK policy.
. `domain/transformations/derived_column_policy.py` -- apply the equivalent rule
  in its FK-consuming branch; it must not retain an independent unsafe
  durable-policy interpretation.
. Lookup Enrichment -- paired collaborator and independent physical producer of
  the conflicting column; it remains a separate semantic owner.

Canonical Semantic + Technical + Developer design wording may state the one
testable deletion-authority/precondition rule. Discriminator split/merge,
XRef/final cleanup, Cell Codec, Workbook View, persistence, Trusted Ingress,
and schema maintenance remain constraints/counterexamples only.

Out of scope:

* cells, arbitrary sheets/resources, renderer/backend artifacts, or generic
  object ownership;
* all derived artifacts or a whole-Domain producer census;
* stable artifact IDs, UUIDs, global rename identity, or a generic artifact
  object/registry;
* a central claim ledger, claim AST, claim registration/release, reference
  counting, or shared-claim protocol;
* canonical claimant/current-reliance vocabulary;
* metadata migration;
* Trusted-Ingress or schema-maintenance redesign;
* persistence changes; and
* every runtime, test, registry, or canonical-document change in this research
  correction task.

== P. DMC and methodology feedback

The DMC successfully prevented this research from treating shared `_meta`
storage as shared ownership and directed attention to H5 as a material
handoff. Its one-authority-per-fact, lifecycle, reconstruction, and bounded
scope rules are directly useful.

Independent Review 001 found no DMC defect. The finding is a legitimate future
specialization of existing material-handoff and provenance guidance, not a
missing methodology foundation. If future methodology work revisits the area,
the load-bearing question is narrowly:

[quote]
____
Before a cleanup executor deletes physical state identified through a
requested or declared name, what fact establishes that the requester is
authorized to delete that state?
____

For the evidenced FK/Lookup case, the minimum fact is current FK physical
production, not name equality. This task proposes no DMC or Developer Guide
edit. The Human Readability guidance needs no change: the problematic FK
`continue` is locally readable; the defect is cross-capability semantics, not
line-level clarity.
