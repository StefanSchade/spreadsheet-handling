= FK Helper Deletion Authority — Slice Sequence Summary

Date: 2026-08-23

Scope: durable summary of the completed FK Helper Deletion Authority nested
subroutine, a bounded interruption of FK/reference-helper DMC hardening.

This is a factual sequence record, not a new architecture review. It is
substantially shorter than the accumulated review artifacts it summarizes;
see those artifacts in this same directory for full evidence and reasoning.

== Why the subroutine was entered

The FK/reference-helper family's first bounded DMC hardening slice (H5, the
seam where FK direct cleanup hands off to Lookup provenance reconciliation;
see `../provenance_handoff/`) surfaced a research question: a derived
physical artifact (an FK-attributed helper column) can be materialized by one
capability (Lookup Enrichment) while another capability (FK Relation Helpers)
independently believes it produced or owns that column. H5 could truthfully
reconcile Lookup's *provenance metadata* after the fact, but could not
authorize or undo a *physical deletion* already made on a false or
insufficient basis. That gap was pulled out as a bounded, independently
reviewed nested subroutine rather than folded into ordinary Family cycle 1
work.

== Research

`derived_artifact_claim_lifecycle_assessment_2026-08-21.adoc` (and its
independent Review 001) established the concrete evidence: a cleanup
executor that deletes a column identified only through an FK-attributed
*requested/declared* name -- whether same-run transient FK provenance or
durable FK relation policy -- can wrongly delete a column that Lookup
Enrichment, not FK, actually produced. Independent Review 001 reproduced this
through supported registered-step-equivalent calls in three variants,
including two durable-policy-only cases where FK materialization never ran
in the session at all. The research concluded that requested/declared
identity alone is insufficient deletion authority, and explicitly rejected a
central claim ledger, claimant/reliance vocabulary, reference counting, and
global artifact identity as unjustified by the evidence. Verdict: *ACCEPT
WITH MINOR CORRECTIONS*; Design Phase authorized with narrowed scope.

== Accepted design

`derived_artifact_deletion_authority_design_2026-08-21.adoc`, corrected
through two independent adversarial reviews
(`..._review_001.adoc`, `..._review_002.adoc`) and a final targeted
finding-closure pass, established the accepted core rule:

* requested/declared artifact identity is never itself sufficient deletion
  authority;
* durable FK relation policy (`_meta.helper_policies.fk.relations`) remains a
  *materialization/configuration intent* input to `add_fk_helpers`, but is
  permanently removed as a *deletion-candidate* input from both cleanup
  executors;
* current, truthful, same-run transient FK provenance
  (`_meta.derived.sheets.*.helper_columns`) is the FK-attributed deletion
  authority, and only "truthful and current" together authorize deletion --
  a same-run decoupled rebind (by `enrich_lookup` or by
  `derived_column_policy` itself) invalidates a sibling's provenance at
  write time, closing a same-run staleness gap the design's own first pass
  missed;
* Workbook-View `_meta.sheets[*].helper_columns` remains a separate,
  independently documented, explicit durable cleanup authority, unaffected
  by this design;
* Lookup Enrichment's own `enrich_lookup` provenance describes Lookup's own
  producer identity and projection; it is not redesigned and does not gain
  or lose authority over FK-attributed columns;
* generic persistence continues to prune transient `_meta.derived` at the
  persistence boundary, so a durable FK relation surviving reimport cannot
  regain deletion authority on its own -- no new durable state is
  introduced anywhere in this design.

Eight independently reproduced defect variants (same-run collision;
durable-policy-only with FK never run; the second `derived_column_policy.py`
executor; three further decoupled-rebind staleness shapes surfaced by two
independent adversarial reviews) are closed by four jointly sufficient,
independently necessary runtime corrections. No claim ledger, claimant,
reliance, generation ID, or value/formula equivalence machinery was
introduced.

== Implementation sequence

Summarized by meaning, not by line. Full detail is in each commit and its
sibling review artifact.

* *Slice 1 -- materialization truth* (`3b50701`, `refactor(fk): report
  helper materialization results`): `core/fk.py`'s materialization primitive
  reports, per resolved FK definition, whether it actually wrote the column
  this call or found the label already occupied, without changing
  `apply_fk_helpers`'s existing signature or return type.
* *Slice 2 -- truthful FK provenance* (`d66ae9c`, `fix(fk): write provenance
  only for materialized helpers`): `fk_helpers/enrich.py` writes
  `_meta.derived.sheets.*.helper_columns` entries only for definitions it can
  truthfully claim, closing the confirmed materialized-only contract
  violation and the same-run collision variant.
* *Slice 3 -- decoupled Lookup-rebind invalidation* (`c570db2`, `fix(lookup):
  invalidate provenance on decoupled rebind`; reviewed and accepted in

  `enrich_lookup`'s own provenance writer unconditionally invalidates any
  provenance already present at a decoupled `output` (foreign or its own
  stale prior record) whenever it writes into an `output` that is not its
  own `source` for that call.

== Non-goals

Do not reopen this subroutine to add a central claim ledger, claim AST,
claim registration/release, reference counting, generation IDs, global
artifact identity, or name-based/value-equivalence deletion ownership. The
accepted design and both independent reviews considered and rejected each of
these against the evidence; nothing evidenced since has changed that.

Do not use this closure to opportunistically start FK/reference-helper
Family cycle 1 continuation, `FTR-FK-REFERENCE-HELPER-SURFACE-CONSOLIDATION-P5`
implementation, or any other unrelated Domain family/FTR. This subroutine
closes one nested question; it does not decide or advance the enclosing
FK/reference-helper hardening cycle.
