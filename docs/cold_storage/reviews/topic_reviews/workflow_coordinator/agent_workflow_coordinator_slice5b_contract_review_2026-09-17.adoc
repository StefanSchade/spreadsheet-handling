==== P5B-C1 -- IMPORTANT -- New-Finding birth-state rule contradicts accepted authorized creation

Normative clause:: "A new Finding proposal cannot be born resolved, residual, or
superseded." (FTR 2597), unconditional; and "accepted `N=1`, Slice-5A,
reducer/replay ... behavior remains green except for the stated Run-wide completion
safety change" (2824-2826).
Conflicting evidence::
* FTR section 10 matrix G (952-955): "Authorized declared gate ... records
  `residual` and advances/completes without a forced correction loop."
* `test_replay_g_declared_gate_accepts_residual_without_correction`
  (`test_workflow_coordinator_replays.py:247-270`) creates `delta(None, "residual")`
  at a gate with `finding_authority=("residual",)` and asserts `COMPLETED`.
* `test_new_finding_creation_uses_explicit_authority_operation`
  (`test_reducer.py:136-162`) asserts authorized creation as `resolved`,
  `residual`, `superseded`; `reducer.py:126-139` implements it.
* Neither the plan nor re-review `6fe3a9dd` contains this rule; the re-review states
  "replay G-style residual completion unaffected".
Reachable consequence:: A newly discovered minor at a gate cannot be recorded as
`residual` in one Hop (its ID is unknown until allocation), so matrix G becomes
unsatisfiable for new Findings. An implementer must either break an accepted binding
replay and unit test (violating 2824-2826) or ignore 2597. The later implementation
review cannot certify both.
Smallest correction:: "Without applicable gate or human authority, a new Finding
proposal can only be born `open`; birth as `resolved`, `residual`, or `superseded`
requires the same operation authority as the corresponding transition." (Existing
behavior.) If open-only birth is truly intended, the contract must instead declare
the matrix-G/replay-G change explicitly -- not recommended.
Changes:: one-slice disposition: no; implementation readiness: yes (correction
before authorization); architecture: no.

==== P5B-C2 -- IMPORTANT -- Required-artifact obligation outcome applicability is ambiguous

Normative clauses:: "For `required`, ... a successful eligible result shall
reference and commit every required artifact" (2685-2686); precedence step 1:
"required-artifact ... anomalies retain their existing mechanical disposition ...
No Finding proposals apply after such a preemption" (2729-2733); `blocked`: "After
otherwise valid and authorized Finding proposals are reduced ... `result_blocked`"
(2661-2663); `changes_required`: CommitIntent eligible "only at a current declared
review gate" and "may take only a declared non-terminal route" (2652-2656).
Conflicting evidence:: `_durable_artifact_anomalies` is outcome-independent
(`vertical.py:77-88`; probe P6) and feeds `repository_anomaly`, which preempts before
Finding deltas (`reducer.py:258-268`). Re-review DW3 asked the contract to settle
exactly this ("`blocked` proposals are not applied at a `required` phase that lands
nothing"). WFC-OUTPUT-04: "The agent cannot weaken the declared value."
Reachable consequence:: Two incompatible readings:
. *Existing/outcome-independent (step 1):* at any `required` phase, `blocked` and
  `failed` (CommitIntent forbidden) always become artifact anomalies; `result_blocked`,
  `result_failed`, and `blocked` Finding application are unreachable there, silently
  voiding 2661-2668 for the committed DMC dogfood profile shape (`assess:
  durable_artifact: required`).
. *"Successful eligible result" only:* `blocked`/`failed` escape (reasonable), but so
  does `changes_required` at a non-gate `required` phase (not CommitIntent-eligible),
  which may then take a declared `goto`. An agent in a `required` design or work phase
  can advance without its artifact by choosing `changes_required` -- a WFC-OUTPUT-04
  weakening. The contract also never states whether a non-review phase may return
  `changes_required` at all.
Different implementations therefore produce different stop reasons, Finding
application, and, under reading 2, an agent-selectable artifact bypass.
Smallest correction:: One sentence, e.g.: "The required-artifact obligation applies
to every validated result whose outcome may reduce a route (`completed`,
`changes_required`); a `changes_required` result at a `required` phase that is not an
eligible review gate is a required-artifact anomaly; `blocked` and `failed` carry no
artifact obligation and receive their outcome disposition." Alternatively declare
the obligation outcome-independent and state that `result_blocked`/`result_failed`
are unreachable in `required` phases. Either choice must be listed under P5B-C3 if it
changes current N=1 behavior.
Changes:: one-slice: no; readiness: yes; architecture: no.

==== P5B-C3 -- IMPORTANT -- N=1/5A behavior-change exception list is incomplete

Normative clauses:: "`N=1` and accepted Slice-5A behavior remain supported except for
the explicit Run-wide completion invariant below" (2590-2591); "... remains green
except for the stated Run-wide completion safety change" (2824-2826).
Conflicting evidence:: The same contract mandates further N=1-visible changes (section
16 table): outcome dispositions (probe P1: today `failed`/`blocked`/`changes_required`

Reachable consequence:: A `max_autonomous_hops: 1` WorkItem whose result is

Changes:: one-slice: no; readiness: yes; architecture: no.
