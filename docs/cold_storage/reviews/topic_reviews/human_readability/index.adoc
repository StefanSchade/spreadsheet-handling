== Purpose and lifecycle

This bundle groups the completed three-family Human Readability investigation:
a sequence of read-only reviews, two bounded and independently accepted FK
implementation slices, two deliberate `Stop here` outcomes, a provisional
synthesis concept, and its independent review. It exists so a later reader can
reconstruct what the investigation established, what it only suggested, and which
residue was consciously left alone — without re-deriving it from scattered warm
storage.

The evidence here is historical. Current code, executable contracts, accepted
independent reviews, and corrected technical documentation outrank these
documents wherever they disagree. Nothing in this archive authorizes a runtime
change, a workflow rule, a static style check, a Human Readability Guide, or a
Human Readability Sentinel implementation.

== The three domain families

*FK Helpers — first family, two bounded accepted slices.* A real broad
raw-options bag justified making the raw/resolved split locally visible; a local
`target_registry` name overstated an execution lookup index. Two bounded slices
were implemented and independently accepted: the Enrich pilot Slice 1 and the
Formula Provider slice (which surfaced the existing `HelperValueProvider`
callback rather than redesigning it). The remaining residue did not justify
another cycle.

*Enrich Lookup — second family, counterevidence, correct `Stop here`.* There was
no comparable authored options bag. One coherent resolved policy was narrower
than all frames, yet scalarization would have scattered its invariants —
demonstrating that "narrow" means the smallest coherent owned value, not maximal
scalar atomization. `_ResolvedKeys` was a positive lifecycle/type boundary; real
workbook and persisted `sheet` vocabulary was retained. The review concluded
`Stop here` with no readability implementation.

*XRef Crosstable — third family, algorithmic locality, correct runtime `Stop
here`.* The 516-line operation and the 411-line dense-axis owner were positively
validated as cohesive and locally reconstructable; validation order was
contract-bearing; authored Intent and transient Resolution were distinct; and
`Any` was legitimate at opaque/heterogeneous boundaries. The only defects were
documentation: a stale package docstring and reversed direction descriptions in
the technical model, corrected at commit `8c769eb` (`expand_xref` is
matrix→relation; `contract_xref` is relation→matrix). No runtime readability
slice followed.

== Architecture-gated findings and why findings are not tasks

A readability observation is not a finding; a finding is not a task. A finding
that touches public, persisted, lifecycle, ownership, or architecture authority
transfers to that owner before any change (as `EL-HR-002` did). Implementation
requires an explicit authorized FTR or slice that defines scope, invariants,
characterization, non-goals, and acceptance; independent acceptance remains
distinct from implementation. `accept`, `defer`, `coordinate elsewhere`, and
`stop here` are all legitimate, semantically distinct dispositions.

== Concept 001 and Independent Review 001

Independent Review 001 accepted Concept 001 as a provisional, non-normative
concept: *0 Blocking, 0 Important, 4 Minor*. It remains provisional and
non-normative and creates no runtime, guide, Sentinel, workflow, or
automated-rule authority.

The four Minor qualifications are retained as guidance for later work and were
*not* resolved by editing the accepted concept:

. Measured human benefit was not established; the direct evidence is that
  truthful cues were *identified as reducing* caller/consumer/history questions
  (analytic, not measured).
. `HR-08` substantially overlaps `HR-02` and rests on thin, uneven evidence; it
  remains weak and provisional.
. The dispositions and the single deferred/accepted-residue bucket need sharper
  operational distinctions between `defer`, `accept`, and `stop here`.
. The trade-off framing centers reader cost but several pairs also trade
  contract, compatibility, maintenance, architecture, and change cost — not
  reader cost alone.
