==== 2. Problem statement and contract vocabulary

Current framework-managed execution has a load-time function named ingress,
but that function establishes only one `_meta.legend_blocks` shape. It does
not establish a scalar grammar, general metadata substrate, DataFrame or
`ExactTable` carrier contract, policy result, or post-step payload guarantee.
`run_pipeline` is intentionally step-only, and executable steps may replace
payload after the initial seam.

This design makes the following statement precise without using “trusted
payload” as a synonym for conformance:

[source,text]
----
Successful Trusted Ingress establishes a named payload-conformance state whose
assumptions downstream code may rely on under its declared execution-state
contract.
----

The following terms are used normatively in this design:

* *Ingress-Conformant Domain Payload* is the postcondition established over an
  external/router-loaded candidate or an uncertified producer return. Its
  ordinary DataFrame and `ExactTable.data` cells contain only the six-category
  Scalar grammar. It says nothing about who authored the payload.
* *Conformant Framework Execution State* is a later state in which every
  location not covered by a controlled role still satisfies the ordinary
  ingress postcondition, while zero or more exact reviewed framework
  transitions establish explicitly declared roles. The current closed role set
  is `LookupFormulaSpec` cell Intent, `GroupedMatrix` top-level structural
  carrier, and the artifact-manifest `source_frames` field. These roles are not
  external ingress carriers, Scalar categories, or `DeclaredComposite`.
* *controlled framework role* is a closed descriptor naming its kind, exact
  introducing transition, affected top-level/frame/column location, structural
  or cell contract, permitted aware/transparent/consuming transitions, and
  termination or conversion behavior. Framework authorship, Python class,
  factory, matching shape, or matching column name does not establish a role.
  Overlapping/nested roles are invalid unless an exact descriptor explicitly
  defines their composition. No public runtime role registry is required.
* *disjoint role product* is the coexistence of independently located roles.
  A transition preserves an existing disjoint role only when its exact
  read/write/rename/relocation footprint and locality proof establish that the
  existing role's location, contained payload, descriptor/provenance, and
  relocation identity are unchanged. Incidental non-mutation in one execution
  is not proof. A footprint may prove non-interference by locality; it need not
  enumerate every unrelated role or define a general commutative algebra.
* *nested role composition* is an exact reviewed transition in which one role
  location occurs within another. It is forbidden by default. Phase E admits
  exactly the maintained `GroupedMatrix` containing `LookupFormulaSpec` in the
  grouped dynamic cells derived from one exact formula-helper value column,
  under section 10's complete composition descriptor. No other nested role or
  grouped producer/configuration inherits that authority.
* *exact composition transition* derives, relocates, retains, consumes, or
  terminates the proof for an admitted composition, or preserves a disjoint
  role product through an exact locality/non-interference proof. It is semantic
  role authority, not an ordinary preserving certificate.
* *copy/derive* means that an authorized source role remains live at its exact
  location while another authorized occurrence is established at an exact new
  location. *Relocate* means authorization moves to a new exact location and
  the old location no longer carries that role. *Consume/terminate* means role
  authorization ends. *Pending cleanup* means the role remains in the
  published execution state at its current location while an exact later final-
  cleanup transition is authorized and required to terminate it. A cleanup
  marker is not termination. These are bounded semantic transition effects,
  not a general effect algebra or new role kinds.
* *conformant execution state* means either of those two states, according to
  the precondition declared by the next framework consumer.
* *ordinary Domain operation* is a transformation, cleanup, validation, or
  reconstruction consumer that declares the exact conformant execution-state
  contract it accepts as its general payload precondition. An ordinary scalar
  consumer that is not explicitly role-aware accepts only Ingress-Conformant
  Domain Payload. Family-specific preconditions remain additional.
* *conformance interval* is explanatory/contractual state: the period after a
  successful establishment or reviewed transition during which downstream code
  may rely on the applicable conformance facts, provided caller and producer
  obligations continue to hold. It is not a runtime token, ownership proof,
  alias tracker, mutation detector, or isolation guarantee.
* *preserving producer invocation* is one exact reviewed bound behavior and
  configuration whose closed output contract preserves its declared input
  execution-state contract. Callable/family review may establish eligibility;
  a callable name, registry entry, module, family, wrapper, factory, or
  built-in status is never the certificate.
* *controlled-role transition* is an exact reviewed bound behavior authorized
  to introduce, derive/copy, carry without relocating, relocate, consume, or
  terminate one named role under its closed descriptor. It is a required
  semantic transition for a maintained non-ordinary state. It is distinct from
  an optional preserving certificate that merely avoids redundant ordinary
  re-establishment.
* *uncertified producer* is an executable step without that reviewed producer
  invocation contract. Unknown/unreviewed options, nested user callbacks,
  mutable post-binding behavior, wrapper/factory composition, dotted plugins,
  generic custom callables, and caller-supplied `BoundStep` values are
  uncertified by default.
* *ordinary re-establishment* is a new successful Ingress-Conformant Domain
  Payload pass over an uncertified producer's returned candidate before the
  framework resumes ordinary macro flow. This design selects no dedicated
  controlled-role re-establishment. An uncertified transition ends every prior
  controlled-role authorization; `FormulaSpec`, `GroupedMatrix`, and manifest
  list cells therefore cannot gain or retain authority by identity, shape,
  location, copying, or returning the same object.

Four responsibilities remain independent throughout:

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
|Responsibility |Owner and meaning

|Description / control input
|Structural parsing and authorization of what operation/capability may be
selected. This is the pipeline-description trust plane.

|Payload input
|Recognized structural carriers, scalar grammar, framework-owned metadata
substrate, and any evidenced operator narrowing. This is the payload-
conformance responsibility of Trusted Ingress.

|Domain invariants
|Family- or reconstruction-local semantic validity, including reusable
Finding-based checks where appropriate.

|Output adaptation
|Persistence projection and sink-specific capability. Writers do not become
payload validators, and formula-looking text is not globally invalid.
|===

Runtime/operator trust remains a fixed precondition. Payload authority and
pipeline-description authority remain independent variables. Output-sink
capability remains separately owned. This design introduces no scalar trust
level. A less-trusted authored payload may become conformant; a trusted authored
payload may be non-conformant. Neither authority class bypasses the globally
supported grammar.

==== 3. Current-state evidence to which the design responds

The accepted census establishes these load-bearing facts:

* router-backed high-level flows converge on `orchestrate`, whose fixed order
  is load, current ingress, linear steps, final Domain cleanup, metadata
  persistence projection, and save;
* `sheets-run` and `run_app` bind configured strings before payload load, so a
  dotted import can activate capability before any payload ingress occurs;
* the current ingress coordinator has three production call sites and one
  Legend Blocks rule;
* the shared six-category scalar classifier has no production caller;
* DataFrame cell carriers differ across XLSX/ODS, CSV, JSON, YAML, XML, and
  direct Python inputs;
* exact spreadsheet paths may carry `ExactTable` into reconstruction;
* maintained `add_lookup_helpers(helper_value_mode="formula")` behavior creates
  framework-owned `LookupFormulaSpec` output Intent in DataFrame cells for the
  XLSX/ODS output adapters;
* maintained grouped-XRef producers publish `GroupedMatrix` as a top-level
  Frames carrier for exact inverse or workbook projection consumers;
* reference/graph warn reports can publish MultiIndex row labels as tuple cells,
  while the shared finding serializers already scalarize comparable diagnostic
  locations; and
* the implemented artifact manifest publishes `source_frames: list[str]` and
  has an exact JSON/YAML artifact-record writer;
* metadata has initial parser origins plus post-load bootstrap/override origins;
* every executable step return is passed directly to the next step, with no
  automatic post-step ingress; and
* `run_pipeline`, supported lower-level APIs, mixed direct reachability, and
  importable internals do not have one uniform public contract.

Those facts show why “call `scalar_category()` once in `orchestrate`” cannot be
the contract.

==== 5. Coverage-model evaluation and decision

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Model |Fit to repository |Decision

|A -- universal ingress
|A simple slogan, but it would either wrap every supported low-level call or
silently call importability public. It also obscures producer-created payload.
|Rejected. Too broad for the actual API and not the smallest truthful contract.

|B -- framework-managed guarantee
|Matches the main convergence seam and preserves `run_pipeline`, but by itself
does not answer replacement payload after a custom step.
|Necessary but insufficient alone.

|C -- capability/payload certification wrapper or type state
|Could track provenance across many producers, but current Frames APIs and
pandas-heavy transformations do not use wrapper state. Retrofitting it would
be a large API rewrite.
|Rejected for Phase E. No evidence justifies the complexity.

|D -- hybrid
|Framework-managed load establishes ordinary external conformance; exact
reviewed producer invocations preserve it or perform a named controlled-role
or exact composition transition; every other return loses role authority and
triggers ordinary re-establishment; lower-level APIs state caller
preconditions.
|Selected with a closed uncertified default. Preservation is an evidence-backed
optimization, not a requirement to certify the repository before wiring.
|===

The selected coverage model is therefore:

[source,text]
----
external router-loaded payload
  -> backend parse/conversion (including reserved YAML metadata sidecar)
  -> establish Ingress-Conformant Domain Payload
     [ordinary DataFrame/ExactTable data cells = six-category Scalar]

conformant ordinary execution state
  -> exact bound preserving invocation
       -> preserves the declared conformance interval
  -> exact reviewed controlled-role transition
       -> Conformant Framework Execution State
          [ordinary facts outside a closed, producer-declared role]
       -> only descriptor-declared aware, transparent, consuming, or exit
          transitions
  -> exact reviewed composition transition
       -> admit section 10's one nested FormulaSpec/GroupedMatrix role
          OR preserve disjoint roles through footprint/locality proof
  -> uncertified invocation
       -> returned candidate loses every controlled-role authorization
       -> re-establish Ingress-Conformant Domain Payload
       -> ordinary macro flow may resume only on success

output adaptation
  -> consumes ordinary values and declared roles under adapter/sink contract
  -> FormulaSpec-capable XLSX/ODS adaptation translates LookupFormulaSpec
  -> GroupedMatrix is projected to ExactTable/render IR before XLSX/ODS render
  -> manifest records are emitted only by a declared artifact adapter
  -> unsupported sink/role combinations reject or require prior resolution

trusted programmatic composition
  -> caller supplies the conformant execution state required by its first step
  -> caller owns every preserving, controlled-role, and re-establishment
     transition

supported lower-level Domain API
  -> local documented contract, including Ingress-Conformant Domain Payload
     where the operation consumes ordinary payload
----

The current controlled role set is deliberately narrow:

[source,text]

exact reviewed add_lookup_helpers invocation
  with helper_value_mode = formula
  and reviewed closed parameters
    -> may create LookupFormulaSpec only in its declared helper output columns
    -> may flow through declared cell-transparent cleanup/projection operations
    -> may be consumed by FormulaSpec-capable XLSX/ODS output adaptation

exact reviewed contract_grouped_xref or reconstruct_grouped_matrix invocation
  with its closed effective configuration
    -> may create GroupedMatrix only at its producer-declared frame role
    -> transition validation separately proves ordinary Scalar conformance of
       the contained DataFrame outside any explicitly authorized nested role
    -> may flow only through declared GroupedMatrix-aware/transparent consumers
    -> is consumed by expand_grouped_xref or projected to ExactTable/render IR

exact formula helper role followed by exact contract_grouped_xref(value=helper)
  under section 10's closed configuration
    -> retains every source helper FormulaSpec role at its existing location
    -> derives/copies the selected helper role only to corresponding grouped
       dynamic cells, establishing a disjoint product of the retained source
       role(s) and one nested GroupedMatrix[FormulaSpec] role
    -> keeps every other contained location ordinary Scalar
    -> may project to FormulaSpec-aware render IR or transition through matching
       expand_grouped_xref under section 10's exact retain/drop endpoints

exact transition beside an existing disjoint role
  under a bounded read/write/rename/relocation footprint
    -> may preserve the existing role only by non-interference proof
    -> supports repeated GroupedMatrix introduction at distinct top-level keys

exact reviewed write_artifact_manifest invocation
  with its closed output name and manifest schema
    -> may create list[str] only at
       ArtifactManifest.source_frames@producer-declared-output-frame
    -> may flow only through declared artifact-aware/whole-frame-transparent
       consumers
    -> is consumed by the manifest JSON/YAML artifact adapter or must be
       excluded/resolved before an unsupported general sink

validate_references or validate_graph warn/report output
  -> publishes row_index as a safe stable Scalar diagnostic projection
  -> therefore returns an ordinary report DataFrame, not a controlled role
----

An ordinary Domain transformation may not receive a controlled role unless its
exact contract is named in that role descriptor. “Transparent” is never
inferred because an implementation happened not to inspect one location in a
test: review must prove that the exact behavior does not interpret, relocate,
duplicate, transform, or expose the role. Sink capability remains separate:
authorizing a framework role does not authorize every output adapter.

Initial ingress always rejects externally supplied FormulaSpec, GroupedMatrix,
tuple/list cells, and other framework objects. An uncertified producer cannot
introduce, preserve, or authenticate a controlled role by constructing,
copying, mutating, or returning the same object; ordinary re-establishment
rejects it. If arbitrary/custom code intervenes, every prior role ends. A role
can reappear only through a fresh exact controlled transition after the
candidate has returned to ordinary state.

==== 7. Ordinary payload grammar and controlled framework roles

The Phase-E *external/ordinary* cell-payload grammar is positive and closed:

[source,text]
----
CellPayload       ::= Scalar | DeclaredComposite
Scalar            ::= String | Boolean | Number | Missing | Date | DateTime
DeclaredComposite ::= <no admitted variants in Phase E>
----

Successful ordinary scalar ingress is defined by membership returned by the
accepted `scalar_category(value)` classifier. Initial Phase E has no
configurable payload-policy matrix; the global grammar is the admitted set.

The globally supported carrier variants are exactly those owned by the current
Internal Scalar Value Model:

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
|Category |Admitted carrier variants

|String
|`str`, including `numpy.str_` through its string-subclass behavior.

|Boolean
|`bool`, `numpy.bool_`.

|Number
|`int`, `float`, `numpy.integer`, `numpy.floating`, subject to the classifier's
explicit exclusions.

|Missing
|`None`, `""`, supported float/NumPy NaN, `pandas.NA`, `pandas.NaT`, and
`numpy.datetime64('NaT')`, exactly as recognized by
`is_missing_carrier`; `numpy.timedelta64('NaT')` is not admitted.

|Date
|`datetime.date` after DateTime precedence.

|DateTime
|`datetime.datetime`, `pandas.Timestamp`, and every non-NaT
`numpy.datetime64`, with no unit-based Date inference.
|===

`datetime.time`, Duration/Timedelta carriers, `FormulaSpec`, spreadsheet Error,
collections, bytes, callables, classes, modules, generators, file handles, and
opaque objects are not scalar payload. An unsupported carrier does not become
valid because pandas can store it in an object-dtype cell.

The ordinary grammar remains unchanged. Framework execution uses a separate,
closed role algebra:

[source,text]
----
ConformantFrameworkExecutionState
  ::= OrdinaryFactsOutsideControlledRoles + ControlledRole*

ControlledRole
  ::= LookupFormulaSpec@declared-helper-cell-role
    | GroupedMatrix@producer-declared-frame-role
    | ArtifactManifestSourceFrames@producer-declared-output-frame.source_frames

ControlledRoleComposition
  ::= DisjointRoleProduct@exact-locality-proof
    | GroupedMatrix[
        LookupFormulaSpec@grouped-dynamic-value-cell-role
      ]@exact-formula-grouped-composition
----

This algebra is not initial ingress admission and is not a generic
“framework-owned object” exemption. Each role has the descriptor required by
section 2. The FormulaSpec role is a cell Intent; the `GroupedMatrix` role
replaces one ordinary top-level frame carrier; the manifest role permits one
exact `list[str]` field under its complete schema. Every location outside those
roles remains subject to the ordinary carrier, axis, Scalar, `ExactTable`, and
metadata facts. A role found at external ingress or after an uncertified return
is rejected. FormulaSpec and the manifest list are not Scalar or
`DeclaredComposite`; `GroupedMatrix` is not a cell value at all.

`ControlledRole*` permits multiple independent roles. It does not assert that
roles compose automatically. For a disjoint product, every later transition
that could affect an existing role MUST provide the exact footprint/locality
proof defined in section 2. For nesting, the only admitted composition is the
formula-in-grouped descriptor in section 10. All locations inside a controlled
carrier but outside that exact nested role continue to satisfy ordinary Scalar
facts. Matching class, factory, shape, current mapping key, copying, or a claim
that code “did not touch” an object proves neither product nor nesting.

The state algebra is bounded to every maintained non-ordinary framework state
discovered by accepted Follow-up Review 002 that would otherwise fail
conservative E5 re-establishment. It is not claimed complete for every future
value. A future non-ordinary value MUST add an explicit role and transition
contract before it can participate in a framework-managed conformance interval.
That addition needs an ADR only if it changes a durable authority axis.

DataFrame axes are structural labels, not `CellPayload`. Phase E preserves the
existing physical-column addressability authority:

* non-missing-like;
* hashable;
* unambiguously self-comparable; and
* unique where physical scalar addressing requires uniqueness.

“Deterministically comparable” in this contract means only that self-comparison
produces an unambiguous Boolean. It is not total ordering, cross-label
comparability, or sorting compatibility. Row indexes are positional locations,
not ordinary payload data, and Phase E imposes no new closed scalar/tuple
grammar on them.

The earlier `AxisAtom ::= Scalar` / finite-tuple proposal is retained only as a
candidate hypothesis for the E2 compatibility census. E2 may propose it (or a
different bounded grammar) only through a separately accepted design gate that
inventories framework-produced and supported consumer-reachable axes. E2 MUST
NOT silently tighten the accepted contract. Any confirmed supported label that
would be removed requires explicit compatibility/migration treatment before
implementation.

Classification occurs before any policy decision and before any future
category-specific normalization. The current design performs no scalar
normalization afterward.

The classifier's bounded dispatch is the ordinary-cell robustness boundary: it does not run
general equality, hashing, `repr`, string conversion, iteration, or NumPy array
protocols on an unsupported value. Trusted Ingress must not add such probes to
make diagnostics richer. It does not attempt to contain arbitrary hostile
Python already executing in process.

String content is never shape-guessed. Numeric-looking, boolean-looking,
date-looking, formula-looking, SQL-looking, shell-looking, and HTML-looking
strings remain String. Sink-specific interpretation remains sink-owned.

==== 8. Classification, normalization, and Missing

The scalar-normalization alternatives were evaluated as follows:

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Alternative |Benefit |Disposition

|A -- validate only
|Preserves accepted carriers, pandas behavior, temporal precision/timezone,
and the established classifier boundary.
|Selected for scalar payload.

|B -- minimal canonicalization
|Could stabilize a demonstrated problematic carrier, but no such general
post-classification carrier is evidenced for this slice.
|Preserved as a future per-category option, not selected now.

|C -- full canonical scalar set
|Would simplify types superficially but force precision, timezone, Missing,
and pandas rewrite decisions without consumer evidence.
|Rejected.

|D -- category-specific policy
|Is the correct shape if a future category proves it needs conversion.
|Extension rule only; all six categories currently specify identity
preservation.
|===

Ordinary payload-conformance ingress therefore does not convert `numpy.int64` to `int`,
`numpy.bool_` to `bool`, `pandas.Timestamp` to `datetime.datetime`, or
`numpy.datetime64` to another carrier. It does not strip/convert timezone
information or reduce precision. DataFrame dtype behavior may still box values
according to pandas; ingress does not add another conversion.

Missing remains recognition, not representation. Every admitted Missing carrier
is preserved. In particular:

* `""` is not replaced by `None` or a sentinel;
* NaN/NA/NaT carriers are not globally unified;
* literal string `"nan"` remains String, not Missing; and
* ordinary empty domain text remains possible. Where `""` means missing,
  unresolved presentation, or legitimate empty text depends on the consuming
  feature's authority and context, not on a new ingress sentinel.

Successful ordinary ingress promises that every Missing-shaped cell is one of the
accepted recognized carriers. It does not promise representation equality
between Missing cells. Downstream generic code may use the accepted missing
predicate; family semantics such as unresolved FK authority remain unchanged.

Ingress may still perform two non-scalar forms of normalization:

* construct a new owned top-level Frames mapping and owned metadata containers;
  and
* apply explicit feature-owned authoring-to-canonical metadata rules such as
  the existing Legend Blocks rule.

Parser conversion and persistence-local conversion remain owned by their
respective boundaries and are not renamed scalar normalization.

Creating `LookupFormulaSpec`, publishing `GroupedMatrix`, or publishing the
manifest-specific list role under its reviewed transition is neither scalar
normalization nor external payload admission. Each is an explicit controlled
framework transition governed by sections 5, 10, and 12.

===== `GroupedMatrix` lifecycle decision

*Option G1 -- transition-only internal carrier is selected.* The two current
introducing behaviors are exact reviewed invocations of
`contract_grouped_xref` and `reconstruct_grouped_matrix`, including their
closed effective configuration. The role location is the exact producer-
declared top-level frame name.

The existing grouped factory proves that the current canonical flat physical
column schema projects to the carried immutable `GroupedHeader` descriptor
under the resolved mapping. It does not prove the six-category cell grammar,
metadata conformance, arbitrary future mutation safety, or the family-owned
geometry, bijection, source-axis, visible-tuple, or inverse-reconstruction
semantics. The controlled transition therefore adds a separate check that the
contained DataFrame's physical columns satisfy current authority and that all
contained locations outside an explicitly authorized nested role are ordinary
Scalar. The only such nested role is the exact FormulaSpec-in-`GroupedMatrix`
composition below. The `GroupedHeader` is carrier-owned structure, not `_meta`
and not a cell composite.

The current closed consumer/exit chain is:

[source,text]
----
GroupedMatrix@producer-declared-frame-role
  -> configure_workbook_view
       [role-aware: validates selection; does not apply column options]
  -> final whole-frame cleanup / persistence-metadata projection
       [transparent only for reviewed behavior that neither opens nor relocates
        the carrier]
  -> select_render_frames
       [role-aware selection/order/rename without opening the carrier]
  -> compose_workbook / grouped_matrix_to_exact_table
       [consuming output projection to ExactTable/TableBlock/render IR]
  -> XLSX/ODS renderer

or

GroupedMatrix@producer-declared-frame-role
  -> expand_grouped_xref
       [role-aware transition; revalidates the pair under the live mapping,
        publishes ordinary DataFrame output for a standalone role, or performs
        section 10's exact FormulaSpec product/location transition for the
        admitted nested role; drop_source=false retains the same carrier;
        drop_source=true terminates GroupedMatrix identity inside expansion,
        publishes the restored flat source at the same logical key, and marks
        that flat source for later cleanup]
  -> if the standalone contained/output cells are ordinary, ordinary
     re-establishment may follow after carrier termination; if a FormulaSpec
     role remains at the retained, flat pending-cleanup, or output location,
     only another descriptor-declared role transition may follow
----

Concrete XLSX/ODS renderers do not consume `GroupedMatrix` identity directly;
backend-neutral workbook composition consumes it by exact projection first.
Any other consumer or sink rejects unless a later exact descriptor extends the
role. Column-targeted workbook-view operations are not transparent and remain
unsupported for this role.

Option G2, dedicated structural re-establishment, is rejected. Recovering a
plugin-produced carrier would require the live grouped mapping/configuration
and family reconstruction authority, not merely independently checkable generic
carrier state. Centralizing that proof in ingress would weaken the accepted
separation. A plugin-created, copied, mutated, or same-object `GroupedMatrix`
therefore loses all role authority at return; ordinary re-establishment rejects
it even if the feature factory was used. Only a fresh exact controlled producer
transition may introduce the role.

===== Controlled-role composition decision

Two or more roles may be live at once only under one of two proofs.

* A *disjoint role product* uses an exact transition footprint and a proof of
  non-interference. The transition MUST preserve every affected existing
  role's current top-level location, contained payload, role metadata and
  producer provenance, and rename/relocation identity. The proof is semantic:
  copying the Frames mapping, retaining the same Python object, or observing
  that one test did not mutate it does not establish transparency. A bounded
  footprint may prove that an unrelated location cannot be affected, so a
  transition need not enumerate every unrelated role.
* A *nested role composition* remains invalid unless this FTR names its exact
  producer, nested locations, remainder, transitions, and termination. The
  only admitted nested composition is the one below.

The repeated grouped introduction is the minimum evidenced disjoint-product
case. Given a live `GroupedMatrix` role at top-level key A, a later exact
`contract_grouped_xref` transition publishing key B preserves A only when B is
distinct and the resolved transition footprint proves no read/write/drop/
rename/relocation of A, its contained frame, or its descriptor. The result has
two independently authorized `GroupedMatrix` roles. The same locality rule may
preserve multiple exact FormulaSpec helper roles at disjoint declared cell
locations when the introducing transition's closed footprint proves the other
locations unchanged. This is not a general commutative algebra and creates no
public or universal runtime role registry.

The formula-to-grouped producer is the one exact role-aware use of a selected
source FormulaSpec helper role. It derives/copies the selected role into the
nested grouped locations while retaining every source helper role. Its output
therefore applies the same accepted product semantics to the retained source
role(s) and the newly established nested role. This is not generic authority to
read another role as producer input.

===== FormulaSpec-in-`GroupedMatrix` composition descriptor

The following exact maintained chain is admitted:

[source,text]
----
Ingress-Conformant Domain Payload
  -> add_lookup_helpers(helper_value_mode="formula")
       [introduces LookupFormulaSpec in exact declared helper value cells]
  -> contract_grouped_xref(value=<that helper column>,
                            row_keys=<including its source-key column>, ...)
       [composition transition: retains every exact source helper role and
        derives/copies the selected formula cells into the grouped dynamic
        cells derived from that value column]
  -> LookupFormulaSpec@retained-source-helper-cell-role
       * GroupedMatrix[
           LookupFormulaSpec@grouped-dynamic-value-cell-role
         ]@exact-formula-grouped-composition
  -> bounded workbook view / render selection
       [either excludes/terminates an unselected source helper frame or carries
        a selected source helper role only through its exact standalone path]
  -> grouped_matrix_to_exact_table / compose_workbook
       [consumes GroupedMatrix identity and relocates FormulaSpec to the exact
        TableBlock/render-IR formula-cell role]
  -> XLSX/ODS FormulaSpec adapter
       [consumes FormulaSpec]
----

The nested descriptor requires all of the following:

* formula provenance is the exact reviewed `add_lookup_helpers` formula-mode
  invocation, including its declared source frame/key, lookup frame/key, and
  helper output column; no pre-existing, copied, or merely type-matching value
  is promoted;
* the outer producer is the exact reviewed `contract_grouped_xref`
  configuration selecting that helper output as `value`; this authority is not
  inherited by `reconstruct_grouped_matrix` or another grouped producer;
* only dynamic cells mapped from that selected value column may contain
  `LookupFormulaSpec`; every other contained data location is ordinary Scalar;
* the contained DataFrame still satisfies current physical-column authority,
  and the grouped constructor/family continues to own pairing, geometry,
  bijection, mapping, and reconstruction semantics;
* the configured grouped row keys retain the FormulaSpec source-key column so
  its same-row reference remains defined; a configuration that discards or
  changes that reference is outside the descriptor and rejects;
* the composition transition records derivation/copy from the selected declared
  helper column to the exact grouped dynamic locations. The source helper role
  remains live; neither factory identity nor dynamic-column shape establishes
  the derived provenance;
* every FormulaSpec helper role in the source frame remains at its exact source
  frame/column location. Only the selected grouped value helper gains the
  derived nested occurrence; unselected helper roles stay standalone and no
  source helper role is consumed merely because grouping occurs;
* with `drop_source=false`, all source helper roles remain normally live. With
  `drop_source=true`, the exact transition return still contains the source
  formula frame and nested grouped role, and the source roles are pending
  cleanup rather than terminated. Configured steps may run before final
  cleanup. Exact final cleanup terminates every role located only in that source
  frame when it removes the whole frame; and
* every later transition is limited to those below. No formula may be nested
  in an arbitrary grouped cell, and no other overlap is admitted.

`configure_workbook_view` is role-aware only for the bounded selection/
ordering behavior that preserves the contained carrier. The role's logical
Domain location remains its current top-level mapping key; a `GroupedMatrix`
output-sheet rename binds a separate physical projection name in workbook-view
metadata and the transition carries that binding with the outer role. A true
top-level key rename or relocation requires its own exact transition. Workbook
view MUST NOT apply column-targeted options to the carrier. For this nested composition,
the referenced lookup sheet name MUST remain unchanged. The maintained frame
selector does not open a `GroupedMatrix` to rewrite nested FormulaSpec sheet
references, and the output adapter consequently rejects a missing old sheet.
Therefore renaming the referenced lookup sheet is unsupported and E5 MUST
reject that configuration before renderer entry. This is compatibility rule
*B*, not a claim of workbook-view transparency. Unrelated selection or rename
is allowed only under the exact locality/non-interference rule.

Workbook composition consumes the outer role at
`grouped_matrix_to_exact_table`/`compose_workbook`; FormulaSpec survives the
projection by relocation from the grouped dynamic locations to corresponding
dynamic render cells and is then consumed by the XLSX/ODS FormulaSpec adapter.
The old grouped locations no longer exist after outer-role consumption. No
concrete renderer receives raw `GroupedMatrix` identity.

`expand_grouped_xref` is also an exact composition transition when applied to
this nested role. Under the matching inverse configuration it derives the
configured output DataFrame FormulaSpec value-column role from the nested
dynamic cells; that output is not ordinary while the role remains.

With `drop_source=false`, the unchanged nested source role and the disjoint
output FormulaSpec role both remain live. This is a product transition: no old
location is consumed or called ordinary.

With `drop_source=true`, expansion restores the flat source DataFrame and
replaces the source key with that frame before returning. GroupedMatrix identity
and its nested-role location terminate inside `expand_grouped_xref`. FormulaSpec
authorization relocates from the nested dynamic cells to the exact restored
flat source value locations, and those standalone FormulaSpec cells remain in
the published state with a pending-cleanup marker. Expansion also derives the
standalone FormulaSpec role in the configured output value column. The exact
pre-cleanup post-state is therefore:

[source,text]
----
FormulaSpec@restored-flat-source-value-locations[pending-cleanup]
  * FormulaSpec@expanded-output-value-column
----

Final cleanup later removes the whole flat source frame and terminates only the
pending-cleanup FormulaSpec role(s) located there; the output FormulaSpec role
remains. Final cleanup does not consume GroupedMatrix identity because that role
already ended inside expansion. A mismatched inverse, dropped reference key,
unsupported rename, or later uncertified return ends all roles and rejects
under ordinary re-establishment.

Termination is therefore exact: workbook composition consumes the outer
carrier role and relocates the nested FormulaSpec role into render cells before
the capable output adapter consumes it; matching retain-mode expansion keeps
the nested source and derives the output role; matching drop-mode expansion
terminates the outer role, relocates FormulaSpec to the restored flat pending-
cleanup locations, and derives the output role; final cleanup terminates the
pending-cleanup flat-source role; explicit frame/cell removal may terminate
other roles; and every uncertified return terminates all standalone, disjoint,
and nested authority. No controlled-role re-establishment or new pending-drop
role kind is introduced.

===== Validation finding-frame decision

*Option R1 -- scalarize diagnostic location is selected.* Reference and graph
warn/report producers MUST publish `row_index` as an ordinary Scalar-compatible
diagnostic value. An already-admitted Scalar label may be preserved. A
multi-component label is projected by the shared diagnostic serializer to a
stable String that preserves ordered component boundaries sufficiently for
diagnosis; an unsupported/unsafe label is reported by positional ordinal rather
than inspected. This is diagnostic presentation, not a reversible Domain-key
codec, and exact formatting remains implementation-local.

This matches the shared finding-frame architecture, whose other producers
already render tuple labels/values to strings, and allows generic tabular report
consumers and persistence paths to receive an ordinary DataFrame. It does not
admit arbitrary tuples or establish a generic report exemption. The narrow
migration belongs to E4's minimum maintained-state work and requires release
notes because a MultiIndex finding cell changes from Python tuple to Scalar
diagnostic representation.

===== Artifact-manifest role decision

*Option M1 -- manifest-specific controlled artifact role is selected.* An exact
reviewed `write_artifact_manifest` invocation may establish
`ArtifactManifestSourceFrames` only in column `source_frames` of its producer-
declared output frame and only under the complete existing manifest schema.
Each such value is a freshly owned exact built-in `list` containing only
strings, with one level and no opaque or nested values. Every other manifest
cell is ordinary Scalar.

The role may pass only through reviewed whole-frame-transparent cleanup or
metadata projection and is consumed by the manifest's exact JSON/YAML artifact
record adapter (`_manifest_to_records` / the owned writer). Generic ordinary
Domain steps may not receive it. A selected final sink must explicitly support
the manifest role or the frame must be excluded/resolved before that sink;
current generic backends do not provide a stable cross-format manifest-role
round trip. External JSON/YAML reload does not recreate role authority.

The manifest role is transition-only. A plugin-created list, matching column,
copied legitimate manifest frame, or same-object return loses authority and is
rejected by ordinary re-establishment. This preserves the implemented manifest
semantics without admitting generic `List[Scalar]`, delimiter heuristics, or
arbitrary report objects.

Controlled carrier/role instances may be produced inside a conformance interval
only by their reviewed transitions. External mappings that merely resemble
them are never promoted by shape, and dataclass/factory identity never bypasses
payload grammar. E2 MUST complete and independently accept its physical-axis
census before proposing any vocabulary narrower than the current physical-
column contract. Such a proposal is a design gate, not hidden implementation
freedom.

==== 11. Metadata contract

`_meta` participates in payload-conformance ingress, but cell payload and
metadata remain different flows. Phase E accepts this contract now:

* `_meta` is absent or becomes an owned, bounded, acyclic framework metadata
  substrate before publication;
* arbitrary opaque object graphs, callables, binary/runtime objects, and
  undeclared recursion are not valid merely because Python can store them;
* external/persisted metadata and later runtime-created metadata remain
  distinct origins whose relevant transitions are checked;
* family meaning, references, conflicts, and invariants remain local; and
* root-specific authoring normalization is possible only through the closed
  delegate contract below.

The exact node vocabulary is *not yet accepted*. The preferred hypothesis is
an exact built-in `dict` root and nested mappings, exact string keys, exact
built-in `list` sequences, admitted scalar leaves, identity-based cycle
detection, and explicitly named root-specific structural contracts. Before E3,
a focused compatibility census MUST inventory current external/persisted and
runtime-produced/consumed node kinds, aliases, and special roots. That evidence
gate must be independently accepted and must either:

. confirm the preferred vocabulary;
. revise it to another bounded positive vocabulary; or
. name every supported-shape migration and release consequence.

Here “bounded” means a closed positive node/edge vocabulary and named delegate
roots. It does not promise maximum depth, cardinality, bytes, or traversal cost;
those resource limits remain separately owned and are explicit non-guarantees.

E3 MUST NOT silently turn the preferred hypothesis into runtime rejection.
`meta_registry.yaml` remains a manual review artifact and MUST NOT become
runtime authority without separate architecture.

===== Controlled metadata delegate governance

A metadata ingress delegate MUST:

* be centrally reviewed and owned; arbitrary family/plugin code cannot
  dynamically register one;
* be selected by a statically declared root contract, not by caller-provided
  executable identity;
* operate only on a staged candidate and perform no caller-input mutation;
* avoid unsafe `repr`, `str`, truthiness, equality, hashing, iteration, or
  protocol probing on a rejected unsupported value beyond operations explicitly
  safe in its already-admitted container contract;
* return either the common accepted substrate or one explicitly named
  root-specific structural contract;
* have its entire output checked against that declared postcondition before
  publication;
* never use delegate status to introduce opaque values or bypass the global
  bounded/acyclic structural postcondition; and
* own authoring shape only, not absorb family semantics merely because it
  normalizes the root.

The current Legend Blocks normalizer is compatibility evidence for the
authoring form, not an automatically approved delegate implementation. E3 must
migrate its Mapping acceptance, identity resolution, shallow alias retention,
and `str`/truthiness behavior to the accepted node vocabulary and the rules
above.

Bootstrap and YAML overrides MUST build merged candidates without mutating the
published Frames graph, run accepted substrate validation and every relevant
delegate, and publish only on success. Exact preserving producer invocations
may create framework/family metadata under their reviewed output contract;
uncertified producer returns undergo complete ordinary re-establishment.

===== YAML reserved metadata-sidecar decision

*Option A is selected.* In `yaml_dir`, every input file whose stem is `_meta`
(`_meta.yaml` or `_meta.yml`) is a reserved framework metadata sidecar. The YAML
backend, not Domain ingress, safe-loads at most one such file and places its
mapping value at `Frames["_meta"]` before payload-conformance ingress. A
non-mapping root or duplicate `.yaml`/`.yml` reserved sidecar is rejected at the
backend/parse boundary with a stable compatibility error. Domain ingress never
reinterprets an already-created DataFrame as metadata.

This choice is consistent with `_meta` being reserved, JSON's mapping-sidecar
interpretation, and the YAML saver's existing refusal to write an ordinary
`_meta` sheet. It does not add a YAML metadata-roundtrip promise: the current
YAML saver may continue to omit `_meta` unless persistence work separately
changes that contract.

The observable migration is that a YAML file with stem `_meta` can no longer be
addressed as an ordinary DataFrame sheet. That was not a stable roundtrip
contract because the maintained saver already skips `_meta`; consumers that
used it as ordinary data must rename the file/sheet, while consumers intending
metadata must provide a mapping compatible with the E3-accepted substrate.
E3 owns the loader conversion/rejection and MUST include release-note
documentation for the reserved-name change. No release note is created by this
design correction.

“Custom output is automatically untrusted” is not the decision. Its authorship
may be fully trusted; its payload conformance is simply uncertified until
re-established. Likewise, “built-in output is automatically trusted” is not the
decision: exact behavior becomes preserving only through a reviewed closed
contract.

The applicable conformance assumption remains available only during a
*conformance interval*. This is explanatory/contractual state, not mechanically
tracked state. The framework can know that a pass succeeded, that an exact
invocation has a reviewed contract, or that an uncertified return passed
re-establishment. It cannot know or prevent retained DataFrame/nested aliases,
later caller writes, concurrent mutation, plugin closures, threads, or other
same-process writes.

Therefore no-out-of-band mutation is a caller and preserving-producer
*obligation*, not an isolation guarantee. A preserving invocation contract MUST
state that its own work does not intentionally retain or exercise a mutation
path that violates the interval, but certification does not prove absence of
all aliases in the process.

Framework-managed loaded DataFrames need not be deep-copied solely to establish
atomicity because scalar validation is read-only. Logical ownership transfers to
the macro for the duration of the call. An implementation may copy for another
documented reason, but this contract does not require a full DataFrame copy.

===== Bounded E4 migration rule

E4 defines the transition representation, exact-invocation granularity, closed
uncertified default, and two deliberately separate sets:

* the optional *preserving-certificate set* may be zero; it is an optimization
  that omits redundant ordinary re-establishment for an exact invocation whose
  ordinary-state postcondition is already closed; and
* the required *controlled transition/state set* contains every maintained
  non-ordinary state needed by current framework-managed flows: FormulaSpec,
  `GroupedMatrix`, their one exact nested composition, source-retaining forward
  derivation, reverse retain product, reverse drop relocation to a standalone
  pending-cleanup flat FormulaSpec location plus output role, the bounded
  disjoint-role locality/product transition, the R1 scalarized reference/graph
  report transition, and the manifest-specific role and exits.

A controlled-role descriptor may name exact aware, transparent, consuming, and
exit transitions that retain or discharge its role. Those are required parts
of the role lifecycle, not preserving optimizations and not entries in a broad
family certification census. E4 does *not* audit every transformation family,
all 44 registry forms, or every parameter combination.

[source,text]
----
proven exact preserving invocation
  -> omit redundant full re-establishment

proven exact controlled-role invocation
  -> apply its named introduction/aware/transparent/consuming/exit transition
     and closed role checks

proven exact composition/locality invocation
  -> derive/copy, retain, relocate, consume, or terminate the one admitted
     nested/standalone role endpoints exactly as declared, or preserve an
     existing disjoint role only under its non-interference proof

anything else
  -> all prior standalone, disjoint, and nested role authority ends
  -> ordinary re-establishment of the returned candidate
  -> reject unless it reaches Ingress-Conformant Domain Payload
----

There is no general role re-establishment path in this design. Consequently an
uncertified return is not *expected* to become ordinary; it is subjected to the
closed ordinary rule and rejects when any FormulaSpec, `GroupedMatrix`, manifest
list role, tuple report cell, or other unsupported value remains. A controlled
role can reappear only through a fresh exact introducing transition.

This conservative rule lets E5 reach the Payload-Conformance Milestone without
pretending every built-in preserves because the minimum required role set is
closed before wiring. Later family hardening may add exact preserving
certificates as a performance optimization without changing Model D or Phase-E
completion. A failed pass still cannot roll back custom side effects or prior
successful steps.

==== 13. Operator payload-policy disposition

Global framework capability and operator policy remain architecturally
different, but Phase E has no evidenced need to expose configurable payload
narrowing:

[source,text]
----
initial Phase-E payload capability = globally supported payload grammar
----

E1 implements the global ordinary scalar grammar, not cell-category,
carrier-variant, metadata-leaf, or structural-carrier policy knobs. No current
operator requirement justifies those dimensions. In particular, carrier
variants remain admitted according to Phase D, and an independent metadata-
leaf knob must not break valid framework metadata merely because it is
configurable.

The architecture still preserves the accepted ADR rule for any *future
evidenced policy kind*: it is supplied by the trusted operator outside a
less-trusted description, fixed for the relevant conformance interval,
narrowing-only, and combined per kind. Unknown, contradictory, incomparable, or
widening requests reject. No policy can make an unsupported object, undeclared
collection, external FormulaSpec, Time, or Duration into ordinary scalar
payload. A future description may request only additional restriction for an
already-defined kind.

Trusted/less-trusted payload remains exclusively an authorship/authority
assessment. A less-trusted authored payload may become Ingress-Conformant; a
trusted authored payload may fail conformance. Both receive the same globally
supported grammar unless a separately accepted operator-policy use site
defines narrowing.

Maximum bytes/cells/text lengths, parser limits, metadata-root authorization,
filesystem capabilities, formula policy, and custom-step capability are not
added to this payload policy without their own concrete owning use sites.
Parser/resource bounds and sink capability remain separately owned.

This design does not choose payload-policy transport, CLI syntax, environment
variables, profile names, or a public policy class. E7 separately requires
operator-owned *description capability* policy because that use site is already
mandated by the accepted ADR and capability-activation evidence.

==== 14. Pipeline-description trust and capability activation

Payload-conformance ingress and pipeline-description authorization are two
coordinated but separately owned subcontracts:

[source,text]
----
description/control plane:
  parse shape -> prepare/canonicalize identifier -> combine policy -> authorize
  -> only then import/bind/acquire/invoke governed capability

payload plane:
  parse/load carrier -> structural/scalar/metadata conformance -> ordinary
  Domain or reviewed controlled-role transition
----

Their execution order is not universally serial: current CLI and `run_app`
bind descriptions before loading payload. Therefore a later successful payload
pass cannot retroactively authorize an earlier import.

Phase E's minimum description seam is:

* an entry must state whether its description is trusted or less trusted;
* less-trusted description requests must be combined with operator policy
  outside that description;
* safe syntactic parsing, structural checks, static policy metadata lookup, and
  identifier canonicalization may occur before authorization;
* Python module import is capability activation and MUST NOT occur before an
  allow decision;
* direct Python callables and pre-bound `BoundStep` values are trusted
  programmatic capabilities, not a supported less-trusted-description format;
  and
* description authorization success does not establish payload conformance,
  and payload ingress success does not authorize the description.

Concrete description policy/allowlist/binding mechanics belong to E7 after an
*accepted* dotted-extension-surface architecture assessment supplies its per-
surface stability contract. That assessment remains open; this FTR does not
pretend its current draft is accepted. Until E7 is accepted and implemented,
`sheets-run` and `run_app` publish payload-conformance guarantees only under a
mandatory trusted-description precondition. This is a support boundary, not a
claim that less-trusted descriptions become safe after payload ingress.

===== Phase-E completion decision

*Option A is selected.* E1-E6 may reach an independently reviewed and accepted
*Phase-E Payload-Conformance Milestone* under the mandatory trusted-description
precondition. That milestone is useful but does not complete Phase E. Phase E
remains *Open* until E7 is independently accepted and its less-trusted-
description authorization contract is implemented.

While Phase E is in that intermediate state:

* current CLI/`run_app` configured execution MUST document that its description
  is trusted operator input;
* no less-trusted configured YAML protection may be advertised;
* the Domain Maintainer Contract, Phase F, and D-T3 remain blocked by the
  roadmap's Phase-E prerequisite; and
* E7 continues to depend on the accepted output of
  `FTR-PIPELINE-DOTTED-EXTENSION-SURFACE-P4A`, not merely its current draft.

No attempt is made to sandbox trusted Python.

==== 15. Failure atomicity

Atomicity is local to the work performed by conformance ingress and every
approved metadata or controlled-role delegate/transition validator:

* traversal and scalar classification are read-only;
* structural and metadata normalization are built in staged containers;
* the top-level candidate is not published to the caller, next step, cleanup,
  persistence projection, or saver until every required check succeeds;
* failure on cell N does not expose normalization performed for cells or
  metadata visited earlier;
* ingress/delegates MUST NOT mutate caller-owned Frames, DataFrames, `_meta`, or
  retained nested metadata as part of their own work; and
* no ordinary configured step or saver runs after an initial ingress failure.

This does not require transactional machinery. A staged top-level mapping plus
owned staged metadata and read-only cell traversal is sufficient under the
validate-only scalar decision. Input DataFrames need not be copied when they
are not mutated.

This is not a concurrency, alias, or ownership-isolation guarantee. The
framework does not prove that another actor did not mutate a retained DataFrame
or nested alias during the attempt, and it does not prevent later caller,
closure, thread, plugin, or other same-process mutation. No DataFrame deep copy
is required merely to pretend otherwise.

For re-establishment after an uncertified step, rejection prevents that
candidate from reaching the next ordinary Domain operation, final cleanup, or
framework saver. It cannot roll back mutations or external side effects the
custom callable already performed, nor prior successfully completed pipeline
steps, and it cannot revoke aliases retained by that callable. Plugins with such
effects remain trusted-programmatic capability and must document/own them.

==== 16. Error and diagnostic model

Boundary rejection raises exceptions; it does not emit reusable Domain
`Finding` values. Findings describe Domain semantic/invariant observations and
remain separately governed by severity policy.

The contract requires distinguishable failure kinds:

[cols="3,4",options="header"]
|===
|Failure kind |Required diagnostic context

|Unsupported scalar carrier
|Frame and positional column/row ordinal, or `ExactTable` data row/column
ordinal; constant safe detail. The rejected value is never rendered.

|Invalid structural carrier
|Top-level frame name when it is an exact validated string, carrier role, and
failed structural field/ordinal.

|Invalid metadata substrate
|Safe metadata path composed only from keys and ordinals admitted by the
accepted E3 node vocabulary; expected node kind. Delegates obey the same
rejected-value inspection rules.

|Unauthorized controlled framework carrier/role
|Safe frame/positional or top-level role location, expected declared role kind,
and transition contract identifier. The rejected object is never rendered.

|Future evidenced payload-policy rejection
|Safe location, classified semantic category/carrier kind, and policy kind
that rejected it. No value content is required.

|Pipeline-description authorization rejection
|Description location, declarative identifier/capability kind where safely
available, and policy reason. It is a description-plane exception, not an
ingress scalar error.
|===

A typed exception hierarchy or an exception carrying a stable `kind` field is
required by contract; exact class names are an implementation choice. The
boundary may fail fast at the first deterministic error. Multi-error collection
and report/result objects are optional later enhancements.

Diagnostics MUST NOT call `repr`, `str`, equality, hashing, iteration, or NumPy
protocols on a rejected unsupported value. Frame/row/column values that have not
themselves passed a safe structural contract are reported by ordinal/role, not
by content. This restriction does not prohibit the minimum self-comparison and
hash operations required to test a candidate physical column label against the
existing addressability contract; it prohibits additional diagnostic
inspection. Metadata delegates do not gain such an exception for unsupported
leaf values.

Role facts are not proved by initial ingress or Python identity. Existing
constructors prove only their already-owned structural invariants; the
transition proves the additional role/location and ordinary-remainder facts.
Continuing absence of out-of-band mutation is a
*CALLER/PRESERVING-PRODUCER OBLIGATION*, not a framework isolation fact.

===== C. After an uncertified producer return

The prior conformance interval and every controlled-role authorization end.
The returned candidate may reach the next ordinary Domain operation, cleanup,
projection, or saver only after complete ordinary re-establishment succeeds.
Success proves the same facts as section 17.A. It is not promised that every
candidate can become ordinary: FormulaSpec, `GroupedMatrix`, manifest list
cells, tuple report cells, every controlled-role composition, and every other
undeclared non-ordinary value reject.
A later exact reviewed producer may introduce a fresh controlled role again.
Re-establishment proves nothing about operations or side effects that occurred
inside the uncertified callable.

This bounded postcondition is useful enough for later family hardening to
remove arbitrary-object/callable/container defenses from ordinary scalar paths
that genuinely declare the conformance precondition. It does not authorize
removal of carrier-variant handling, Missing recognition, comparison/sort/hash
rules, family-specific label identity/role rules, FormulaSpec lifecycle checks,
or family semantics. Role-aware family consumers may remove only defenses made
redundant by their exact descriptor; no generic ordinary consumer may infer
permission from “framework-created” identity.

==== 18. Explicit downstream non-guarantees

Successful conformance establishment or a reviewed execution transition does
not prove:

* required frames or columns exist;
* FK targets/references exist or resolve;
* values in key columns, visible tuples, or Domain mappings are unique;
* grouped geometry, header depth, row keys, or reconstruction bijection is
  valid beyond generic carrier self-consistency;
* a transformation can sort, compare, hash, or serialize every admitted
  category for its own semantics;
* uniform comparison, sorting, hashing, equality, timezone, or Missing behavior
  across admitted scalar carriers;
* family configuration or every `_meta` root is semantically valid;
* metadata is authoritative merely because it has framework shape;
* cleanup has been selected or output is canonical;
* a string is safe for every sink, including XLSX formula interpretation;
* persistence roundtrip preserves carrier identity, temporal precision, or
  timezone-name identity beyond each backend's own contract;
* the pipeline description is authorized, unless the separate description
  boundary has also succeeded;
* an arbitrary trusted callable cannot misbehave or perform side effects; or
* payload or metadata resource boundedness beyond separately owned parser/
  resource contracts;
* concurrent mutation, retained-alias isolation, later same-process mutation,
  or rollback of producer/plugin effects;
* a framework-created output Intent is an ordinary scalar merely because it
  occupies a DataFrame cell;
* independently valid roles compose without an exact product or nesting proof;
* an implementation that happened not to mutate an object is role-transparent;
* any FormulaSpec may occupy any `GroupedMatrix` cell or enter every grouped
  operation;
* a role product requires a generic registry or requires every transition to
  enumerate every unrelated role;
* a framework-created object, tuple/list cell, carrier factory, matching schema,
  or report name establishes any controlled role;
* a `GroupedMatrix` factory proves contained-cell conformance or all grouped
  reconstruction semantics;
* an artifact manifest role is portable through every general sink or becomes
  authorized ordinary payload on reload;
* a reviewed producer-preservation contract prevents arbitrary same-process
  mutation outside that contractual obligation; or
* process, filesystem, network, container, or Python sandbox isolation exists.

Ingress validity is not reconstruction validity, transformation precondition,
Domain invariant validity, persistence projection, or sink safety.

==== 19. Bounded-composite extension seam

The `DeclaredComposite` branch in section 7 is intentionally empty. A future
bounded composite such as `List[Scalar]` can be added only by a separately
accepted contract that defines:

* a named composite kind distinct from arbitrary Python collection identity;
* a finite cardinality/depth/resource bound;
* the permitted scalar-leaf grammar and Missing behavior;
* an explicit carrier and ownership/copying rule;
* a schema/declaration and operator policy capability;
* ingress classification/normalization and safe path diagnostics;
* DataFrame/pandas interaction; and
* parser and persistence codecs/representations for every claimed external
  surface.

The extension must enter through Trusted Ingress and cannot make arbitrary
`list`, `tuple`, mapping, set, or object graphs valid by default. This seam does
not choose delimiter encoding, JSON encoding, Python list identity, spreadsheet
representation, or schema syntax. `List[Object]` and nested structured child
records remain `FTR-NESTED-LISTS` territory and do not use this scalar-composite
hook. FormulaSpec does not use this hook: the maintained helper path is
framework-authored output Intent under section 7's controlled execution-state
contract, not composite payload and not external formula ingress/roundtrip.

The four proof classes remain distinct:

* Scalar is the six-category ordinary cell grammar;
* `DeclaredComposite` is the currently empty ordinary bounded-composite seam;
* a framework-controlled cell/report role is authorized only by its exact
  transition and schema/location descriptor; and
* a structural carrier such as `GroupedMatrix` occupies a top-level frame role
  and owns separate structural authority.

The admitted FormulaSpec-in-`GroupedMatrix` case does not populate
`DeclaredComposite` and does not turn FormulaSpec into ordinary payload. It is
one exact nested role composition: FormulaSpec is authorized only at the
grouped dynamic locations projected from its proven helper value column, and
the outer carrier remains structural. A disjoint role product is likewise an
execution-state proof, not a new cell category.

`ArtifactManifestSourceFrames` is report/artifact-specific and therefore does
not populate `DeclaredComposite`. It permits exactly one one-level `list[str]`
field in one named output schema, not arbitrary list identity, `List[Scalar]`,
or external round trip. The R1 finding decision emits Scalar String diagnostics
and creates no report-cell role. `GroupedMatrix` remains structural rather than
a cell composite.

==== 20. Normative-style contract sentences for later promotion

If independently accepted, the following sentences are suitable for later
promotion into normative documentation:

* A framework-managed external entry MUST establish Ingress-Conformant Domain Payload
  before invoking an ordinary Domain operation.
* Framework-managed means that the fixed application macro flow owns the
  loader, initial Trusted Ingress, final Domain cleanup, persistence
  projection, and saver; it does not mean arbitrary executable code inside the
  flow is framework-validated while it executes.
* A preserving producer certificate applies only to one exact reviewed bound
  behavior/configuration and MUST return the execution state named by that
  contract; no callable, family, registry entry, wrapper, or built-in is
  preserving merely by identity.
* An exact reviewed controlled transition may establish only its named role at
  its declared location and only under its closed structural/cell, consumer,
  and exit contract; framework authorship or class identity is insufficient.
* Multiple disjoint roles may coexist only when each later exact transition
  proves through its footprint/locality contract that the existing role's
  location, payload, provenance, and relocation identity remain valid.
* Nested roles are forbidden except for the exact FormulaSpec-in-
  `GroupedMatrix` composition in section 10; that composition MUST preserve the
  ordinary remainder, reference identity, retained standalone source helper
  role(s), exact derive/relocate/consume endpoints, pending-cleanup timing, and
  terminal adapter contract.
* A copy/derivation transition MUST keep the source role authorized at its old
  location while establishing the new occurrence; relocation MUST end the old
  location; pending cleanup MUST remain published role state until the exact
  cleanup transition terminates it. A cleanup marker is never termination.
* The current FormulaSpec transition neither admits external FormulaSpec nor
  changes Scalar; the `GroupedMatrix` and artifact-manifest roles likewise do
  not widen external carriers or ordinary cell grammar.
* A framework-managed flow MUST re-establish Ingress-Conformant Domain Payload for an
  uncertified producer's returned candidate before any later ordinary Domain
  operation, final cleanup, or persistence; every prior controlled-role
  authorization ends at that return and the candidate rejects unless ordinary
  re-establishment succeeds.
* `run_pipeline` assumes its caller supplies the conformant execution state
  required by its first step and owns all preservation, controlled-role
  transitions, or re-establishment between supplied steps; `run_pipeline`
  performs no automatic Trusted Ingress.
* A documented supported direct Domain API states its local input contract and
  is not implicitly covered by framework-managed ingress.
* Arbitrary importable internals carry no framework entry, support, or stability
  guarantee.
* Successful Trusted Ingress establishes Ingress-Conformant Domain Payload, not payload
  authorship, pipeline-description authorization, Domain semantic validity, or
  sink safety.
* Trusted/less-trusted payload names authorship only: either may conform or fail
  conformance, and neither bypasses global grammar.
* Any future evidenced operator policy may narrow its globally supported kind
  but MUST NOT make an unsupported carrier valid; E1 exposes no speculative
  payload-policy matrix.
* A conformance interval is contractual/explanatory state, not a token,
  mutation detector, alias tracker, or isolation guarantee.
* A preserving certificate is an optional redundant-check optimization; it is
  not a controlled transition and cannot authorize a maintained non-ordinary
  execution role.
* A future maintained non-ordinary value MUST acquire an explicit role and
  transition contract before framework-managed flow may carry it.
* A less-trusted pipeline-selected capability MUST be rejected before
  capability activation; payload ingress neither performs nor substitutes for
  that authorization.

==== 22. Pydantic disposition

No concrete Phase-E payload use site justifies a Pydantic ADR.

Scalar semantics remain project-owned and already have a bounded classifier.
DataFrame/`ExactTable` traversal is carrier-specific, and the central metadata
promise is an evidence-gated bounded structural substrate plus centrally
governed delegates. Dataclass or custom validation is sufficient for the
accepted contracts. Adopting Pydantic here would merge payload, metadata, and
description errors without solving a demonstrated problem.

A later description-plane slice may discover a concrete control/config parsing
use site. If it does, it must separately compare existing dataclass/ad-hoc
parsing, Pydantic, and another schema mechanism while keeping payload scalar
semantics and Domain Findings outside that decision. This design neither adopts
nor forbids Pydantic. No ADR stop condition is triggered now.

==== 23. Implementation slice plan (not authorized by this design)

Each slice requires the design to have passed independent review first.

===== E1 -- ordinary scalar admission and safe boundary errors

*Scope:* compose the accepted classifier with the global six-category ordinary
cell grammar and define stable failure kinds/location records. No configurable
payload-policy matrix and no orchestration wiring.

*Preconditions:* accepted Phase-D classifier and this reviewed contract.

*Acceptance criteria:* every accepted carrier/category pair is admitted; every
unsupported carrier, including external FormulaSpec, rejects without value
`repr`/conversion/protocol dispatch; no carrier is normalized.

*Test strategy:* focused unit matrix over six categories and variants, explicit
unsupported temporal/Formula/collection/opaque values, and hostile
`repr`/NumPy-protocol probes.

*Exclusions:* future operator-narrowing knobs, DataFrame traversal, metadata,
public entry wiring, description authorization, resource limits.

===== E2 -- Frames/DataFrame/ExactTable structural dispatch and atomic traversal

*Scope:* staged top-level mapping; complete positional ordinary DataFrame cells;
`ExactTable` generic self-consistency and data traversal; deterministic paths;
and rejection of undeclared top-level/internal roles at external ingress. E2
establishes ordinary external structure; it does not own later controlled-role
descriptors.

*Preconditions:* E1. Structural dispatch/traversal that preserves current
physical-axis authority unchanged has no physical-axis compatibility-evidence
prerequisite. Independently accepted compatibility/migration evidence for
current framework-produced and supported consumer-reachable physical axes is a
mandatory additional prerequisite for any E2 proposal or implementation that
tightens, replaces, or narrows that authority.

*Acceptance criteria:* complete ordinary-cell traversal; unknown top-level
carriers including external `GroupedMatrix` reject; current physical-column
authority is enforced without a new
row-index grammar; failure leaves caller Frames/DataFrames unchanged through
ingress's own work; no DataFrame deep copy is required solely for validation;
reconstruction invariants stay local. Any narrower axis vocabulary requires a
separately reviewed compatibility/migration decision before that narrowing is
implemented. Until such evidence is independently accepted, E2 MUST preserve
the existing physical-axis contract unchanged.

*Test strategy:* multiple frames/dtypes, late-cell rejection, currently valid
duplicate/odd axis compatibility cases, valid/invalid exact geometry,
unauthorized FormulaSpec, and unchanged-caller-graph assertions.

*Exclusions:* silent axis tightening, family semantic checks, grouped
reconstruction changes, internal controlled-role implementation, generic
structured-carrier registry.

===== E3 -- metadata substrate and backend metadata introduction

*Scope:* the accepted `_meta` structural contract, owned acyclic substrate,
centrally governed delegates, bootstrap/override staged validation, and the

*Preconditions:* E1-E2; independently accepted focused census of external,

*Acceptance criteria:* supported metadata survives in an owned bounded

*Test strategy:* external workbook/JSON/YAML metadata, bootstrap/override
candidates, deep late failure, alias/cycle cases, delegate failures, duplicate
YAML metadata spellings, non-mapping YAML roots, and no partial publication.

*Exclusions:* Pydantic, all-family semantic schemas, resource ceilings, a YAML
metadata round-trip guarantee.

===== E4 -- exact producer-transition representation and bounded certificates

*Scope:* represent optional ordinary-preserving certificates, required
controlled-role introduction/awareness/transparency/consumption/exit, the exact
FormulaSpec-in-`GroupedMatrix` nested descriptor, bounded disjoint-role
locality/non-interference semantics, and uncertified transitions at exact
bound-behavior/configuration granularity.
Establish a deliberately small, possibly zero, evidence-backed preserving set
and the bounded minimum maintained-state set: FormulaSpec, transition-only

artifact-manifest `source_frames`.

*Preconditions:* E1-E3; accepted step/binding call-shape evidence sufficient to
represent immutable effective configuration and nested callbacks safely.

*Acceptance criteria:* the default is closed and uncertified; no callable,
registry entry, family, wrapper, factory, or built-in inherits certification;
unknown options, mutable post-bind configuration, and uncovered callbacks stay
uncertified; each certificate names its exact input/output state and has focused
evidence; each controlled-role descriptor names the complete section 2 contract
and cannot be established by identity; the R1 report projection emits only
Scalar cells; composition descriptors default closed; the exact locality proof
is stronger than observed non-mutation and does not require a universal role
registry; all other returned candidates lose role authority and are routed to
ordinary re-establishment. The preserving-certificate set may be zero, but the
required controlled-transition set may not omit a maintained role or admitted
composition. The exact descriptors distinguish copy/derive, relocate,
consume/terminate, and pending cleanup without creating a general effect
algebra or new pending-drop role kind.

*Test strategy:* exact bound-invocation construction and mutation tests, the
formula-helper Intent path, grouped producer/consumer/render and inverse paths,
formula-helper-to-grouped XLSX/ODS projection, nested reverse endpoint, two

MultiIndex reference/graph reports, artifact manifest production/writing, a

dotted/prebound forms.

*Exclusions:* auditing all registry forms, broad family hardening, generic
report exceptions, generic `List[Scalar]`, dedicated role re-establishment,
plugin sandboxing, description authorization. Later family work may add
certificates as an optimization without changing this contract.

*Scope:* initial ordinary conformance establishment, exact producer-state and

*Preconditions:* E1-E4.

*Acceptance criteria:* all six router backend families reach initial ingress;

independent grouped introductions
preserve both roles by exact locality proof;
reference/graph reports are ordinary after R1; the manifest role reaches only

==== 27. Design acceptance criteria

. all 15 accepted census questions are answered without being merged;
. the hybrid coverage model is truthful for every named entry surface;
. `run_pipeline`, supported lower-level APIs, and internals have distinct
  contracts;
. initial ingress, exact controlled-role transitions, optional preserving
  certificates, and uncertified-return re-establishment each have a short,
  distinct, testable postcondition;
. scalar grammar matches the accepted six categories and carriers exactly;
. validate-only scalar behavior preserves precision, timezone, and Missing
  representation;
. DataFrame, `ExactTable`, metadata, `GroupedMatrix`, FormulaSpec, finding
  reports, artifact manifests, and future carriers have distinct ownership/
  traversal rules;
. current physical-axis authority is preserved and any narrower vocabulary is
  gated by independently accepted compatibility evidence;
. metadata's exact node vocabulary is evidence-gated and every delegate is
  centrally governed and post-checked;
. custom producer output cannot reach later ordinary macro flow without
  preservation or re-establishment;
. producer certification is exact-bound-behavior scoped, defaults closed and
  uncertified, and does not require a repository-wide family audit;
. the maintained FormulaSpec helper path reaches capable output adaptation
  without admitting FormulaSpec at external ingress;
. the two maintained grouped producers reach only their exact inverse or
  workbook projection chain without admitting external/plugin carrier identity;
. the exact maintained formula-helper-to-grouped path retains every standalone
  source helper role, derives only the selected nested dynamic occurrence, and
  carries that role to XLSX/ODS or matching reverse expansion; retain mode
  publishes the nested/output product, drop mode terminates GroupedMatrix inside
  expansion and publishes the flat pending-cleanup/output FormulaSpec product,
  final cleanup terminates only the flat-source role, referenced lookup-sheet
  rename rejects, and no other nesting is admitted;
. repeated independent grouped introduction preserves both roles through a
  semantic locality/non-interference proof rather than incidental non-mutation;
. reference/graph MultiIndex locations become Scalar diagnostics and the
  manifest list remains confined to its exact artifact schema/consumer;
. a zero preserving-certificate set plus the bounded required controlled-role
  and composition set represents all twelve conservative-readiness flows
  without a broad family audit;
. payload and description trust remain independent and capability activation
  cannot precede authorization in any less-trusted-description claim;
. ingress/delegate work is non-mutating and staged before publication, while
  alias/concurrency isolation and rollback of prior effects are not promised;
. the ordinary bounded-composite seam remains empty and the manifest-specific

  milestone/E7 completion distinction, and do not hide a giant FTR;

. no runtime, ADR, Technical Model, Project Memory, guard, D-T3, or family-pilot

==== 30. Blocking-finding closure proofs

===== FormulaSpec lifecycle proof (Review 001 F1)

The maintained formula path composes without expanding external ingress:

[source,text]
----
router-loaded external payload
  -> initial ordinary ingress (FormulaSpec rejects)
  -> Ingress-Conformant Domain Payload
  -> exact reviewed add_lookup_helpers(helper_value_mode="formula") transition
  -> Conformant Framework Execution State
       with LookupFormulaSpec only in the declared helper-value role
  -> exact Intent-aware or Intent-transparent cleanup/projection
  -> FormulaSpec-capable XLSX/ODS output adaptation
  -> sink under its separate capability contract
----

FormulaSpec is neither Scalar nor `DeclaredComposite`. Only the exact reviewed
framework invocation may introduce the role; object shape or Python type alone
does not authorize it. If an uncertified producer intervenes, the role
authorization ends and ordinary re-establishment rejects all FormulaSpec. A
later formula helper may introduce a fresh role only through its own exact
reviewed transition. Thus the supported helper-to-renderer path remains
representable without external admission, composite abuse, re-establishment
bypass, or sink-authority collapse.

===== Bounded producer migration proof (Review 001 F2)

Phase E does not need a repository-wide producer audit, but the required
controlled transition/state set is not optional:

[source,text]
----
zero exact ordinary preserving certificates
  + exact FormulaSpec lifecycle
  + exact GroupedMatrix lifecycle
  + exact FormulaSpec-in-GroupedMatrix source-retaining derivation
  + exact reverse retain product and reverse drop pending-cleanup endpoint
  + exact disjoint-role locality/product transition for repeated grouping
  + exact R1 Scalar finding-report transition
  + exact artifact-manifest role lifecycle
  + closed uncertified default for every other bound behavior
  + ordinary re-establishment after each uncertified relevant return
  = truthful Phase-E Payload-Conformance Milestone
----

Callable/family review establishes only eligibility. A preserving certificate
requires the exact effective configuration, including covered callbacks, to
have a closed output contract; it remains an optional performance optimization.
A controlled transition instead supplies semantic authority for one maintained
non-ordinary state and its exact aware/transparent/consuming exits. Therefore
all registry forms and parameter combinations do not have to be certified
before E5, while the evidenced standalone, composition, report, and artifact
state decisions cannot be omitted.

===== 37.2 Implemented metadata grammar

The enforced common grammar (unchanged from the accepted census sections
17.4/18.9/19.10/20.7):

....
_meta root:                    exact dict (absent/None is also accepted)
recursive ordinary containers: exact dict, exact list
ordinary dict keys:            exact str, zero exceptions
ordinary leaves:                E1-admitted ordinary Scalar
aliases:                        shared acyclic aliases permitted
cycles:                         rejected
delegate output:                subject to this same recursive post-check

Exact-type dispatch (`type(x) is dict`/`type(x) is list`/`type(key) is str`)

non-`str` dict key of any origin (`int`/`bool`/`float`/`None`/legacy `tuple`/

===== 37.3 Safe diagnostics and metadata paths

`MetadataSubstrateAdmissionError` carries a stable `kind`
(`invalid_metadata_root`, `unsupported_metadata_key`, `invalid_metadata_node`,
`metadata_cycle_detected`), a structural `metadata_path` string (e.g.
`_meta.foo[2].bar`), and, for a leaf failure, the constant `carrier_type_name`
E1 already exposes. An unsupported dict key is never named: the diagnostic
uses the parent path plus the entry's zero-based ordinal
(`_meta[<entry 3>]`), never `repr`/`str` of the key. No rejection path calls
`repr`, `str`, equality, hashing (beyond the hashing Python itself required to
construct the caller's own dict, before the rejected candidate ever reaches
this module), or iteration on a rejected value or key; cycle detection uses
only `id()`. A dedicated adversarial suite (hostile leaf with raising
`__repr__`/`__str__`/`__eq__`/`__hash__`/`__iter__`; a hostile dict key whose
`__eq__`/`__repr__`/`__str__` raise but whose real `__hash__` is left intact
only so the fixture dict can be constructed; a `__class__`-spoofed
pseudo-dict) confirms zero hook invocations on every rejection path.

===== 37.4 Cycle and alias semantics

Cycle detection is path-active: a small `set[int]` of `id()`s currently on the
DFS ancestor stack, pushed on container entry and popped on exit (in a
`finally` block, so it unwinds correctly through a raised rejection). A
self-cycle and an indirect two-node ancestor cycle both raise
`metadata_cycle_detected` at the exact revisited path; a shared acyclic alias
(the same nested `dict` reachable from two sibling branches, or twice inside
one list) is admitted without rejection or copying, because its `id()` is
only ever active on one branch's stack at a time. No global "seen once" set is
used, so no benign alias is ever misclassified as a cycle.

implementation with a `BoundStep.binding is _TRUSTED_BINDING` sentinel

bypass, and section 44 below moves the freeze into `__post_init__` itself

Every classifier (`classify_formula_helper_step`,
`classify_grouped_producer_step`, `classify_expand_grouped_step`,
`classify_artifact_manifest_step`) returns the closed `Uncertified` value
unless, first, `resolve_trusted_call(step, expected_target=...)` returns a
non-``None`` `BoundFramesTargetCall` -- which requires `type(step.fn) is
BoundFramesTargetCall` (exact type, never `isinstance`) *and* `step.fn

reviewed shape, which the subsequent option/value checks still establish
independently -- see section 42 for the full correction record and its
adversarial probes (forged plain-closure `fn`, reused/copied
`BoundFramesTargetCall` pointed at an unrelated target, a subclassed
`BoundFramesTargetCall` overriding `__call__`, and a *genuine* copied
binding, which certifies but can only ever execute the reviewed behavior).

Mutation-after-bind handling: every `pipeline/steps.py` factory now exposes
`BoundStep.config` as a read-only `MappingProxyType` view built from the same
shallow-copied values its execution closure reads, so a top-level key
reassignment on `config` (the exact vector that previously diverged the
certificate from execution) now raises `TypeError` immediately instead of
silently succeeding. A nested mutable value (e.g. a `list` under
`helpers["fields"]`) remains mutable in place and is still the *same object*
the execution closure reads, so mutating it still changes both the exposed
config and actual execution identically -- a real, consistently-observed
change, not a divergence. `test_bound_configuration_mutation_safety.py`
proves both: (a) mutating that shared nested list in place after an earlier
classification really does change what the next invocation of the same
`BoundStep` executes, and a *fresh* classification honestly reflects it; (b)
the value already returned by the earlier classification is an immutable
snapshot (a `tuple[str, ...]` copy, not a reference) and is not
retroactively corrupted by the later mutation, and top-level reassignment is
no longer possible at all. No classifier caches a "certified" flag against a
`BoundStep`; every call re-reads `step.config` fresh.

===== 41.1 F1 -- exact-bound certification tied to executable behavior

[NOTE]
====
*Superseded.* The `_TRUSTED_BINDING` sentinel mechanism this subsection
describes was found forgeable by Independent Follow-up Review `ad700f0`
(section 16.1) and replaced by the `BoundFramesTargetCall`/
`resolve_trusted_call` mechanism in section 42. This subsection is left
unedited as the historical record of the first correction attempt.
====

*Reproduced first, exactly as the review specified.*

Counterexample A (forged `BoundStep`): confirmed that a hand-constructed
`BoundStep` with a byte-for-byte certifiable `add_lookup_helpers` config and
an unrelated `fn` (`lambda frames: {**frames, "PWNED": ...}`) was certified
by the pre-correction classifier.

Counterexample B (TOCTOU): confirmed that a real `build_steps_from_config`
step built with `helper_value_mode="values"` classified `Uncertified`,
executed values-mode; after `step.config["helper_value_mode"] = "formula"`
(a single top-level dict write), the classifier returned a
`FormulaHelperCertificate` while the step still executed values-mode on the
next call -- a genuine, silent config/execution divergence with no forgery.

*Root cause* (independently re-derived from `pipeline/types.py`,
`pipeline/steps.py`, `pipeline/build.py`, `pipeline/registry.py`):
`make_frames_target_step` built `cfg = {"target": ..., **dict(kwargs)}` (a
new, separate dict) but its closure captured the original `kwargs`, not
`cfg` -- two different objects from the moment of binding. `BoundStep` had
no field linking `config` to `fn` at all, so any `config`/`fn` pairing was
constructible.

*Chosen mechanism (combines both directions the task authorized):*

. *Authenticity* -- `pipeline/types.py` adds a private `_TrustedBinding`
  marker type and a shared `_TRUSTED_BINDING` instance, plus a new
  `BoundStep.binding: object = None` field. Every `BoundStep`-constructing
  factory in `pipeline/steps.py` (all eight: `make_plugin_step`,
  `make_builder_target_step`, `make_frames_target_step`,
  `make_identity_step`, `make_validate_step`, `make_apply_fks_step`,
  `make_drop_helpers_step`, `make_check_fk_helpers_step`) now passes
  `binding=_TRUSTED_BINDING`. `pipeline/build.py`'s name-rebinding fallback
  path now carries `tmp.binding` through instead of dropping it. A `BoundStep`
  built by calling the public constructor directly leaves `binding` at its
  default of `None` and cannot present a matching token merely by
  constructing a public-looking `config` dict -- `_TrustedBinding` is never
  instantiated outside `pipeline/steps.py`.
. *Immutable effective configuration* -- every factory now wraps its config
  dict in `types.MappingProxyType` before returning it as `BoundStep.config`.
  A top-level key reassignment (Counterexample B's exact vector) now raises
  `TypeError` immediately instead of silently diverging from execution. A
  nested mutable value (e.g. a `list` under `helpers["fields"]`) remains
  mutable in place and still changes both the exposed config and the
  executing closure identically (they were never independently copied), so
  this is a real, consistently-observed change, not a divergence.

`pipeline/execution_state/bound_configuration.py` adds
`is_trusted_binding(step) -> bool`, checked *first*, before any
configuration inspection, by all four classifiers
(`classify_formula_helper_step`, `classify_grouped_producer_step`,
`classify_expand_grouped_step`, `classify_artifact_manifest_step`). A new
closed `Uncertified` reason, `"unauthenticated_binding"`, names this
failure distinctly from `"unrecognized_target"`.

*Post-fix results:*

* Counterexample B: `step.config["helper_value_mode"] = "formula"` now

  values-mode both before and after the attempted mutation
  (`test_uncertified_default.py::test_post_bind_config_mutation_cannot_upgrade_execution_to_certified`).
* Independent layering proof: a genuinely trusted-bound step

* Nested-mutation regression: the already-shipped

* Full independent regression matrix (task section 25) verified: framework-

===== 43.2 Root correction -- frozen, deep-immutable `BoundFramesTargetCall`

`pipeline/types.py`'s `BoundFramesTargetCall` becomes
`@dataclass(frozen=True, slots=True)`: ordinary attribute reassignment on
either field now raises `dataclasses.FrozenInstanceError` -- real enforced
immutability at the instance level, not a documented convention. This
closes target/kwargs-reference reassignment (task matrix items E/F), but by
itself does not close nested-container mutation through a still-shared
list/dict, so the module also adds a paired freeze/thaw:

* `_freeze_effective_value(value)` -- recursively rebuilds `dict` into a
  `MappingProxyType` wrapping a *new* dict of recursively frozen values
  (never the caller's own dict) and `list`/`tuple` into a *new* `tuple` of
  recursively frozen values; any other value (an already-immutable scalar,
  or a shape outside this closed vocabulary -- a DataFrame, a plugin's own
  object, a callback) is returned by reference. `BoundFramesTargetCall.bind`
  -- the sole construction path `make_frames_target_step` uses -- freezes
  `kwargs` this way before storing it, so the stored snapshot shares no
  mutable object with anything the caller still holds, closing nested
  mutation through the *original* alias (task item H).
* `_thaw_effective_value(value)` -- the deterministic inverse, materializing
  fresh, invocation-local `dict`/`list` containers from the frozen snapshot.
  `__call__` calls `target(frames, **thaw(self.kwargs))`: every invocation
  gets ordinary mutable containers (preserving call-surface expectations
  such as `isinstance(helpers, dict)` inside `enrich_lookup`'s own
  `policy.py`), the stored snapshot itself is never mutated, and a later
  invocation of the same call thaws fresh containers from the same
  unchanged snapshot again.

Bounded to E4-relevant semantics per task section 6: only `dict`/`list`/
`tuple` are recursively frozen (the shapes the five reviewed targets'
closed configuration vocabulary actually uses -- `helpers={"fields":
[...]}`, `row_keys`, `label_columns`, etc.); any other value shape passes
through unchanged, exactly as a generic binder used by ~30 non-E4-reviewed
registered steps must (task section 7) -- and such a shape is never
accepted by an E4 classifier's `snapshot_scalar`/`snapshot_string_sequence`
regardless, so nothing E4-relevant needed protecting for it. No universal
Python object freezer, no generic deep-copy of arbitrary graphs, no
serialization format.

*Why classifier and execution cannot diverge* (task section 11):
`resolve_trusted_call` returns `step.fn` itself; every classifier reads
configuration from `call.kwargs` directly. `__call__` reads that exact same
`self.kwargs` object (via `thaw`, not a copy taken at a different time).
There is one immutable snapshot, read by both; no second, independently
maintained "classifier view" exists that could itself drift.

`make_frames_target_step` now calls `BoundFramesTargetCall.bind(fn, kwargs)`
and builds `BoundStep.config` from `call.kwargs` (the same frozen structure,
plus the descriptive `"target"` label) rather than a separately constructed
dict, so `step.config` and `step.fn.kwargs` remain the same data by
construction, not merely by convention.

One classifier-side fix was required: `formula_helper._classify_formula_fields`
checked `type(helpers) is not dict`, but a dict-shaped `helpers` value now
arrives frozen as `MappingProxyType`, not `dict`; the check now accepts
either exact type.

===== 44.2 Root correction -- the invariant moves into `__post_init__`

*Chosen invariant* (task section 2): every normally constructed
`BoundFramesTargetCall` that `resolve_trusted_call` can accept owns an
immutable effective execution snapshot from the instant construction
completes -- not merely the subset built through one particular classmethod.
There is no longer a "safe constructor" (`bind()`) and an "unsafe
constructor" (the public dataclass constructor): there is exactly one.

* `BoundFramesTargetCall.bind()` is *removed*. `pipeline/steps.py`'s
  `make_frames_target_step` now calls `BoundFramesTargetCall(fn, kwargs)`
  directly -- the same constructor available to any other caller.
* `BoundFramesTargetCall.__post_init__` unconditionally replaces whatever
  `kwargs` value the constructor received with
  `_freeze_effective_kwargs(self.kwargs)`'s canonical result, via
  `object.__setattr__` (the same mechanism the frozen dataclass's own
  generated `__init__` uses internally). No constructor argument, keyword
  form, or code path skips this -- confirmed by
  `test_construction_freezes_unconditionally_regardless_of_keyword_style`
  (positional and keyword construction both produce a frozen
  `MappingProxyType`).
* `_freeze_effective_kwargs(kwargs)` is the one root-level canonicalization
  boundary (task section 5): it accepts only an exact `dict` or exact
  `MappingProxyType` -- anything else raises `TypeError` immediately (task
  section 7's arbitrary-`Mapping` rule; verified: `repository-wide grep`
  confirms every current `make_frames_target_step` caller supplies plain
  `**kwargs`, so no maintained path is narrowed) -- then delegates to
  `_freeze_effective_value`, which now treats `dict` *and* `MappingProxyType`
  identically at every nesting depth (task section 6): both are read into a
  **new**, disconnected dict before being re-wrapped in a **new**
  `MappingProxyType`, so a `MappingProxyType` that happens to still be
  backed by a live caller-owned dict is never trusted as "already frozen" --
  its *current* contents are copied out from under that live backing at the
  moment of construction. `list`/`tuple` continue to freeze into a new
  `tuple` of recursively frozen values; every other leaf is returned by
  reference (unchanged from the prior correction; see 44.6 for why this
  remains sufficient).

===== 45.4 Execution-state algorithm (classify -> execute -> apply)

New package-external module `application/managed_pipeline.py` (deliberately
*not* added under `pipeline/execution_state/` itself -- that package must
stay unaware of `application`/`io_backends`/`cli`, per its own architecture
guard, section 45.7 below). `ManagedExecutionState` is a frozen dataclass
holding `roles: tuple[ControlledRole, ...]` (E4's own role types, reused
unchanged) plus `last_formula_certificate` -- the one additional fact needed
to prove the FormulaSpec-in-`GroupedMatrix` nested composition against a
*later* `contract_grouped_xref` step, not a general certificate history or
registry (excluded by task section 3/31).

`execute_managed_step(frames, state, step)` tries each of E4's four
classifiers in turn (`classify_formula_helper_step`,
`classify_grouped_producer_step`, `classify_expand_grouped_step`,
`classify_artifact_manifest_step`); a `BoundStep` can structurally execute at
most one exact reviewed target (`bound_configuration.resolve_trusted_call`'s
object-identity check is exclusive), so this is an exhaustive, unambiguous
dispatch, not a "closest match" heuristic. For a certified transition, the
handler:

* checks the transition's own declared `TransitionFootprint` against every
  currently-live role's frame via `vocabulary.proves_disjoint` (generalizing
  E4's `disjoint_grouped_introduction`, which is one specific instance of
  this same check, to every role kind and every certified transition kind);
* for `contract_grouped_xref` specifically, first tries
  `compose_formula_to_grouped` against the retained
  `last_formula_certificate`; on success it replaces the consumed standalone
  `LookupFormulaSpecRole`s with the composition's retained-source and nested
  roles, otherwise it falls through to the standalone-introduction path;
* for `expand_grouped_xref`, resolves whichever live role (standalone
  `GroupedMatrixRole` or nested `GroupedMatrixFormulaRole`) occupies the
  certificate's declared `matrix` frame and applies
  `resolve_grouped_matrix_expand_transition`/`resolve_formula_expand_transition`
  -- for the nested/drop case, `dynamic_column_labels` is read from the
  *live* `GroupedMatrix` object in `frames` *before* the step executes
  (matching task section 8's classify-then-execute-then-apply ordering:
  classification-relevant live-state reads happen before execution, the
  transition is applied to the state *actually* produced by that one
  execution);
* executes the step exactly once, through `run_pipeline(frames, (step,))` --
  a single-step call, so `run_pipeline`'s own debug/meta-diff tracing keeps
  working and its code/signature are untouched;
* for any classifier returning `Uncertified` (all four, for a step that
  matches none of the exact targets, or one exact target with unreviewed
  configuration), `_fallback_uncertified` executes the step once and then
  calls `admit_ordinary_frames` on the result, resetting
  `ManagedExecutionState` to empty regardless of outcome-in-progress state.
  Because none of the maintained controlled-role carriers (`GroupedMatrix`,
  `LookupFormulaSpec`, the manifest `list[str]` cell) are recognized ordinary
  carriers/Scalar values, this single call is sufficient to enforce FTR
  section 12's "anything else" rule -- no separate role-aware uncertified
  path was needed.

`run_managed_steps` folds `execute_managed_step` over the configured step
list in declared order.

evidence points one way.
