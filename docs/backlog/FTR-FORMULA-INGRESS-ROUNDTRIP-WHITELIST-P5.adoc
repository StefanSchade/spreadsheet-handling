===== FTR-FORMULA-INGRESS-ROUNDTRIP-WHITELIST-P5 -- Durable constraints for any future formula-preservation/roundtrip policy

*Status:* Open (deferred -- design/review slice, not implementation-ready)

*Priority:* P5

*Area:* Formula ingress/roundtrip policy / carrier-vs-Intent boundary /
adapter formula vocabulary

*Type:* Constraint anchor (durable knowledge, not a runtime proposal)

*Relationship:* Split out of `FTR-MINIMAL-INTERNAL-VALUE-MODEL-P4A`'s
section 9 ("Formula hard non-goals") so that Phase D's own scope stays
concise while the durable formula-policy constraints remain discoverable on
their own. Not part of the Minimal Internal Value Model MVP and not a
dependency of it: Phase D may proceed, and close, without this FTR ever
being implemented.

[NOTE]
====
This FTR is a *durable knowledge anchor*, not implementation authorization.

It does not implement formula preservation, formula parsing, formula
translation, or any runtime, CLI, YAML, or registry surface. It does not
choose a public API, a policy schema, or CLI flag names. It records
constraints a *future* design/review slice must satisfy before any of that
work begins. That future slice still needs its own evidence, its own
acceptance criteria, and its own independent review -- nothing here
pre-authorizes it.
====

==== Purpose

Record, in one place, the durable design constraints that already exist for
formula handling in this project -- gathered from
`BUG-UNRESOLVED-HELPER-VALUE-NAN-SEMANTICS-P4A`'s "Deferred formula /
typing constraint" section, `BUG-ODS-XLOOKUP-FORMULA-INTEROP-P4A`'s
concrete interoperability evidence, `ADR-TRUSTED-RUNTIME-AND-POLICY-AUTHORITY`,
and current FK-helper behavior -- plus the additional constraints that
evidence implies but the closed BUG did not yet state explicitly (a strict
allowlist, no general Excel/OpenFormula compatibility promise, and a
feature-owned roundtrip contract requirement). This FTR does not re-derive
that evidence; it consolidates the future-facing rules and cites the
evidence that produced them.

==== Why this exists as its own FTR, not a section of the value-model FTR

A generic "preserve formulas because we already have formula strings" path
is an architecturally unbounded interoperability surface, not a small
feature. It deserves its own durable anchor precisely so a future formula
feature author cannot miss it inside a document scoped to primitive scalar
typing. See `FTR-MINIMAL-INTERNAL-VALUE-MODEL-P4A` section 9 for the
pointer back to this FTR from the Phase-D side.

==== 1. No generic formula-language promise

The framework must *not* set a goal of:

* accepting arbitrary workbook formulas;
* parsing arbitrary Excel formulas;
* parsing arbitrary OpenFormula formulas;
* translating arbitrary Excel formula syntax to ODS;
* translating arbitrary ODS formula syntax to XLSX;
* preserving every user-authored formula it encounters; or
* becoming a cross-spreadsheet formula compatibility layer.

The two adapters expose different carrier/specification vocabularies and
capabilities. General formula compatibility would open an effectively
unbounded interoperability surface -- the framework would be signing up to
track two evolving, only partially overlapping formula languages
indefinitely.

*Evidence, not the whole rationale:* `BUG-ODS-XLOOKUP-FORMULA-INTEROP-P4A`
(Fixed) shows that even one already-supported semantic function
(`LookupFormulaSpec` rendered as `XLOOKUP`) required an adapter-specific
vocabulary correction -- ODS/LibreOffice only recognized the
extension-qualified `COM.MICROSOFT.XLOOKUP`, not the unqualified name XLSX
uses. One function, one already-modeled `FormulaSpec`, and cross-backend
translation still needed adapter-specific handling. A generic "preserve any
observed formula text" policy would face this class of problem for every
formula family, unbounded, with no `FormulaSpec` modeling functions to
anchor the translation.

==== 2. Strict allowlist / feature-owned support

If formula preservation/roundtrip is ever supported, it must be based on a
small, explicit allowlist of formula families/contracts known to the
framework -- never "any formula text the adapter happens to observe."

Support must be *feature-owned*. A transformation or projection that
introduces a formula and expects formula semantics to matter on reimport
must define, at introduction time:

* what authored formula intent exists;
* which backends support it;
* how each backend renders it;
* what readback behavior is required;
* whether preservation is required at all;
* what happens if the formula is changed by a user;
* what happens when a carrier/backend cannot support it; and
* whether the formula may instead be safely materialized to its value.

"We already have formula strings, so preserve them generically" is
explicitly rejected as an architectural shortcut. Every future
formula-preserving feature earns its own allowlist entry and its own
answers to the questions above; none may piggyback on another feature's
formula support without its own contract.

==== 3. Observed formula != authored FormulaSpec

Preserve the distinction already established and confirmed unchanged
(`core/formulas.py`, re-verified in `FTR-MINIMAL-INTERNAL-VALUE-MODEL-P4A`
section 1 and section 9):

[source,text]
----
Authored FormulaSpec:
    framework-owned, backend-neutral output intent
    (ListLiteralFormulaSpec / LookupFormulaSpec)

Observed workbook formula:
    carrier state supplied by an input document,
    produced by whoever last edited that workbook
----

A parser must not automatically convert arbitrary observed formulas into
framework-authored `FormulaSpec` intent merely because their text resembles
a known function. Future support may *recognize* an allowlisted formula
shape only under an explicit contract (constraint 2); recognition is not
generic trust, and a resemblance match is not itself the contract.

==== 4. Formula handling is pipeline/projection dependent

Formula preservation must not become immutable column typing. The same
logical/canonical column can legitimately be used in different pipelines:

[source,text]
----
A. editable roundtrip / workbook maintenance
   -> formula preservation may be useful

B. extraction for a downstream consumer
   -> evaluated/materialized values are normally desirable
----

The policy therefore belongs to ingress/read/projection configuration,
never permanently to the column's scalar type. This is consistent with
`FTR-MINIMAL-INTERNAL-VALUE-MODEL-P4A`'s recommended categories
(String/Boolean/Number/Missing/Error), which describe *payload values*, not
*projection policy*, and do not conflict with this constraint.

==== 5. Timing: pre-load / adapter ingress

The formula-vs-materialized-value decision must be available *before or
during* workbook loading. A normal transformation step after parsing is too
late if the parser has already discarded either the formula text or the
cached/materialized value.

Confirmed unchanged (re-verified in `FTR-MINIMAL-INTERNAL-VALUE-MODEL-P4A`
section 9): `bootstrap_meta` still runs inside `run_pipeline`, strictly
after `router.get_loader(kind)` has already parsed the file. A future
formula-preservation read policy is an adapter/application-boundary
concern, not a pipeline-step concern. Future integration with trusted
ingress / runtime policy (roadmap Phase E) must account for this timing;
this FTR does not design where exactly such a policy threads through the
loader boundary.

==== 6. Default direction

Preserve the current design direction, clearly labeled as deferred and not
elevated beyond its recorded status: ordinary spreadsheet formulas should
normally enter the domain as their evaluated/materialized values; formula
preservation should require explicit support and explicit pre-load policy.

This direction is recorded in `BUG-UNRESOLVED-HELPER-VALUE-NAN-SEMANTICS-P4A`
("Default formula-reading direction (deferred design direction, not decided
here)") and is *not* upgraded to an ADR-level decision by this FTR. It
remains a recorded direction pending its own design/review slice, and is
consistent with the current, informal, hardcoded XLSX loader behavior
(`data_only=True`), which already reads formula cells as their last-cached
value rather than formula text -- without that being a deliberate,
documented, cross-backend policy today.

==== 7. FK helper precedent

Current FK helper behavior is the concrete precedent for "emitting a
formula does not imply preserving it on reimport":

* `add_fk_helpers` can author `LookupFormulaSpec`-based `XLOOKUP` formulas
  (`helper_value_mode: formula`) for workbook usability -- a live,
  recalculating helper cell.
* On reimport, those helper columns are derived/presentation helpers, never
  resolution authority (`docs/ai_info/concepts_fk_helpers.adoc`,
  `BUG-UNRESOLVED-HELPER-VALUE-NAN-SEMANTICS-P4A`'s adopted contract): they
  are cleaned and reconstructed from the authoritative source FK
  relationship plus a live target-frame lookup
  (`validate_fk_helpers`/`check_unresolvable_fks`), not from whatever the
  helper cell currently displays.
* The roundtrip therefore remains correct even though every maintained
  parser reads the helper cell as its evaluated value, never as formula
  text -- no maintained code path preserves the `XLOOKUP` formula text on
  read at all, and none needs to.

This demonstrates the general rule:

[source,text]
----
transformation emits a formula
    !=
transformation requires formula preservation on reimport
----

A future transformation that *does* require formula-preserving roundtrip
semantics must prove why its formula carries information/intent that
cannot safely disappear into its evaluated value -- FK helpers prove the
opposite case, not the general case, and must not be cited as precedent for
skipping that proof.

==== 8. Failure / unsupported behavior

Not fully designed here, but bounded for future design: unsupported
formulas must never trigger "best effort" semantic translation between
XLSX and ODS. A future policy must explicitly choose among bounded
behaviors such as:

* consume the materialized value;
* reject with a clear diagnostic; or
* preserve only under a known, supported, carrier-specific contract
  (constraint 2's allowlist).

This FTR does not decide the exact public API, error type, or diagnostic
wording for the unsupported case.

==== 9. Security/trust relationship

Tied carefully to the accepted `ADR-TRUSTED-RUNTIME-AND-POLICY-AUTHORITY`,
without reopening it. Do *not* conflate:

* formula-looking payload text (a string that merely resembles formula
  syntax);
* executable pipeline extension/callable capability (arbitrary Python
  execution, governed by Decision 1 of that ADR); and
* output-sink formula capability (what a spreadsheet renderer does with an
  accepted value, governed by Decision 5 of that ADR).

The accepted ADR already says sink capability is separate from payload
normalization: "A string is not globally invalid merely because its text
resembles spreadsheet formula syntax ... for some sink." Formula
preservation/roundtrip, as scoped by this FTR, is therefore a
carrier/projection *capability* decision (constraints 1-8 above), not a
declaration that a string beginning with `=` is globally unsafe or a
distinct scalar type. `FTR-MINIMAL-INTERNAL-VALUE-MODEL-P4A` already
reaches the same conclusion independently (section 9: "a formula is not a
scalar payload value").

At the same time, a future trusted-ingress implementation (roadmap Phase E)
must know whether a formula carrier is allowed/recognized *before* it
normalizes the workbook into its internal value representation --
constraint 5's timing requirement applies equally to that future ingress
work. This FTR does not reopen `ADR-TRUSTED-RUNTIME-AND-POLICY-AUTHORITY`
and does not decide any of that ADR's own listed non-goals (security-policy
representation, parser/resource limits, or "formula or literal-text output
policy for XLSX or any other sink" -- Decision 5's own non-goal list).

Related, narrower, and *not* superseded by this FTR: the accepted Technical
Concept `trusted_ingress_and_runtime_security.adoc`'s FTR-candidate-map
"candidate 8" (concrete sink-local protections, illustrated by the XLSX
leading-`=` write-side gap) is a different, write-side, sink-capability
question -- whether an ordinary string should ever become a live formula
cell on write. This FTR is about the read/roundtrip side: whether an
already-existing workbook formula may be preserved rather than
materialized. The two are related through Decision 5 but are independent
pieces of work with independent authorization.

==== Relationship to `BUG-UNRESOLVED-HELPER-VALUE-NAN-SEMANTICS-P4A`

That BUG is closed (Done) and is not reopened or rewritten by this FTR. Its
"Deferred formula / typing constraint for future Internal Value Model work"
section already recorded, and remains authoritative evidence for:

* formula is outside the typing MVP;
* observed formula is not automatically authored Formula Intent;
* the deferred default-read-direction design note;
* formula preservation as pipeline/projection policy, not immutable column
  type; and
* the pre-load timing constraint.

This FTR consolidates those into the constraints above (sections 3-6) and
adds what that BUG's own scope did not need to decide, because it was
scoped to FK-helper carrier/semantic closure, not general formula policy:

* the explicit *strict allowlist* requirement (section 2);
* the explicit *no arbitrary Excel/OpenFormula compatibility* rejection
  (section 1), evidenced concretely by the since-closed
  `BUG-ODS-XLOOKUP-FORMULA-INTEROP-P4A`, which did not exist yet when the
  BUG above recorded its formula constraint;
* the explicit *feature-owned roundtrip contract* obligation -- a
  transformation introducing formula-dependent roundtrip semantics must
  design its ingress/readback contract at the same time it introduces the
  formula, not retrofit one later (section 2);
* the *FK helpers as evidence that emitting a formula does not imply
  preserving it on reimport* precedent, stated as a general rule future
  authors must satisfy, not merely as a fact about FK helpers (section 7);
  and
* the bounded *failure/unsupported-behavior* menu (section 8).

No contradiction was found between the BUG's recorded constraint and the
constraints added here; this FTR is additive.

==== Non-goals

* Any formula parser, formula translator, or formula evaluator
  implementation.
* Any public API, CLI flag, YAML configuration key, or policy schema for
  formula preservation.
* A general Excel-to-OpenFormula or OpenFormula-to-Excel translation layer.
* Any change to `core/formulas.py`'s `FormulaSpec` union or its
  constructors.
* Any change to `add_fk_helpers`, `helper_value_mode`, or FK helper
  behavior -- section 7 cites current behavior as evidence, it does not
  propose changing it.
* Reopening `ADR-TRUSTED-RUNTIME-AND-POLICY-AUTHORITY`,
  `ADR-DOMAIN-BOUNDARY-ROBUSTNESS`, or
  `ADR-VISIBLE-CELL-TYPING-STRING-SUBSTRATE`.
* Deciding the allowlist's first member, if one is ever added -- that is a
  future feature's own evidence-gated proposal.
* Trusted Ingress (roadmap Phase E) implementation.
* Any change to `FTR-MINIMAL-INTERNAL-VALUE-MODEL-P4A` beyond the pointer
  it already carries to this FTR.
