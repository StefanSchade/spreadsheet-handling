== 10. Findings

=== Blocking B-1 -- H2 conflates certification with current controlled-role state

*Observation:* The controlled-role checklist asks how a classifier proves a
bound call "actually carries this role."

*Evidence:* Architectural Layers, "Three distinct authority questions,"
separates certificate/binding identity, controlled-transition authority, and
current role state. Current managed execution publishes a role only after the
certified invocation executes and the exact transition is applied.

*Risk:* A maintainer can follow the guide and treat a certificate/classifier
result as present role authority, recreating the appearance/label-as-authority
failure the accepted architecture rejects.

*Smallest correction:* Replace the bullet with an explicit two-stage
relationship: certification proves the exact invocation is eligible for its
named transition; successful execution plus exact transition application
publishes the separate current-state descriptor. Keep both linked to the
Technical Model.

=== Blocking B-2 -- H5 names three macro wiring seams where architecture has one

*Observation:* The guide tells maintainers to wire a rule into the relevant
macro seam and lists `orchestrate`, `run_app`, and `run_schema_maintenance`.

*Evidence:* Current code and `test_execution_state_not_wired.py` establish
`application.orchestrator.orchestrate` plus its small managed component as the
one framework-owned macro seam. `run_app` and schema maintenance delegate to
it and must not become parallel policy/execution-state owners.

*Risk:* The permanent extension guide invites enforcement to be scattered
across entry adapters, undermining the single-seam architecture and making
coverage diverge by entry point.

*Smallest correction:* Name only `orchestrate`'s managed macro seam as the
wiring location; require reachability tests through `run_app`, schema
maintenance, and their CLIs as delegation coverage, not additional wiring.

=== Important I-1 -- The interface matrix merges surfaces with different description-authority contracts

*Observation:* One row combines `run_app` and `sheets-run` under "Yes,
opt-in"; another combines the builders and six direct factories under the
same answer.

*Evidence:* `run_app` and both builders accept
`description_authorization`. `sheets-run` and the six exported direct
factories do not. The file's own summary correctly says the CLI does not
advertise the feature.

*Risk:* The compact public navigation surface gives the wrong answer to one
of its three defining dimensions for supported APIs.

*Smallest correction:* Split the mixed rows or give explicit per-member
answers. In the same bounded edit, replace "byte-for-byte" with the semantic
no-wiring contract and scope the maintained-caller claim to the E6 consumer
census/no-helper decision.

=== Important I-2 -- Project Evolution omits two required closure gates

*Observation:* The Phase-E history moves from independent documentation
review to archival and then to the Domain Maintainer Contract/Phase F.

*Evidence:* Governing lifecycle authority requires the isolated fresh-agent
comprehension test after this review, then archival decision, then
documentation-closure lifecycle curation, and only then the Domain Maintainer
Contract / Phase F / D-T3.

*Risk:* A durable lifecycle narrative authorizes readers to skip both the
comprehension gate and final lifecycle curation.

*Smallest correction:* State the complete sequence exactly once in the Phase-E
section.

=== Important I-3 -- Developer Guide ownership wording can create competing or misplaced authority

*Observation:* The guide duplicates the exact current three-role census and
says all new authority always lands in the Technical Model.

*Evidence:* The accepted owner split assigns current role/mechanism authority
to the Technical Model, but Domain-language and future composite *meaning* to
the Semantic Model. The guide itself says it should not reproduce the current
role set.

*Risk:* The copied role count/names will drift at the next extension, while the
blanket ownership rule misroutes semantic contracts and weakens the established
Semantic/Technical split.

*Smallest correction:* Link to the Technical Model's current role set without
copying its count/names. Scope the final rule to Trusted Ingress mechanism/
authority and explicitly route Domain-language meaning to the Semantic Model.

=== Minor M-1 -- Reference Readability misclassifies its example ID categories

`E1`-`E7`, `D-T1`, `A1`/`C10`, and review findings are all valid examples of
non-semantic IDs, but they are not respectively "era / slice / phase" in the
way stated. Name them as Phase-E work-package slices, a Date/Time slice, design
inventory invariant IDs, and review finding IDs. The rule itself remains
understandable.
