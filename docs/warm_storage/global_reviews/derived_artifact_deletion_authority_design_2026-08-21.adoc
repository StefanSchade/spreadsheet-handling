== A. Executive design decision

[.lead]
Close the confirmed FK/Lookup deletion-authority defect with the smallest
rule the accepted research evidences: *a cleanup executor may delete a
column identified through an FK-attributed name only when truthful, same-run
transient FK materialization provenance names that column.* Durable FK
relation policy (`_meta.helper_policies.fk.relations`) is retained as a
*materialization* input (it still tells `add_fk_helpers` what to build) but is
permanently removed as a *deletion-candidate* input from both
`fk_helpers/drop.py` and `derived_column_policy.py`. No value/formula
equivalence check, no claimant/reliance vocabulary, and no new durable state
are introduced anywhere in this design.

Two runtime corrections, applied together, close all three reproduced
variants (same-run collision, durable-policy-only with FK never run, and the
second `derived_column_policy.py` executor):

. *Truthful FK provenance* (site 1) — `core/fk.py`'s materialization
  primitive reports, per resolved FK definition, whether it actually wrote
  the helper column this call or found the label already occupied.
  `fk_helpers/enrich.py` writes `_meta.derived.sheets.*.helper_columns`
  entries only for definitions it can truthfully claim (see <<E>>). This
  alone closes the same-run collision variant, because FK no longer writes a
  false claim for a column it did not write.
. *Deletion-authority rule* (sites 2 and 3) — `fk_helpers/drop.py` and
  `derived_column_policy.py` both stop treating durable FK relation policy as
  sufficient evidence to delete a physically present column. Each keeps
  reading FK's own transient provenance (already correct data, already
  correctly scoped per caller); each simply stops falling back to durable
  policy when transient provenance is silent. This alone closes both
  durable-policy-only variants, because "declared but never proven
  materialized this run" no longer authorizes deletion.

Both corrections are independently necessary and jointly sufficient against
the accepted evidence in <<repro-recap>>. Neither requires value/formula
equivalence machinery, a claimant/reliance primitive, or new durable state.

== B. Confirmed problem and bounded scope

Recap of the accepted, independently reproduced defect (full detail in the
cited research and review artifacts; not re-litigated here):

[cols="1,4,3", options="header"]
|===
| Repro | Setup | Confirmed outcome (pre-design)

| 1 — same-run collision
| `configure_fk_helpers` requests `_B_name`; `enrich_lookup` materializes
  `_B_name` with different values into the same frame; `add_fk_helpers` runs
  and silently skips (label occupied) but writes FK provenance anyway;
  `remove_fk_helpers` runs.
| `_B_name` physically deleted using FK's *false* transient provenance. Real,
  irretrievable data loss; H5 only reconciles metadata after the fact.

| 2 — durable-policy-only, `add_fk_helpers` never ran
| `configure_fk_helpers` writes the durable v2 relation; `enrich_lookup` is
  the *only* producer of `_B_name` this session; no FK
  `helper_columns` transient record exists at all; `remove_fk_helpers` runs.
| `_B_name` physically deleted purely from
  `_meta.helper_policies.fk.relations`'s declared name, with zero FK
  transient provenance of any kind.

| 3 — second executor, same durable-policy-only setup
| Identical to repro 2, cleaned up via
  `apply_derived_column_policy(source="A", policy="drop")` instead of
  `remove_fk_helpers`.
| `_B_name` physically deleted by `derived_column_policy.py`'s own,
  independent `_safe_policy_helper_names` durable-relation fallback.
|===

Bounded scope of this design (identical to the accepted research's Section N
and the review's conclusion I, restated here as the binding scope for
implementation):

* mandatory: `domain/transformations/fk_helpers/enrich.py` and the FK
  materialization primitive in `core/fk.py`;
* mandatory: `domain/transformations/fk_helpers/drop.py`;
* mandatory: the FK-consuming branch of
  `domain/transformations/derived_column_policy.py`;
* paired collaborator, not redesigned: Lookup Enrichment
  (`domain/transformations/enrich_lookup/`), whose existing transient
  provenance and H5 predicate (`reconcile_enrich_lookup_provenance`) are
  read-only inputs to this design, unchanged.
* constraints only, not touched: Discriminator split/merge, XRef/final
  cleanup, Cell Codec, Workbook View, persistence boundary mechanics,
  Trusted Ingress, schema maintenance.

== C. Canonical semantic distinctions

This design uses exactly the vocabulary the accepted research converged on
(Section F of the assessment; Review 001 Area 3 and FIND-007). No broader
vocabulary is introduced.

[cols="2,4", options="header"]
|===
| Term | Meaning in this design

| Physical producer
| The operation that actually wrote the currently present column/cell state.

| Requested / declared policy
| A family's transient or durable statement that it *wants* a helper at a
  named identity. Never sufficient deletion authority by itself.

| Truthful materialization provenance
| A same-run transient record written only for a definition the writing
  primitive can affirmatively claim as its own current output (freshly
  written this call, or self-verified as its own unchanged prior output —
  <<E>>). This is the *only* thing that can authorize FK-attributed deletion
  in this design.

| Deletion authority / precondition
| The verified fact ("truthful materialization provenance names this
  column") that must hold before a cleanup executor removes a column
  identified through a requested/declared name.

| Cleanup executor
| The operation that mutates the physical frame to remove a column:
  `fk_helpers/drop.py` and `derived_column_policy.py`, the two mandatory
  sites.
|===

*Explicitly not introduced:* claimant, current reliance, claim
registration/release, reference counting, a central claim ledger, stable
artifact identity, or an adopt/share protocol. <<Q>> restates why each is
unnecessary against the evidence in <<repro-recap>>.

[[D]]
== D. FK collision semantics

*Design Question 1 answer.* The smallest sufficient policy is *A: reject on
collision, no provenance claim* — `core/fk.py`'s materialization primitive
must never overwrite an existing column at the requested helper label
(Candidate D — FK overwrites — was independently rejected by Review 001 as
strictly worse: it trades a wrongful-deletion defect for silent, unwarned
data corruption of whichever producer's column FK overwrote). On collision,
the primitive continues to skip the write, exactly as today; what changes is
that the skip must be *reported*, not silently absorbed, so provenance can be
truthful (<<E>>).

Policy A alone is *sufficient* to close all three reproduced variants once
paired with the deletion-authority rule in <<F>> — see <<repro-closure>>. Per
the design brief's own instruction ("If A or B is sufficient, do not design
generic adoption"), *Candidate C (value/semantic-equivalence adoption of a
foreign producer's column) is rejected outright and not designed here* —
<<L>> gives the full disposition.

*Recommended, separable refinement (narrow B, optional):* within
`fk_helpers/enrich.py` only (Domain layer, not Core), a definition that
collided this call may be recognized as FK's own *idempotent prior output* —
never a foreign producer's — solely by comparing the collided definition's
shape (`fk_column`, `target`, `target_key`, `value_field` for the same
`column` label) against FK's *own already-present* transient provenance entry
for that sheet (read via the existing `derived_helper_columns_by_sheet`
reader in `fk_helpers/policy.py`). If every field matches, the label is
FK's own unchanged prior materialization from earlier in the same
frames-mutation chain (e.g. a second `add_fk_helpers` invocation in one
pipeline run); its provenance entry is refreshed (kept), not dropped. If any
field differs, or no matching entry exists, the collision is treated exactly
as the mandatory Policy A case: no claim, no diagnostic escalation beyond
<<J>>.

This refinement:

* compares only FK's own previously-written metadata shape, never cell
  *values* and never a foreign family's provenance — so it cannot become
  Candidate C by construction;
* requires no new metadata, no cross-family read, and no new primitive;
* closes a narrow, currently-unevidenced-as-broken idempotence edge case
  (re-running `add_fk_helpers` twice in one session) that a pure Policy-A
  implementation would otherwise quietly regress (today's silent skip
  already tolerates this; a naive truthful-only rewrite that always drops
  unmatched provenance on re-run would not);
* is *not required* to close the confirmed defect (<<repro-closure>> closes
  without it) — implementers may defer it to a follow-up slice without
  reopening the mandatory scope.

*Does not help post-persistence.* This self-check only ever matches against
*transient* provenance, which does not survive persistence
(`docs/ai_info/agent_rules.adoc`'s persistence-boundary rule: Intent
survives, Resolution/derived provenance does not). On a freshly reimported
frame there is no surviving transient record to match against, so a
physically present FK-shaped column is *always* treated as an unverified
collision after reimport, regardless of this refinement. That is intentional
— see <<I>>.

[[E]]
== E. Truthful FK materialization provenance

*Design Question, site 1 concrete answer.*

*Is the existing return contract sufficient?* No. `core/fk.py::apply_fk_helpers`
today returns only the resulting `pd.DataFrame`; the per-definition
materialized/skipped fact is computed (`if helper_col in first_cols:
continue`) and then discarded. `fk_helpers/enrich.py::enrich_helpers` cannot
write truthful provenance without that fact.

*Is a narrow result structure needed?* Yes, but it must not change
`apply_fk_helpers`'s existing signature or return type. `apply_fk_helpers` is
re-exported from `spreadsheet_handling.core.__init__` (`__all__` includes
`"apply_fk_helpers"`) and is exercised directly, as a DataFrame-returning
call, by `tests/unit/domain/transformations/test_fk_helpers_enrich.py` and by
the primitive-package architecture guard
(`tests/architecture/dependency_guards/test_fk_helper_primitives_consume_policy.py`,
which allow-lists `apply_fk_helpers` by name). Changing its return type is a
gated public-surface break for no evidenced benefit.

*Proposed shape (design-level, not final naming):*

[source,python]
----
class FkHelperMaterializationResult(NamedTuple):
    frame: pd.DataFrame
    materialized: list[FKDef]        # definitions this call actually wrote
    skipped_existing: list[FKDef]    # definitions whose helper_col collided
                                      # with an already-present column

def materialize_fk_helpers(
    df, fk_defs, id_value_maps, levels, helper_prefix="_",
    helper_value_provider=None,
) -> FkHelperMaterializationResult: ...

def apply_fk_helpers(df, fk_defs, id_value_maps, levels, helper_prefix="_",
                      helper_value_provider=None) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Preserved for compatibility; existing callers unaffected."""
    return materialize_fk_helpers(
        df, fk_defs, id_value_maps, levels, helper_prefix,
        helper_value_provider,
    ).frame
----

`materialize_fk_helpers` becomes the real implementation (identical
materialization logic to today, plus bucket bookkeeping); `apply_fk_helpers`
becomes a thin compatibility wrapper. No duplicated materialization logic, no
change to any existing caller's observed behavior or type.

*How `fk_helpers/enrich.py` uses it:* `enrich_helpers` calls
`materialize_fk_helpers` per frame instead of `apply_fk_helpers`. For each
frame, it builds the *provable* set passed to `_write_helper_provenance`:

* every definition in `materialized` (freshly written this call); plus,
  only if the optional refinement in <<D>> is implemented,
* every definition in `skipped_existing` whose shape matches an existing
  transient provenance entry for that exact `column` on that sheet.

Definitions in `skipped_existing` that are not matched write *no* provenance
entry — the direct fix for the confirmed contract violation (FK provenance
must record only materialized state; three independent maintained sources —
`docs/semantic_model/12_contract_matrices.adoc`,
`docs/technical_model/ch04_concepts/fk_helper_metadata.adoc`,
`docs/ai_info/concepts_fk_helpers.adoc` — already say this; only the runtime
was wrong).

*Avoiding duplicated physical-materialization decisions in Domain code:*
Core (`core/fk.py`) remains the *sole* place that decides column-level
collision (`helper_col in first_cols`); it only gains a reporting
obligation, not new policy. Domain (`fk_helpers/enrich.py` /
`fk_helpers/provenance.py`) owns the *meaning* of that report (whether a
skip may still be claimed via the optional self-check) — this matches the
existing Core/Domain boundary (Core is a content-free mechanism; Domain owns
metadata lifecycle, per the Domain Maintainer Contract §8).

*Replay/idempotence:* a single call to `enrich_helpers` is unaffected. A
second call in the same session against frames that already carry FK's own
prior output either (a) refreshes provenance via the optional self-check, or
(b) — without the refinement — writes no provenance for the re-collided
definitions, which is an accepted, narrow, currently-unevidenced-as-relied-on
behavior change flagged in <<D>> and <<P>>.

*Pre-existing requested label — final rule:* absent → materialize and claim;
present and self-verified as FK's own (optional refinement only) →
no-op and claim; present and unverified/foreign → no-op, no claim, no
overwrite, no error by default (diagnostic per <<J>>).

[[F]]
== F. Deletion-authority rule

[.lead]
*A cleanup executor may delete a column identified through an FK-attributed
candidate name if and only if that name is currently present in
`_meta.derived.sheets.<sheet>.helper_columns` (truthful, same-run,
materialized-only transient provenance, per <<E>>). `_meta.helper_policies.fk`
(durable v2 relation policy) never independently authorizes deletion — it
identifies what FK would request, not what FK currently produced.*

[[repro-closure]]
=== Why this closes all three reproductions

[cols="1,4,3", options="header"]
|===
| Repro | Mechanism that closes it | Residual risk

| 1 (same-run collision)
| <<E>> alone closes it: FK never claims `_B_name` (collision unverified,
  Lookup's shape does not match any prior FK record), so transient
  provenance never names it; `remove_fk_helpers` finds no FK evidence and
  <<F>> then also independently blocks any durable-policy fallback.
| None evidenced.

| 2 (durable-policy-only, FK never ran)
| <<F>> alone closes it: no transient provenance exists for the sheet at
  all; durable policy is no longer consulted for deletion candidates; the
  column is left in place.
| None evidenced.

| 3 (second executor, same setup)
| <<F>> applied identically at the `derived_column_policy.py` site (<<H>>);
  its own durable-relation fallback (`_safe_policy_helper_names`) is removed
  as a deletion-candidate source.
| None evidenced.
|===

Both corrections are independently sufficient for their respective repro;
together they are the minimum coherent design (removing only one would leave
one variant open, per Review 001 Area 6's own smallest-local-repair test).

=== Design Question 2 — what proves current FK production?

*Same-run transient provenance:* sufficient, once truthful (<<E>>), because
by construction it is written only when FK either wrote the column this
call or self-verified it as its own unchanged prior output. The one
invalidating path this design identifies — a later same-run step
overwriting the physical column's content while leaving FK's transient
record untouched — is not evidenced as reachable by any accepted
reproduction or maintained consumer; no supported transformation is known to
silently overwrite another family's helper column in place without also
touching its own or the sibling provenance. This is named as an open
assumption, not a proven invariant — see <<S>>.

*Durable-policy-only path:* does not, and structurally cannot, prove current
physical production, with or without persistence (<<I>>). This design
deliberately does *not* build a value-verification override
(`should cleanup verify the current column against what FK would produce?`
from the design brief) — see <<L>> for why. It does *not* ask the caller to
supply a stronger durable declaration — that would be new durable producer
history, explicitly disallowed (the design brief's own instruction: "Do not
invent durable producer history merely to preserve old cleanup behavior"). The
chosen answer is the brief's own named conservative option: *the existing
persistence/reimport contract intentionally leaves the helper column in
place rather than risk deleting Lookup- or user-owned data.*

[[G]]
== G. FK direct-cleanup design

`fk_helpers/drop.py::_columns_to_drop_by_sheet` currently builds its result
from `provenance_by_sheet` (transient, preferred) with a *fallback* to
`relations` (durable policy) for any sheet absent from provenance. That
fallback branch is removed entirely for deletion-candidate purposes.

*New behavior:* `columns_to_drop` is built from `provenance_by_sheet` only —
i.e. from `derived_helper_columns_by_sheet(frames)`, which is *already*
truthful once <<E>> lands (no separate new accessor is required; see the
non-design rationale in <<K>> and <<9-facade>>). `resolve_v2_fk_relations`
remains called by `drop_helpers` for exactly one *unrelated, unchanged*
purpose: the existing "was FK helper policy ever configured at all" clear
error (`missing_fk_policy_error`) when *neither* provenance nor durable
policy exist for the whole run. That precondition check is not a deletion
decision and is out of scope for this design; it is left as-is.

*Concrete disposition:*

* sheet has truthful transient provenance naming the column → delete
  (unchanged happy path);
* sheet has no transient provenance for that column, durable policy names it
  → leave in place, no error, no exception raised by default (diagnostic per
  <<J>>);
* neither transient provenance nor durable policy exist anywhere in the run
  → `missing_fk_policy_error` (unchanged, pre-existing behavior, not a
  deletion-authority question).

H5 (`_clean_helper_provenance` → `reconcile_enrich_lookup_provenance`) is
unchanged — see <<K>>.

[[H]]
== H. Derived-column-policy integration design

`derived_column_policy.py::_safe_policy_helper_names` exists *solely* to
supply FK-attributed deletion candidates from durable v2 relation policy.
Under <<F>> that source is never authorized, so this function's entire
purpose is removed: `apply_derived_column_policy` stops calling it, and its
result is no longer unioned into `durable_helper_names` in
`apply_derived_column_policy`'s body.

*What remains, and why it is not touched:* `_resolve_derived_identity`'s
reading of `_meta.derived.sheets[source].helper_columns` (FK's transient
provenance) is *already correct* — it reads the same truthful subkey
`fk_helpers/drop.py` reads, with its own (stricter) shape validation that
raises clear `ValueError`s on malformed entries. This design does *not*
force that reading through a shared function merely because
`fk_helpers/policy.py` already has an equivalent reader
(`derived_helper_columns_by_sheet`) — per the design brief's own caution
("do not create generic shared infrastructure merely because two callers
need the same FK-owned answer"), see <<9-facade>>. `_resolve_derived_identity`
also reads Lookup's own `enrich_lookup` declared names in the same call —
that half is Lookup's own self-declared identity of its own column and is
*not* the defect (a family deleting a column via its own declaration of its
own artifact is not "requested identity borrowed from a different owner");
it is untouched.

`_safe_durable_helper_names` (`_meta.sheets[source].helper_columns`,
Workbook-View-owned durable sheet helper declaration) is a *different,
non-FK-owned* durable declaration and is out of scope for this design (see
<<repository-boundaries>> in <<Q>>); it is untouched.

*Net effect on `apply_derived_column_policy`:* `durable_helper_names` in the
function body is fed only by `_safe_durable_helper_names` (Workbook-View,
unchanged, out of scope); `policy_helper_names` (`_safe_policy_helper_names`,
FK durable-relation fallback) is deleted. FK-attributed candidate names come
exclusively from `_resolve_derived_identity`'s transient-provenance read,
unchanged.

[[9-facade]]
=== Design Question 9 — why no new shared facade is designed

The two executors do not, in the corrected design, need a shared *data*
accessor: each already reads FK's transient provenance correctly and
independently, at acceptably low duplication risk (a short,
already-well-validated read of one `_meta.derived.sheets[*].helper_columns`
subkey). The defect-causing logic — the *durable-policy fallback* — is not
consolidated into a shared function; it is *deleted outright* from both call
sites, because the answer both sites need ("durable policy never
authorizes deletion") is a fixed rule, not a computed value. This is the
smallest design that prevents both executors from "independently
reimplementing FK authority semantics" (the concern in the design brief):
after this change, neither module contains *any* FK-authority-adjacent logic
beyond reading FK's own truthful transient record.

A narrow, optional hygiene improvement (not required for correctness, not
part of the mandatory scope): `derived_column_policy.py` could call
`fk_helpers.derived_helper_columns_by_sheet` instead of maintaining its own
parallel reader, purely to reduce the duplicate-implementation surface
Review 001's FIND-002 already flagged as a readability/duplication risk one
cycle earlier. This is left to the implementer's judgment at slice time (see
<<N>>) and is explicitly not mandated here.

[[I]]
== I. Persistence/reimport behavior

*Design Question 3 answer.* No special-casing of "before persistence" versus
"after persistence" is designed or required. <<F>>'s rule is stated once,
uniformly: *truthful same-run transient provenance is required; durable
policy alone is never sufficient — regardless of whether a persistence
boundary was crossed.* Persistence does not change the rule; it only changes
which evidence is available. Transient `_meta.derived` is stripped at the
persistence boundary by existing, unchanged, and out-of-scope machinery
(`pipeline/persistence_boundary.py`; "Persist Intent, discard recomputable
Resolution" — `docs/ai_info/agent_rules.adoc`); durable
`_meta.helper_policies.fk.relations` survives.

*Concrete consequence:* after a reimport with no fresh `add_fk_helpers`
materialization pass in the same session, `remove_fk_helpers` and
`apply_derived_column_policy` can no longer automatically delete a
physically present FK-shaped helper column from durable policy alone. This
is a *deliberate, evidenced* compatibility narrowing, not an oversight — see
<<P>> for its scope and <<R>> for the recovery path a future, separately
authorized design could offer. It is consistent with
`derived_column_policy.py`'s own pre-existing docstring, which already
hedges that durable fallback declarations "identify columns only" — the
implementation was out of sync with its own documented contract before this
design.

*Does running `add_fk_helpers` first restore automatic post-reimport
cleanup?* No, and this must not be assumed by an implementer. The optional
self-check in <<D>> only matches against *surviving transient* provenance,
which does not exist immediately after reimport (it was stripped). Running
`add_fk_helpers` against a freshly reimported frame therefore still treats
the physically present column as an unverified collision (no claim). No
mechanism in this design can regenerate trustworthy same-run evidence for a
column that already existed before the current run started, short of
FK genuinely re-materializing it from a state where the label was absent.
This gap is named explicitly in <<R>>, not silently worked around.

[[J]]
== J. Failure and diagnostics contract

[cols="3,3,3", options="header"]
|===
| Case | Disposition | Diagnostic

| Materialization: requested label already occupied, not FK's own
  (verified-or-not per <<D>>)
| No-op: no overwrite, no provenance claim.
| Non-raising diagnostic (warning/log; exact mechanism left to
  implementation) naming frame, helper column label, and the FK relation
  identity (`fk_column`, `target`, `value_field`) that was skipped.

| `drop_helpers`: candidate named only by durable policy, no transient
  evidence
| No-op: column left in place, no exception.
| Non-raising diagnostic naming sheet, helper column label, and "durable
  policy only, no truthful transient provenance" as the refusal reason. No
  existing return-shape change (`drop_helpers` has no findings-frame
  surface today); a log/warning is the only available channel without a
  gated interface change.

| `drop_helpers`: transient provenance names a label no longer present in
  the frame (stale record vs. current structure)
| Safe no-op (unchanged existing behavior — nothing to delete).
| None required; not a new failure mode introduced by this design.

| `apply_derived_column_policy`, `policy="drop"`
| Same no-op as `drop_helpers`'s durable-policy-only case.
| Same non-raising diagnostic channel; `drop`'s documented contract ("no
  value comparison", no findings-frame output) is preserved unchanged.

| `apply_derived_column_policy`, `policy="warn_on_mismatch"`
| Column left in place; call succeeds.
| A new, distinct `DerivedColumnFinding` `rule_type`
  (e.g. `fk_deletion_unauthorized`), severity `"warn"`, added to the
  existing findings frame alongside (never merged with) `derived_value_mismatch`
  findings — per the design brief's explicit instruction not to conflate
  mismatch with authorization refusal.

| `apply_derived_column_policy`, `policy="fail_on_mismatch"`
| Raises `ValueError` via the existing
  `if policy == "fail_on_mismatch" and failures: raise ...` path.
| Same new `rule_type`, severity `"fail"`. *Flagged for explicit maintainer
  sign-off* (<<S>>): this gives `fail_on_mismatch` a new class of failure
  trigger (authorization refusal, not a value mismatch) it did not
  previously have.

| Durable policy names a column belonging to Lookup or arbitrary user/caller
  payload
| Identical to the generic "no transient evidence" case — no special-casing
  by producer identity (<<D6>>).
| Identical diagnostic; the reason never names Lookup or any other producer,
  only "no truthful FK transient provenance."
|===

Diagnostics never inspect or report unrelated frame contents; they name only
the frame, the candidate column label, and (where available) the FK relation
identity that was declined.

[[K]]
== K. Lookup interaction / H5 relationship

*Design Question 5 answer.* No new Lookup-owned pre-deletion handoff is
introduced. <<F>>'s rule is decided entirely from FK-owned evidence (FK's
own transient provenance); it never reads `_meta.derived.sheets.*.enrich_lookup`
or any other Lookup-owned shape. H5
(`reconcile_enrich_lookup_provenance`) is unchanged: it remains a stateless,
recompute-on-demand, *post*-mutation truthfulness predicate over Lookup's own
declared `helper_columns` and the frame's current columns, called by
`fk_helpers/provenance.py::_clean_helper_provenance` exactly as today. Its
scope and contract are unaffected because, with <<F>> in place, FK's own
mutation that triggers H5 will far less often be the wrongful deletion of a
Lookup-produced column in the first place — H5's existing job (deciding
whether Lookup's own sibling record is still truthful) is unchanged and still
necessary for cases outside this defect's scope (e.g. a *legitimate* FK
deletion of its own helper that happens to share a sheet entry with an
unrelated Lookup record).

[[D6]]
[[L]]
== L. Formula/value and foreign-artifact equivalence disposition

*Design Question 6 (user/canonical collisions) answer.* <<F>> and <<D>> are
both stated purely in terms of "does FK have truthful evidence it produced
this column" — neither ever asks "was the current producer Lookup." A
caller-supplied ordinary column that happens to collide with FK's requested
helper label is handled identically to a Lookup-produced collision: skipped
at materialization (<<D>>), never claimed, never deleted by either cleanup
executor without truthful transient evidence (<<F>>). This generality is
structural, not a separately maintained rule — there is no producer-identity
branch anywhere in this design to keep in sync.

*Design Question 7 answer.* Value/semantic-equivalence adoption of a foreign
producer's physically present column (Candidate C) is *rejected outright and
not designed*. Reasons, all evidenced by the accepted research and review:

* Candidate A (reject on collision) is already sufficient to close the
  confirmed defect (<<repro-closure>>); building equivalence machinery
  before it is needed contradicts the design brief's explicit "if A or B is
  sufficient, do not design generic adoption."
* Review 001 names the concrete risk directly: a Lookup-produced formula and
  an FK-produced value can display equal current results while carrying
  different update/lifecycle semantics; current value equality does not
  imply equivalent producer semantics.
* The one place this design *does* compare structured state (<<D>>'s
  optional self-check) deliberately compares only FK's own prior
  *metadata shape* against itself — never a foreign producer's declared
  identity, never cell values, never a formula/value distinction. It cannot
  drift into Candidate C because it has no path to a foreign producer's
  record at all.

No equivalence/adoption machinery is designed for any site in this document.
If a future, separately evidenced need for adopting a foreign producer's
artifact arises, it is out of this design's authorization — see <<R>>.

[[Q]]
== Q. Explicit non-goals

Restated from the design authorization, confirmed absent from every section
above:

* claimant / current-reliance as a required primitive;
* claim registration, claim release, a claim AST, a claim ledger, reference
  counting, a shared-claim protocol;
* a generic artifact registry, stable artifact IDs, global artifact
  identity, or rename-following identity;
* a central producer registry or whole-Domain materialization census;
* Trusted-Ingress redesign, persistence redesign, or schema-maintenance
  redesign;
* promotion, deprecation, removal, or merge of Lookup Enrichment as a
  product concept (<<R>>);
* value/formula equivalence or adoption machinery for a foreign producer's
  artifact (<<L>>);
* any new `_meta` root, sub-key, or schema-version bump — this design adds
  zero new metadata; it only changes which existing, already-registered
  entries are written or consulted, and for what purpose.

[[repository-boundaries]]
Also explicitly out of scope: Workbook-View-owned durable sheet helper
declarations (`_meta.sheets[*].helper_columns`), which are a different,
non-FK-owned durable authority and are untouched by <<H>>.

[[R]]
== R. Deferred follow-ups

* *Restoring automatic post-reimport FK-helper cleanup* (named gap, <<I>>):
  if a real maintained or credible external consumer is later shown to need
  automatic deletion from durable policy alone after persistence, the
  necessary follow-up is a *new*, separately authorized design choosing
  between (a) a narrow, explicitly-scoped, FK-owned physical-production
  verification performed at cleanup time against current source/target data
  (weighing the formula/value risk named in <<L>> honestly, not
  reflexively), or (b) an explicit, opt-in, caller-asserted durable
  declaration analogous to XRef's guarded `drop_source` flag, which shifts
  authority to an explicit caller assertion rather than passive name
  matching. Neither is designed or authorized here.
* *Registry and Functional wording* identified in <<M>> but not written.
* *Optional hygiene simplification* in `derived_column_policy.py`
  (<<9-facade>>): reuse `fk_helpers.derived_helper_columns_by_sheet` instead
  of a parallel reader, purely to reduce duplication flagged by an earlier
  review cycle. Not required for correctness.
* *Lookup Enrichment user-facing publication posture*: a separate maintainer
  question (not decided here, not decided by this design) is whether Lookup
  Enrichment's independent user-facing product status should be marked, in a
  future DMC/publication-posture convention (e.g.
  `user_facing_documentation: false` with an explicit reason), as
  not-yet-established rather than promoted or merged with FK Helpers. This
  design takes no position and does not touch Lookup's user-facing
  documentation, DMC, or product status. Recorded here only so the intent
  is not lost.
* *Architecture guard* for the boundary in <<9-facade>> (recommended in
  <<O>>, not mandatory).

[[T]]
== T. Design readiness verdict

*READY for implementation planning*, subject to the independent adversarial
review recommended below and explicit maintainer sign-off on the two items
flagged in <<S>> (the `interfaces_and_gates` status of the Core return-shape
change, and the `fail_on_mismatch` new-failure-trigger decision). The design
closes all three accepted reproductions (<<repro-closure>>) using only
mechanisms already evidenced as necessary: a truthful provenance report at
the existing materialization primitive, and the deletion of one unsafe
fallback branch at each of the two cleanup executors. It introduces no new
metadata, no claim/reliance infrastructure, and no value/equivalence
machinery, matching the narrowed-scope authorization exactly.
