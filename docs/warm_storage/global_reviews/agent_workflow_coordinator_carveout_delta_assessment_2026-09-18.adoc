== 2. Executive conclusion

The accepted boundary remains sound. Current evidence strengthens it rather
than falsifying it: Slice 5B and the first Useful Loop show that Run/Hop/Finding,
finite profile routing, fresh review, trusted Git effects, persistence, and
structured result handling form a coherent generic product. The post-run IOR-04
architecture miss also makes the consumer boundary more concrete: repository
architecture checks and other domain-specific evidence are consumer-owned policy
values, not Coordinator releases.

The repository is *not yet ready for physical extraction*. Five of the original
six prerequisites remain blocking as prerequisite groups: P1 through P5. P6 is
closed. The minimum pre-split work is therefore *five independently reviewable
closure slices*, not a redesign and not Slice 5C+:

. close F1/F2 authority ingress;
. move consumer evidence commands into pinned consumer policy;
. inject a Coordinator-owned agent operating contract;
. establish a real standalone package/import identity and copy-run proof; and
. take the explicit incubator-FTR lifecycle handoff decision.

C4, C6, C7, and the remaining public-claim part of C10 are split-time
obligations. C8 is partially closed by Slice 5B's pinned per-phase durable
artifact map. They do not justify P7 or later prerequisites. Professional-trust
environment isolation, automated outer-loop authorship, Slice 5C--5F, a
luxurious CLI/TUI, a second real consumer, and PyPI publication remain outside
the carve-out gate.

The smallest truthful destination remains a Git-native, single-WorkItem,
serial, bounded-Hop governed execution tool. `spreadsheet-handling/core` becomes
its first consumer through a pinned tool dependency and consumer-owned governing
inputs.

== 6. F1/F2 relevance without importing the Outer Loop

=== 6.1 Required before reusable extraction

The reusable inner operator must not export two unsafe implications.

F1 minimum:: Admission must prove that no phase path authority can modify any
selected governing input. The governing set is the five supplied inputs plus
selected component sources and the Coordinator-owned injected-contract digest.
The check rejects before Run creation, state-root creation, or adapter entry.
The standalone product contract says that a committed WorkItem is accepted only
as externally authorized input; commit presence is not independent authorization.

F2 minimum:: Pinned consumer policy must place a ceiling over WorkItem path scope
and `max_autonomous_hops`, just as current checks already bound phase scope by
WorkItem and phase actions/evidence by policy. The hard rollout ceiling remains
an additional product limit, not the consumer authority ceiling.

These are small schema/admission checks. They do not require identities,
signatures, a policy engine, a scope lattice, an Outer Loop, or automated intake.

=== 6.2 Required only before automated outer-loop WorkItem authorship

The following remain later work: independently distinguish human/policy-approved
governing-input provenance from agent output across Runs; define who turns a
proposal into authority; govern candidate/backlog admission; authorize
materialization and child launch; propagate parent/child budgets; and prove that
harness-level instructions cannot become self-authored governance. These are the
future F1 transition-owner problem, not carve-out mechanics.

=== 6.3 Explicitly unsupported after carve-out

The initial standalone tool does not author, approve, select, or launch its own
WorkItems; does not operate an Outer Loop; does not pre-authorize child Runs; and
does not claim that any tracked YAML is independently authorized merely because
it is committed. Those unsupported capabilities may remain unsupported
indefinitely without invalidating the carve-out.

. Freeze core at a clean, accepted full SHA on `main`. Never run filtering in
  the incubator or in the empty target.

  verify object/ref completeness.

  spreadsheet-handling release tags or unrelated branches.

  or add one directory-level authority notice. Create a new concise standalone

  silently treated as current standalone authority.

`git-filter-repo` is not installed in the assessed environment. Installing and
pinning a reviewed version is an operational prerequisite for Phase C, not a new

== 11. Bilateral healing

Healing is explicit and bounded. It begins after mechanical filtering and
consumer reconnection; it is not an excuse to move everything and defer all
ownership work.

=== 11.1 Required before first post-split commit acceptance

Standalone:: Import/package/CLI tests pass from an installed artifact; C6/C7/C10
are truthful; provenance map and authority notice exist; current tree contains
no spreadsheet/DMC code or consumer governing values; README, license, test
configuration, and minimal CI/build metadata work from repository root.

Core:: Exact dependency resolves; consumer governing inputs parse; invocation
uses the public package/application; no direct removed-path import exists; no
duplicate executable Coordinator remains; focused consumer integration tests and
diff hygiene pass.

=== 11.2 Required before carve-out closure

Standalone:: Tests live in natural locations; current normative docs describe
implemented 1--5B reality without treating imported incubator artifacts as
authority; historical links either resolve through provenance or are marked as
incubator links; source-style/architecture checks include the package; CI builds
and tests the distribution; repository navigation has no active incubator-path
assumptions.

Core:: Dead Coordinator implementation/test paths are removed; docs and Makefile
use `wfc`; cold historical records remain navigable; active backlog/FTR indexes
do not claim local implementation ownership; an architecture guard rejects
`scripts.workflow_coordinator` and sibling-source imports; core's full suite is
green with the pinned dependency.

=== 11.3 Bounded follow-up debt

Cosmetic relocation of every imported review, perfect repair of every historical
cross-link, richer CLI ergonomics, public publishing, multi-platform installers,
TUI/assistant work, provider plugins, additional real consumers, and 5C+ may
remain debt or future product work. None prevents closure when both repositories
are truthful and independently runnable.

`~/repos/spreadsheet-handling/` or the sibling layout. This is the decisive

== 13. Concrete carve-out exit criteria

The carve-out is complete only when all statements below are true:

. `workflow-coordinator` builds, installs, imports, and runs through `wfc` as a
  native standalone repository, with its generic tests and neutral convergence
  proof green.
. `spreadsheet-handling/core` resolves an exact standalone commit and exercises
  its consumer workflows without local implementation imports or sibling paths.
. Coordinator schemas/mechanics/contracts live standalone; spreadsheet/domain
  values, WorkItems, policies, profiles, evidence commands, and priority remain
  in core.
. Incubator FTR authority has an explicit superseded boundary; standalone current
  authority does not pretend imported historical reviews/FTR are newly normative.
. The old-to-new commit map and both split/reconnection anchors reconstruct
  provenance, while core remains citation authority for pre-split SHAs.
. Accepted inner-loop capability, including deterministic Slice-5B convergence,
  remains green; both repositories pass their required focused and full-suite
  checkpoints.
. Neither repository depends on the pre-split filesystem layout, and neither has
  active dead topology that suggests the implementation still belongs to core.

Filter success alone satisfies none of these except history rewriting. Slice
5C--5F, Outer Loop, automated authorship, CLI/TUI productization, professional
trust enforcement, PyPI publication, and a second consumer are explicitly absent
from the exit.
