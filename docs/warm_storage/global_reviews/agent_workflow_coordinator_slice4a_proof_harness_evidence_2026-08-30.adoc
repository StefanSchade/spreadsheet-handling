== Implementation scope

This change adds the small repository-controlled operator harness
`scripts/workflow_coordinator/proof_harness.py`. It composes the accepted
`run_real_one_hop` API; it does not create a coordinator, change the Slice-4A
adapter/process/reducer semantics, or consume a future live-proof authorization.

The documented file-based operator contract is:

[source,text]
----
.venv/bin/python scripts/workflow_coordinator/proof_harness.py \
  --proof-root /path/to/new/disposable-proof-root
----

The selected proof root must be new. The runner creates the disposable `repo/`
and disjoint `trusted-state/` directories, initializes a no-remote repository,
tracks only `PROOF.md`, commits a deterministic baseline, creates fresh checkout,
Run, and invocation identities, and runs the fixed `WFC-S4-PROOF` / `H001` /
`max_autonomous_hops=1` setup. A real executable is not used in this evidence.

== Durable evidence behavior

Every attempted runner invocation writes `proof-summary.json` and
`launch-provenance.json` under the proof root after setup, including when the
process could not be launched or returns an unresolved result. The summary records
the proof/repository/trusted-state paths, checkout/run/invocation IDs, expected and
actual commit subjects, initial/final HEAD and range, status, remotes, tracked and
changed paths, final `PROOF.md`, lock facts, run status, Hop use, stop reason,
dispatch-marker status, anomalies, process outcome, and bounded diagnostics.

`CodexOutcome.stdout` and `.stderr` are persisted in the summary. They remain the
already-bounded 16,384-character tails captured by Slice-4A; no raw transcript store was
added.

Launch provenance is intentionally weak but useful: current PID/PPID, no more than
eight ancestor PID/process-name records, and three boolean environment-presence
indicators (`CODEX`, `CLAUDE`/`ANTHROPIC`, `OPENAI`). It explicitly records that
environment values and argv are not recorded. This permits a factual review for
ordinary-host versus visibly agent-nested context without claiming cryptographic
attestation, and without environment or credential dumping.

The success fake writes the expected `PROOF.md` content and one expected-subject
commit, produces a valid correlated result, completes the Run/checkpoint, retires
the dispatch marker, and leaves self-contained summary/provenance evidence. The
post-spawn fake exits nonzero without a result and emits oversized stdout/stderr;
the summary proves the conservative Hop charge, retained marker,
`reconcile_required`, and bounded 16,384-character diagnostic tails. The pre-spawn missing-executable
case proves a durable local error with no Hop charge and no retained marker.
