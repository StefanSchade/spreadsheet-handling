== 1. Executive determination

Slice 5B is *implementation-ready in principle*, but it is not presently
contracted or authorized. The accepted Slice-5A path supplies a sound serial,
fresh-context, Git/governing-input, publication, attempt-accounting, and
per-invocation evidence base. The Run/Hop/Finding/reducer model already carries
the authoritative lifecycle semantics needed for review/correction convergence.
No replacement state model, graph engine, supervisor, numerical convergence
score, real-provider proof, Outer Loop, Intake, or Work Selection is needed.

The current integrated path is nevertheless not yet a 5B implementation. Five
load-bearing gaps remain:

. `run_serial_hops()` is an H001/H002 unrolling and operator preflight rejects
  `max_autonomous_hops > 2`; replay J's integrated five-Hop lifecycle cannot run.
. `vertical.run_one_hop()` records `finding_delta_ids=()` before the reducer
  allocates or validates Finding identities. The field remains untruthful for
  every integrated Finding change.
. durable-artifact paths are one unqualified operator-argv tuple validated only
  against the initial phase and passed only to H001. Later required review and
  re-review phases have no pinned phase-specific declaration.
. trusted evidence `fail` and `error` are collapsed into
  `MechanicalFacts.required_evidence_error`; both hard-stop. No profile field
  identifies the one declared route a clean failed gate may take.
. the review `blocking_policy` is parsed as arbitrary prose and is never enforced.
  At current HEAD a gate can request `complete` while an open blocking Finding
  remains.

Two related evidence/context gaps must be closed in the same integration work:
trusted observation status/digest is not durably retained per Hop after the next
checkpoint replaces the previous one, and the continuation prompt contains only
the immediately prior Hop's ID/outcome/range/summary. It does not project the
current Finding ledger, review descriptor, prior trusted observations, or the
correction diff needed for bounded re-review.

These changes form one coherent acceptance surface: a fresh broad gate creates a
Coordinator-identified blocker; a correction receives it but cannot close it; a
fresh declared gate re-reviews the correction and alone resolves/residualizes/
supersedes/reopens according to its authority; deterministic evidence and budget
rules decide whether another bounded Hop is eligible. None of the individual
plumbing corrections is independently useful enough to justify inventing
5B-A/5B-B/5B-C.

Recommendation:: Keep *one Slice 5B*. After independent acceptance of this
assessment, draft one bounded normative Slice-5B implementation contract, review
that contract independently, and obtain explicit maintainer authorization before
any implementation.

The ordering is important. The Hop is recorded before the reducer allocates a
new Finding ID. That is why the integrated path currently persists an empty
`finding_delta_ids` tuple even when the reducer later changes the ledger.

=== 5.2 Identity, state, and authority

Finding identity allocation:: `reducer._next_finding_id()` allocates the first
unused run-local `F%03d` ID. An agent proposes `finding_id: null`; the Coordinator
owns the resulting identity. Existing IDs must exist and their invariant text
cannot be silently rewritten.

Finding state:: `model.FindingState` is exactly `open`, `resolved`, `residual`,
or `superseded`. `Finding` stores current state, blocking flag, introducing and
last-changing Hop IDs, cumulative evidence refs, concise disposition, and
successor/external references.

Existing transitions:: New open Findings may be proposed by an ordinary agent.
Open may become resolved, residual, or superseded only with the corresponding
authority. Resolved may reopen only with reopen authority plus
`new_material_evidence=true`, or become superseded. Residual must be non-blocking;
superseded requires a successor reference. Other transitions fail closed.

Disposition authority:: Human authority may perform all defined disposition
operations. A current review phase with `authority: gate` may perform only the
operations listed in its `finding_authority`. Ordinary agents can create/update
open Findings but cannot resolve, residualize, supersede, reopen, or downgrade
blocking. Supervisors have no Finding mutation surface. Correction is an
ordinary-agent phase unless the profile explicitly makes it a gate; therefore a
normal correction cannot authoritatively close its blocker.

Modeled but not integrated:: Reducer/replay tests prove stable identity,
multi-loop correction, residual, supersession, reopening, advisory/gate
separation, supervisor limits, human escalation, and budget behavior. They do
not prove that the supported operator projects the ledger into later prompts,
attributes IDs to actual Hops, retains trusted evidence per Hop, or drives the
five-Hop non-DMC profile.

=== 5.3 What the reducer already proves

The reducer is already the correct authority owner for:

* mechanical preemption before route or Finding authority;
* route membership in the current pinned phase;
* advisory review's inability to complete;
* gate/human-only Finding disposition;
* stable Finding identity and invariant text;
* reopen-only-with-new-material evidence;
* finite routes, budget stop, supervisor restriction, and human/prerequisite
  stop states.

It does not currently enforce a gate's blocking policy or distinguish trusted
evidence `fail` from `error`. Those are bounded reducer/profile corrections, not
a replacement reducer.

=== 5.4 What the serial driver already knows

`operator.run_serial_hops()` knows the current immutable Run, phase, status,
budget, current HEAD, checkout association, pinned governing digests, task,
components, and per-attempt binding/outcome evidence. It validates the full 5A
inter-Hop transaction before H002. It does not iterate over an arbitrary finite
sequence: it spells out H001 and one conditional H002 and returns.

== 7. Minimum integrated choreography

The accepted Phase/Route/Profile model is sufficient. The minimum supported
flow is replay J expressed through strict YAML and the real serial composition:

[source,text]
----
bug_investigation (fresh, observation)
  -> fix (bounded payload correction)
  -> independent_review (fresh broad gate; creates F001 open/blocking)
  -> correction (receives F001; may add evidence, cannot resolve it)
  -> rereview (fresh delta gate; resolves/residualizes/supersedes/reopens only
               within declared authority)
  -> complete, another declared correction edge, or bounded human/budget stop
----

The five invocations fit the architecture's already stated empirical
`N=1 -> 2 -> 5 -> 10` progression while keeping live execution unauthorized and
the deterministic proof finite. A 5B contract should set the supported local
rollout ceiling to five. The loop implementation may be general, but preflight
must still reject a WorkItem above the contracted ceiling before Run creation or
provider-capable entry.

Driver versus reducer:: The driver owns dispatch eligibility, Git/governing-input
revalidation, phase-specific invocation inputs, publication completion, fresh
identity/binding, and bounded context. The reducer remains authoritative for
preemption, Finding mutation, gate blocking policy, budget, and route/phase
transition. `operator.py` therefore needs a more general finite driver, but 5B is
not only an operator refactor: reducer, vertical evidence, strict input parsing,
prompt projection, and persistence evidence need bounded corrections too.

Profile data versus Python:: Phase names and route keys remain opaque. The
profile declares breadth, authority, blocking policy, permitted Finding
operations, finite routes, and the one evidence-failure route. WorkItem data
declares concrete per-phase durable-artifact paths. Python validates and applies
those declarations; it never recognizes a phase called `review`, `correction`,
or `rereview`.

Fresh review context:: Each adapter call remains a new `fresh` invocation. The
prompt is reconstructed from pinned current inputs and authoritative state:
current phase/HEAD/authority, compact Finding ledger, immediately prior Hop,
trusted evidence refs, and a bounded Git diff/reference. Conversation history is
not reused.

Known Findings in correction/re-review:: Project all current Finding records,
including stable ID, invariant, blocking/state, origin/last-changing Hop,
evidence refs, disposition, and successor ref. Do not create a parallel
"correction Finding" structure. The correction result may cite F001 and add
evidence while keeping it open; an attempted resolution from that non-gate phase
continues to fail closed.

New material and direct regressions:: The rereview profile declares a narrow
breadth over known Findings and direct regressions. The preceding correction
diff supplies the regression surface. Whether evidence is materially related is
a semantic gate/human judgment; the Coordinator only enforces identity,
authority, `new_material_evidence` for reopening, and declared routes. An
unrelated minor may be accepted as a declared residual by the gate without
restarting broad review.

Failed gate versus evidence/error:: At current HEAD, a valid gate result with
`outcome=changes_required` and a declared `goto` can route to correction because
the reducer does not otherwise interpret outcome. A trusted observation `fail`
cannot: it is conflated with `error` and preempts before routing. Malformed,
missing, or uncorrelated structured output after attempt acceptance becomes an
uncertain charged dispatch and `reconcile_required`; repository/hard-invariant
errors mechanically stop. 5B should preserve those hard-stop cases while giving
only a clean trusted `fail` the single profile-declared correction edge.

== 8. Convergence without hidden judgment

The Coordinator may deterministically expose and persist:

* current Finding state and before/after affected IDs;
* new, reopened, residual, superseded, and still-open blocking Findings;
* per-Hop commit ranges, changed paths, trusted observation changes, applied
  routes, and route repetition derived from Hop summaries;
* baseline/current HEAD, governing-input identity, scope/policy drift, Hop count,
  and remaining budget.

It must not turn those facts into deterministic claims of productive progress,
semantic narrowing, mechanism change, wrong owner/workflow, goalpost drift, or
lack of meaningful progress. Those classifications remain human judgment in 5B.
The fake agent may recommend escalation in its attributed result, but the
Coordinator records a human stop; it does not convert the recommendation into
truth.

Deterministic terminal proofs are still possible:

Successful convergence:: A declared gate resolves the blocker, no open blocking
Finding remains under `blocking_only`, required evidence/artifacts are valid, and
a declared `complete` route ends the Run.

Human escalation:: `requires_human`, a non-null escalation, an explicit
`await_human` route, or invalid authority preempts before another invocation.

Budget stop:: A valid route requiring another invocation at the contracted
limit produces `awaiting_human_budget`; no next marker or invocation ID exists.

Non-progress stop/recommendation:: The Run exposes repeated routes, unchanged
Findings/diffs/evidence, and budget facts. A scripted agent may request human or
a declared stop with an attributed rationale. Without a supervisor, only a human
may authoritatively classify no meaningful progress before budget.

Unresolved blocker:: `blocking_only` prohibits completion; a correction edge,
human stop, or budget stop remains.

Evidence error:: Provider `error`, malformed evidence, unknown provider,
repository anomaly, and structured-result error remain hard fail-closed cases
with no correction dispatch.

No convergence score or generic supervisor is needed. Optional supervisor
execution remains Slice 5E and may remain unimplemented indefinitely.

== 10. Smallest non-DMC deterministic fixture

No sufficient committed fixture exists today. Replay J proves reducer semantics
with Python objects, while the only committed profile under
`docs/ai_info/workflows/` is DMC dogfood.

The smallest adequate fixture is one committed input set under a path such as
`tests/fixtures/workflow_coordinator/non_dmc_bug_convergence/`:

* strict WorkItem/Profile/RepositoryPolicy/component-manifest/task files;
* a plain text or configuration payload with a deterministic defect, not Python
  source and not spreadsheet data;
* the five phases `bug_investigation`, `fix`, `independent_review`, `correction`,
  and `rereview`, ending through a route effect `complete`;
* fresh broad gate review and fresh delta gate rereview with
  `blocking_policy: blocking_only` and explicit Finding authorities;
* phase-specific required review artifacts such as `reviews/initial.adoc` and
  `reviews/rereview.adoc` declared in the WorkItem;
* a scripted in-process fake adapter whose outputs are keyed only by phase/Hop
  identity and whose mutation is bounded to fixture payload/artifact paths;
* WorkItem limit five and no provider executable.

The fixture falsifies DMC vocabulary, source-code-only correction, initial-phase
artifact assumptions, hard-coded review phase names, and consumer-specific
Python branches. It does not build Latin, Compliance, a second consumer, Outer
Loop, or another product.

== 11. One-slice decision

*Decision: ONE SLICE.*

The driver loop, Finding ID attribution, phase artifact selection, evidence
fail/error route, blocking-policy enforcement, context/evidence projection, and
non-DMC fixture all meet one acceptance statement: integrated fresh review ->
correction -> re-review converges truthfully under existing Finding authority.
None provides an independently meaningful operator capability without the
others:

* a five-Hop loop without truthful Findings/evidence is unsafe and unreviewable;
* truthful Finding IDs without prompt projection never guide correction;
* artifact mapping without a later-phase driver is unused;
* fail/error separation without an explicit gate route is ambiguous;
* a fixture without these mechanics remains another reducer replay.

They can and should be implemented in a safe dependency order, but separating
them into lifecycle sub-slices would divide code from its only meaningful
acceptance and add ceremony rather than reduce authority risk.

== 13. Deterministic acceptance plan

All acceptance is local/fake. No Coordinator provider invocation is required or
authorized.

[cols="1,5",options="header"]
|===
|Case |Deterministic proof

|Integrated convergence happy path
|Strict non-DMC inputs run five fresh Hops. Broad review creates a blocking
proposal with null ID; Coordinator allocates F001; correction receives F001 and
adds evidence while it stays open; fresh rereview resolves it under declared
gate authority; Run completes cleanly.

|Truthful attribution
|Each final Hop row contains exactly the Finding IDs it validly affected and its
own trusted observation refs/digests. `run.json`, checkpoint, and execution
evidence correlate exact Run/Hop/invocation/base/end/commit/Finding/evidence
facts after reload. Invalid/preempted deltas list none.

|Correction authority negative
|A correction result proposing `F001 -> resolved` fails closed and performs no
next Hop. The same proposal from rereview succeeds only when `resolve` is in the
gate's declared `finding_authority`.

|New material/direct regression
|Correction changes the payload and adds relevant evidence; rereview receives the
correction diff plus F001. A resolved Finding can reopen only at the gate with new
material evidence. A declared unrelated minor can become a non-blocking residual
and complete without rerunning broad review.

|Residual/superseded/reopened
|Scripted variants prove residual is non-blocking and gate-authorized;
superseded requires successor; reopen requires gate authority and new material;
ordinary agent/supervisor attempts fail.

|Phase artifacts
|Initial broad review and later rereview each satisfy their own required path.
Missing, uncommitted, out-of-scope, wrong-phase, or `none`-phase declarations
fail deterministically with correct charged/preflight semantics.

|Failed gate versus error
|A trusted provider `fail` at a gate takes only the explicitly named correction
route. The same fail without that declaration cannot complete. Provider `error`,
unknown provider, malformed result, repository anomaly, and hard invariant stop
without correction.

|Blocking policy
|A gate requesting complete while F001 remains open/blocking cannot complete.
After authoritative resolution, complete succeeds. A non-blocking accepted
residual does not force another broad review.

|Human / non-progress
|A scripted result with `requires_human` or escalation stops before the next
adapter call. A repeated correction route can recommend no progress in attributed
prose and stop/ask human; Coordinator does not label it deterministically.

|Budget
|A route needing H006 at the five-Hop ceiling produces
`awaiting_human_budget`; no H006 identity or marker exists. A smaller WorkItem
limit stops at its own boundary.

|No hidden recovery
|Malformed/uncorrelated/uncertain output, publication fault, dirty/unexpected
HEAD, governing-input drift, or adapter failure causes no retry, automatic
resume, fallback, or speculative next dispatch.

|Regression
|All current N=1, Slice-5A N=2, reducer/replay A--J, persistence, adapter, Git,
and action/scope authority tests remain green.

|Genericity
|The committed non-DMC text/config fixture completes with no DMC/spreadsheet/
Trusted-Ingress vocabulary or consumer-specific branch in
`scripts/workflow_coordinator/`.

== 15. Required explicit answers

. *Is Slice 5B currently implementation-ready in principle?* *YES*, technically,
  on the accepted 5A base and with the bounded gaps in this plan. It is *not*
  normatively contracted or authorized.
. *What exact gaps prevent integrated convergence?* The five-Hop driver/ceiling;
  Finding ID attribution; phase-specific pinned artifacts; evidence fail/error
  plus declared fail route; blocking-policy enforcement; policy domination and
  durable per-Hop attribution of trusted evidence; complete Finding/review/diff
  prompt projection; and the missing committed non-DMC fixture.
. *Are `finding_delta_ids` still incorrect/incomplete?* *YES.* The integrated
  vertical writes an empty tuple for every Hop.
. *Are phase-specific durable-artifact declarations still a real blocker?*
  *YES.* Only the initial phase can receive the current unqualified CLI tuple.
. *Can existing Run/Hop/Finding/reducer structures implement 5B without a new
  state model?* *YES.* Reuse them; add no Run status, Finding state, route effect,
  or aggregate. Two small strict input fields and derived evidence/context are
  sufficient.
. *Does the serial driver generalize cleanly beyond N=2?* The underlying
  one-Hop boundary and state do; `run_serial_hops()` itself does not. Replace its
  literal two calls with the same gate in a finite loop and contract a local/fake
  ceiling of five for the 5B fixture.
. *What distinguishes a failed gate from evidence/error at current HEAD?* A
  valid gate result may request a declared correction route; reducer outcome is
  not otherwise interpreted. Trusted evidence `fail` and `error` are currently
  indistinguishable hard preemptions. Malformed/missing/uncorrelated output is an
  uncertain charged dispatch requiring reconcile, not a failed gate.
. *Does 5B require new judgment machinery?* *NO.*
. *Does 5B require supervisor machinery?* *NO.*
. *Does 5B require a real-provider proof?* *NO.*
. *Does 5B require Outer Loop / Intake / Work Selection?* *NO.*
. *Does any proposed change conflict with the carve-out boundary?* *NO*, provided
  the category-B constraints are observed and the profile/WorkItem remain generic
  data rather than consumer semantics in Python.
. *Which carve-out prerequisites affect sequencing?* None must precede 5B.
  F1/F2, C1/C2/C8/C9 must not be worsened; 5B should close C9 and the relevant
  C8 ambiguity. C3/C4/C5/C6/C7 and T6 may wait until after accepted 5B but before
  carve-out. Outer information-governance/runway work is unrelated to this slice.
. *Should 5B remain one slice?* *YES.* No subdivision meets the independent
  capability/risk/acceptance test.
. *What is the smallest coherent implementation order?* Strict input/profile
  declarations and preflight -> reducer attribution/blocking/fail-route ->
  vertical per-phase evidence/artifacts -> finite driver and prompt/persistence
  projection -> one integrated non-DMC fixture and adversarial regression matrix.
. *What must not be built?* See section 16.
