== 3. Closure standard [[sec3]]

Per the governing chain, Cycle-1 closure may be recommended only if:

. no MATERIAL hidden cross-owner ontology remains in the bounded scope;
. F-002 remains valid;
. the Cell Codec blocker remains closed;
. H5/deletion authority remains valid;
. remaining items are Class B (later-cycle/public/topology/documentation),
  Class C (separate owner-local bug/FTR), or Class D (harmless/legitimate
  handoff);
. no speculative new abstraction is required.

Family Cycle 1's own canonical purpose (roadmap, "Family cycle 1: remove
remaining ontology") is scoped to implicit global classifications, hidden
role assumptions, cross-family semantic reconstruction, foreign metadata
representation interpretation, and duplicated semantic algorithms belonging
to another owner -- not later-cycle concerns (security/defensive cleanup,
supported-type review, structural refactoring) unless they concretely encode
surviving Cycle-1 foreign ontology.
