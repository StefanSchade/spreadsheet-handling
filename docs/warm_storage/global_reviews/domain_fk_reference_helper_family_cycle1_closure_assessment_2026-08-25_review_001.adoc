== 8. Renderer / H6

The exact renderer contract was resolved from current source, the accepted

=== 8.1 Consumed contract

The `derived` registry entry explicitly declares:

* transient, render-relevant current-run helper provenance;
* FK producer shape
  `derived.sheets.*.helper_columns[*].{column,fk_column,target,target_key,value_field}`;
* Lookup producer subkey `derived.sheets.*.enrich_lookup`;
* the two producer functions;
* `rendering.passes._base._derived_helper_column_names` as a consumer; and
* independent producer subkeys in one shared namespace.

The accepted H6 handoff records the semantic split: feature producers own
helper identity and formula intent; Workbook View owns durable visible
presentation; renderer/adapters execute style, protection, and physical
formula presentation.

`_derived_helper_column_names` consumes only presentation helper identity:

* FK rich entries contribute their explicit `column` member;
* Lookup provenance contributes its explicit `helper_columns` strings; and
* sheet/frame mapping contributes only which presentation sheet/frame to
  inspect.

The resulting names are matched against the rendered table header map for
helper fill and `editable: non_helper` protection. The renderer does not read
FK relation identity, target/key/value-field meaning, materialization truth,
deletion authority, Lookup key form, duplicate validity, mismatch semantics,
or DCP policy.

=== 8.2 Adversarial disposition

`column` is therefore an explicit producer-to-renderer material field, not a
private guess. A producer representation change can require coordinated
maintenance of this handoff; that is expected material coupling. It does not
require a renderer semantic decision about FK relations.

`_helper_column_names_from_value` accepts several identity carrier forms
because it serves three presentation inputs: Workbook-View/IR options,
Lookup's string identity list, and FK's rich identity entries. Its permissive
`columns`/`column`/`name` compatibility branches are presentation-carrier
handling. They do not reconstruct a generic relation, key, provenance,
mismatch, or deletion ontology.

== 11. FK/Lookup mechanical parallelism

`domain/validations/fk_helpers.py::check_helper_values` and
`enrich_lookup/mismatch.py` both contain canonical-map-and-compare mechanics.
The parallelism was challenged directly:

. Neither imports, calls, or reads the other's representation.
. FK validation receives FK relations/provenance, groups expected target
   fields by the FK-declared target/key, and emits FK validation findings.
. Lookup mismatch receives an interpreted Lookup-owned provenance value,
   applies Lookup duplicate/unverifiable/formula rules, and returns a
   Lookup-owned report without DCP findings.
. Changing Lookup key/provenance/mismatch semantics does not require FK
   validation to change.
. Changing FK relation/materialization/provenance semantics does not require
   Lookup mismatch to change.
. The common shape is lookup-map construction, normalization, and row
   comparison: algorithmic mechanics, not shared foreign meaning.

A later common-mechanism extraction could reduce duplication if a future
design proves a stable neutral seam. It is not required for present ownership
correctness and is not performed or decided here.
